What Is Robotics?

Robotics combines engineering, electronics, programming, sensors and intelligent software to create machines capable of performing specific tasks.

Sense → Process → Decide → Act

A robot can receive information from its surroundings, process that information and perform an action.

01 Sense Collect information
02 Process Interpret information
03 Decide Select response
04 Act Perform action

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Robotics FAQs

What is robotics?
Robotics is a technology field that combines mechanical systems, electronics, programming and sensors to create machines capable of performing specific tasks.
Is robotics suitable for school students?
Yes. Robotics can be introduced at different levels depending on a student's age and existing knowledge. Beginner programs can focus on simple components, programming, sensors and guided projects.
Do I need programming knowledge to learn robotics?
Not necessarily. Beginner robotics programs can introduce programming concepts gradually while learners work with physical robotic systems.
What can students learn through robotics?
Students can develop exposure to programming, electronics, sensors, motors, automation, engineering concepts, problem solving and project development.
What is the connection between robotics and AI?
AI can provide robotic systems with capabilities such as object recognition, decision making, computer vision and intelligent responses.
Can robotics be combined with IoT?
Yes. Robotics and IoT can be combined to connect robots with sensors, devices, networks and cloud-based systems.
Is robotics useful for engineering students?
Robotics can provide practical exposure to areas such as electronics, embedded systems, programming, automation, control systems and AI.
Does RLT Edzaro conduct robotics workshops for schools and colleges?
RLT Edzaro can provide robotics-focused learning programs and workshops for educational institutions, subject to the specific program, audience and requirements.
Where can robotics be used?
Robotics has applications across manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, logistics, education, research, automation and other technology-driven areas.
What projects can students build in robotics?
Depending on the learning level, projects may include line-following robots, obstacle-avoiding robots, robotic arms, mobile robots, automation systems and AI-enabled robotics projects.
