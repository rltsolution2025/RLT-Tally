FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

IoT FAQs

What is IoT?
IoT, or the Internet of Things, is a technology concept where physical devices use sensors, software and communication technologies to collect and exchange information.
What is an example of IoT?
A smart temperature monitoring system is a simple example. A sensor measures temperature, a controller processes the information and a connected system can display or respond to the data.
Is IoT suitable for school students?
Yes. IoT can be introduced to school students through age-appropriate activities involving sensors, simple programming, electronics and connected devices.
Do I need programming knowledge to learn IoT?
Not necessarily. Beginners can start with basic programming concepts and gradually learn how software interacts with sensors and hardware.
What can students learn through IoT?
Students can explore sensors, electronics, microcontrollers, programming, connectivity, data collection, automation and project development.
What is the difference between IoT and robotics?
IoT primarily focuses on connecting devices and exchanging information, while robotics focuses on machines that can sense, process information and perform physical actions. The two technologies can also be combined.
Can IoT be combined with Artificial Intelligence?
Yes. AI can analyse data collected by IoT devices and support prediction, detection, classification and automated decision-making.
What projects can students build using IoT?
Projects can include smart home systems, weather monitoring systems, smart agriculture solutions, security systems, environmental monitors, smart lighting and connected devices.
Is IoT useful for engineering students?
Yes. IoT can provide practical exposure to electronics, embedded systems, programming, networking, sensors, automation and data-driven technologies.
Can schools conduct IoT workshops?
Yes. IoT workshops can be designed for schools and colleges based on student age, technical level, duration and learning objectives.
What technologies are connected to IoT?
IoT can work alongside sensors, microcontrollers, embedded systems, networking, cloud platforms, Artificial Intelligence, robotics and automation.
