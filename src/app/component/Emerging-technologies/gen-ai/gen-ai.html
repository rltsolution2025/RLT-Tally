What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of Artificial Intelligence that can create new content based on instructions, context and information.

AI That Can Create

Instead of only analysing or classifying existing information, Generative AI can produce new digital outputs based on human instructions.

RESPONSIBLE USE OF GENERATIVE AI

AI Can Help Create. Humans Must Still Think.

AI-generated content should not automatically be treated as correct. Learners should understand how to evaluate AI outputs and use AI responsibly.

AI output requires human review.
Accuracy AI can generate incorrect information.
Verification Important information should be checked.
Privacy Sensitive information should be handled carefully.
Copyright Generated and referenced content may have usage considerations.
Human Oversight People remain responsible for reviewing important outputs.
Learn AI. Use AI. Think critically.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Generative AI FAQs

What is Generative AI?
Generative AI is a type of AI that can create new content such as text, images, code, audio and other digital outputs.
Is Generative AI suitable for beginners?
Yes. Beginners can start with basic AI concepts, prompting and practical AI tools.
What is prompt engineering?
Prompt engineering is the process of creating clear and structured instructions for an AI system.
Do I need programming knowledge?
No. Many GenAI applications can be explored without programming. Coding can be introduced for learners interested in technical applications.
Can school students learn Generative AI?
Yes. Age-appropriate programs can introduce students to AI, prompting, creativity, problem-solving and responsible AI use.
Can Generative AI be combined with robotics and IoT?
Yes. GenAI can be explored alongside robotics, IoT, drones, programming and other emerging technologies.
