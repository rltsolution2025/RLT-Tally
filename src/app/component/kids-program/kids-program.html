GOOD TO KNOW

Questions parents
may have.

A quick overview of the program format and starting level.

What is the age group for this program?

The program is designed for children aged 8–15 years.

Are classes conducted online?

No. The program is offered as offline, in-person classes only.

Does a child need advanced technical knowledge?

No advanced technical knowledge is required. Children can start with foundational concepts and progress into practical activities.

Which technologies can children explore?

Robotics and automation, IoT, drone technology, Generative AI, coding and computational thinking, AI games and digital creation, and 3D design and printing.

Where are the available centres?

The listed centres are Kodambakkam, Madurai and Chengalpattu. Contact RLT Edzaro to enquire about enrollment.
