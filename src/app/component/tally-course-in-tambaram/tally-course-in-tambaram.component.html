Course Curriculum

TallyPrime & Accounting Course Curriculum

The curriculum combines accounting fundamentals with practical TallyPrime, GST, payroll, taxation, inventory and financial reporting.

01

Accounting Fundamentals

Learn fundamentals of accounting, golden rules of accounting, journal entries, ledger creation, groups, trial balance, bookkeeping principles and accounting transactions.

02

TallyPrime Basics

Learn company creation, configuration, ledger creation, groups, voucher entry, sales, purchases, receipts, payments, accounting reports, inventory and multi-currency setup.

03

GST Compliance & Filing

Learn GST fundamentals, GST configuration, CGST, SGST, IGST, taxable invoicing, input tax credit, GST invoices, E-Way Bills, GST reports, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B.

04

TDS, TCS & Payroll

Learn TDS computation, TCS computation, employee records, salary heads, salary processing, pay slips, attendance, PF, ESI and payroll reports.

05

MIS Reporting & Financial Finalization

Learn Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Analysis, Financial Reports, MIS Reporting, Data Security and Financial Finalization.
