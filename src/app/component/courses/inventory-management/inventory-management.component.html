What You Will Learn

The course begins with an introduction to Inventory Management, including a preview video that provides an overview of the topic. It then explains what Inventory Management is, its importance, and the advantages it offers to businesses. The course also covers different types of inventory valuation methods to help learners understand how stock value is determined.

The course covers Stock Group management, including its introduction, creation, and alteration, along with the creation and modification of Stock Categories. Learners will also understand how to create Units of Measurement, explore frequently asked questions, set up Godowns, and create and alter Stock Items for effective inventory management.

The course covers batch management for stock items, including the activation of batches and allocation of batch details during purchase invoice entry. Learners will also understand how to sell stock items from specific batches, identify expired batches during sales invoice processing, enable or disable the printing of batch information, and process returns for expired stock items.

The course covers Price Level management, including the activation of price levels and defining different price levels for customers or sales requirements. Learners will understand how to create and use price lists in sales vouchers, as well as how to revise existing price lists to keep product pricing accurate and up to date.

The course covers the activation and management of Purchase Orders and Sales Orders, along with Delivery Notes and Receipt Notes for tracking inventory movements. Learners will also understand the activation and usage of Debit Notes and Credit Notes. The Purchase Management module covers recording Purchase Orders, Receipt Notes, Purchases, and Purchase Returns, while the Sales Management module covers Sales Orders, Delivery Notes, Sales Transactions, and Sales Returns.
