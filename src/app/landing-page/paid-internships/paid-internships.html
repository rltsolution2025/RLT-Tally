ABOUT THE PROGRAM

More practice.
More understanding.

Classroom concepts become more useful when you get the chance to apply them. RLT Edzaro's paid internship program for college students in Chennai is designed around guided practice, skill development and exposure to practical tasks in your selected area.

RLT Edzaro operates a software and Tally training institute and is an authorised Tally Partner. The wider RLT business also works in instrumentation, enabling internship tracks that connect learners with software, accounting workflows and product-focused training.

This is a paid internship and training program, not a free internship. The applicable fee and program arrangements should be confirmed before enrolment.
