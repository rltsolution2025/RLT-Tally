01 / INTRODUCTION

Introduction

The Bank Book in TallyPrime is an important accounting report that helps businesses track transactions recorded through their bank accounts. It shows money deposited into the bank, payments made, transfers, and the resulting bank balance based on the entries recorded in the accounting books.

For business owners, accountants, and accounting students, learning how to check the Bank Book in TallyPrime is an essential practical accounting skill. It helps users monitor cash flow, verify bank transactions, identify incorrect entries, and prepare for bank reconciliation.

TallyPrime provides reports that allow users to review bank transactions for a selected period and examine individual voucher details.

In this guide, you will learn how to open the Bank Book in TallyPrime, check bank transactions, filter entries by date, understand debit and credit amounts, and compare the Bank Book with a bank statement.

What you will learn

How to open the report, select a bank ledger, set a reporting period, inspect vouchers, understand the closing balance, and identify differences that may need reconciliation.

02 / BANK BOOK BASICS

What Is a Bank Book in TallyPrime?

A Bank Book is an accounting report that records transactions associated with a business's bank account. It is generated from the transactions entered in the accounting system.

The Bank Book generally includes:

Opening Balance

The bank balance at the beginning of the selected period.

Money Received

Customer receipts and other amounts recorded as bank inflows.

Payments Made

Supplier payments, expenses, and other bank outflows.

Bank Transfers

Transfers recorded between cash and bank or bank accounts.

Cheque Transactions

Cheque-related transactions where the details are recorded.

Closing Balance

The balance calculated from the opening balance and recorded movements.

For example, when a business receives a customer payment directly into its bank account, the receipt entry appears in the Bank Book. When the business pays a supplier through the bank, the payment entry is also reflected in the report.

The Bank Book helps accountants understand how the recorded bank balance changes over a particular period.

04 / STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

How to Check Bank Book in TallyPrime: Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to view the Bank Book in TallyPrime.

01

Open TallyPrime

Launch TallyPrime on your computer. Select the company whose bank transactions you want to review and make sure you have opened the correct company and financial data.

02

Open the Bank Book Report

Open Gateway of Tally, navigate to Display More Reports or the relevant reports menu, open Account Books, and select Cash/Bank Book or the relevant Bank Book report. Choose the bank ledger you want to view.

03

Select the Bank Ledger

If your company maintains multiple bank accounts, select the relevant ledger, such as HDFC Bank Account, SBI Current Account, or ICICI Bank Account. TallyPrime will display the transactions recorded against that ledger.

04

Set the Required Date Range

Change the report period to the required dates. For example, select 1 October 2026 to 31 October 2026 to review the transactions recorded during that month.

05

Review the Bank Transactions

Review the date, voucher number, voucher type, particulars or ledger details, debit amount, credit amount, and running or closing balance where displayed.

06

Open an Individual Voucher

Select the transaction and open its voucher. Verify the supplier or recipient, amount, bank ledger, payment date, expense or liability ledger, narration, and supporting details.

07

Check the Closing Bank Balance

Review the closing balance calculated from the opening balance and transactions recorded in the books for the selected period.

The precise navigation may differ slightly between TallyPrime releases. You can also use the report search or Go To feature, where available, to locate the Bank Book. To understand voucher details further, see TallyPrime Voucher Types Explained.

The closing balance in TallyPrime may differ from the bank statement because of uncleared cheques, deposits in transit, bank charges not yet recorded, or other timing and accounting differences. Review it alongside the bank statement.

05 / PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Practical Example: Understanding a Bank Book in TallyPrime

Suppose ABC Enterprises has the following transactions recorded in its bank ledger.

Particulars Money In Money Out
Opening bank balance ₹50,000 —
Customer payment received ₹25,000 —
Supplier payment — ₹15,000
Electricity bill paid — ₹5,000
Customer payment received ₹10,000 —
Total movement ₹35,000 ₹20,000
CLOSING BALANCE CALCULATION
Closing balance = Opening balance + Money received − Money paid
₹50,000 + ₹35,000 − ₹20,000 = ₹65,000
Closing bank balance: ₹65,000

Therefore, the closing bank balance in the books would be ₹65,000, assuming there are no other transactions or adjustments. This example demonstrates how receipts and payments affect the Bank Book balance.

06 / DATE FILTER

How to Check Bank Transactions for a Specific Date

Sometimes, accountants need to find a particular transaction rather than review an entire month's activity.

To check transactions for a specific date:

  1. Open the relevant Bank Book.
  2. Set the report period to the required date or a short date range.
  3. Review the transactions displayed.
  4. Locate the required voucher number or transaction details.
  5. Open the voucher to verify the amount and accounting entry.

Example

If you need to check a supplier payment recorded on 12 October 2026, select the relevant date range and locate the payment entry. This method is useful when answering accounting queries, checking payment status, or investigating differences in the bank balance.

07 / MONTHLY REVIEW

How to Check Bank Book for a Particular Month

To review a monthly Bank Book:

01

Open the Bank Ledger Report

Navigate to the relevant Bank Book or bank ledger report.

02

Set the Monthly Period

Set the start date to the first day of the month and the end date to the last day of the month.

03

Review Bank Movements

Review all receipts, payments, and transfers recorded during the period.

04

Verify the Balances

Check the opening and closing balances and compare transactions with the corresponding bank statement.

Monthly verification helps accountants identify missing entries and review bank activity before closing the accounts for the month. You can also review the Day Book in TallyPrime to inspect transactions recorded across the selected period.

08 / REPORT COMPARISON

Difference Between Bank Book and Bank Statement

Although they contain related information, the Bank Book and the bank statement are not the same.

Bank Book Bank Statement
Maintained in the accounting system. Provided by the bank.
Shows transactions recorded in TallyPrime. Shows transactions processed or reported by the bank.
Reflects the accounting records. Reflects the bank's records.
May include entries not yet reflected by the bank. May include bank charges or credits not yet entered in TallyPrime.
Used to review accounting transactions. Used to verify transactions and balances against the bank.

Both records are important for maintaining accurate financial information. When differences arise, compare them through Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime.

09 / BANK RECONCILIATION

How to Reconcile the Bank Book with a Bank Statement

Bank reconciliation is the process of comparing the transactions recorded in the Bank Book with those shown in the bank statement.

01

Obtain the Bank Statement

Download or obtain the bank statement for the period you want to reconcile.

02

Open the Bank Reconciliation Report

Open the relevant bank ledger and navigate to the Bank Reconciliation feature or report available in your TallyPrime release.

03

Compare Transactions

Compare transaction dates, amounts, references, deposits, and payments between the Bank Book and bank statement.

04

Identify Differences

Look for cheques issued but not presented, deposits not yet credited, bank charges not entered in the books, interest credited by the bank, and duplicate or incorrectly recorded entries.

05

Update Accounting Records

Record missing bank charges, interest, or other transactions where appropriate. Correct accounting errors after verifying supporting documents.

06

Complete Reconciliation

Update the applicable reconciliation details and verify the resulting balances.

Do not alter entries merely to force the Bank Book balance to match the bank statement. Identify and resolve the underlying differences first.

10 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Problems When Checking the Bank Book in TallyPrime

1. Bank Ledger Is Not Visible

Possible reason: The ledger may not have been created under the correct group or may not contain transactions for the selected period.

Solution: Verify the ledger configuration and company data. Confirm that you have selected the correct bank ledger.

2. Transactions Are Missing

Possible reasons: The wrong date range has been selected, the transaction was entered under another ledger, the voucher has not been recorded or saved, or report filters are hiding the entry.

Solution: Check the date range, ledger allocation, voucher details, and report settings.

3. Bank Book Balance Does Not Match the Bank Statement

Possible reasons: Outstanding cheques, deposits not yet credited, unrecorded bank charges, missing interest entries, or incorrect or duplicate accounting entries.

Solution: Perform bank reconciliation and investigate each difference.

4. Incorrect Transaction Amount Appears

Possible reasons: An incorrect voucher amount was entered, the wrong bank ledger was selected, or a duplicate or incorrect voucher was recorded.

Solution: Open the relevant voucher and verify the transaction against the supporting document before making corrections.

If you need to examine the source entries in more detail, the Ledger Reports in TallyPrime can help you review transactions posted to individual ledgers.

12 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I open the Bank Book in TallyPrime?

Open your company, navigate to the relevant account books or cash/bank reports, and select the required bank ledger. The exact menu path may vary depending on your TallyPrime release.

2. Can I check the Bank Book for a specific month?

Yes. Open the Bank Book and set the report period to the first and last dates of the required month.

3. How do I check the closing balance in TallyPrime?

Open the relevant Bank Book and review the closing balance for the selected period. Verify that the opening balance and recorded receipts and payments are correct.

4. Why is my Bank Book balance different from my bank statement?

The difference may result from outstanding cheques, deposits in transit, unrecorded bank charges, interest entries, or accounting errors. Bank reconciliation helps identify the cause.

5. Can I view individual bank transactions in TallyPrime?

Yes. Select the relevant transaction in the Bank Book and open its voucher to review the associated accounting details.

6. What is the difference between a Bank Book and a Cash Book?

A Bank Book records transactions related to bank accounts, while a Cash Book records transactions related to cash. Both help businesses monitor receipts, payments, and balances.

7. Is learning the Bank Book important for accounting jobs?

Yes. Understanding the Bank Book helps accounting professionals review receipts and payments, verify balances, investigate discrepancies, and perform bank reconciliation.

Conclusion

Regular bank reconciliation also helps identify missing transactions, timing differences, and accounting errors before they affect financial reports.

For accounting students and aspiring accountants, practising Bank Book reports in TallyPrime is a useful step towards understanding day-to-day accounting operations.
