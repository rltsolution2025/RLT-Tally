INTRODUCTION

How to Manage Batch-Wise Inventory in TallyPrime

Managing inventory accurately is essential for businesses that handle products from different manufacturing batches. Businesses dealing with medicines, food products, cosmetics, chemicals, and other goods often need to track batch numbers, manufacturing dates, and expiry dates to maintain proper stock records.

TallyPrime provides batch-wise inventory management features that help businesses organise stock by batch, record purchases and sales, monitor stock availability, and track expiry dates when the relevant features are configured.

Learning how to manage batch-wise inventory in TallyPrime is also valuable for accounting students and aspiring inventory executives who want to develop practical accounting and stock management skills.

What this guide covers

Enable batch tracking, configure stock items, enter batch details during purchases and sales, monitor expiry information, and review batch-wise stock reports.

BATCH TRACKING BASICS

What Is Batch-Wise Inventory in TallyPrime?

Batch-wise inventory management is a method of tracking stock according to the batch or lot to which each product belongs.

A batch is a group of products manufactured, purchased, or identified under a common batch number. Businesses can use batch information to distinguish between products with different manufacturing dates, expiry dates, or other identifying details.

For example, a pharmaceutical distributor may have 100 medicine bottles from Batch A and another 150 bottles from Batch B. Even though both batches contain the same product, their expiry dates may differ.

Batch tracking helps the business identify how much stock is available in each batch and which products need attention because of approaching expiry dates.

Batch-wise tracking separates a product's inventory into identifiable lots. The details available in TallyPrime depend on the company features, stock item configuration, and voucher workflow.

SETUP

How to Enable Batch-Wise Inventory in TallyPrime

Before entering batch details, enable the relevant inventory features in your company.

01
COMPANY SETUP

Open Your Company

  1. Launch TallyPrime.
  2. Select the company in which you want to manage inventory.
  3. Verify that the company has the appropriate inventory configuration.
02
FEATURE CONFIGURATION

Enable Batch-Wise Details

  1. Open the company features or inventory configuration screen.
  2. Locate the option for maintaining batch-wise details.
  3. Enable the batch-related feature.
  4. If you need to track product expiry dates, enable the applicable expiry-date tracking option as well.
  5. Save the configuration.
03
VERIFY SETTINGS

Verify Inventory Settings

Review the inventory configuration to ensure that batch tracking is available for the stock items you want to manage. If your business needs to track expiry dates, verify that the relevant option is enabled and that the stock item is configured appropriately.

Important: Enabling batch tracking does not automatically create batch numbers for existing stock. You may need to enter or update batch information through the appropriate inventory workflow.

The exact menu labels and navigation can vary depending on your TallyPrime release.

STOCK INWARD

How to Record Batch-Wise Inventory During a Purchase

When a business purchases goods from a supplier, it can record the received quantity against the relevant batch number.

Purchase example

ABC Traders purchases 100 units of Packaged Food Product from a supplier at ₹50 per unit. The batch number is PF101, with a manufacturing date of 1 October 2026 and an expiry date of 31 March 2027.

Particulars Details
Product Packaged Food Product
Quantity 100 units
Batch number PF101
Manufacturing date 1 October 2026
Expiry date 31 March 2027
Purchase rate ₹50 per unit
Purchase value before applicable taxes ₹5,000

Steps to Record the Purchase

  1. Open the relevant purchase voucher in TallyPrime.
  2. Select the supplier ledger.
  3. Enter the purchase details and select the stock item.
  4. Enter the quantity and rate.
  5. Open the relevant batch allocation or batch details section.
  6. Enter the batch number, such as PF101.
  7. Enter the expiry date if expiry tracking is enabled and required.
  8. Enter the manufacturing date if the workflow supports it and your business needs to maintain it.
  9. Verify the quantity and value, then save the voucher.

After saving, verify that the purchased quantity is reflected against the correct batch. The exact fields available depend on your TallyPrime configuration and the inventory workflow being used.

For more information about transaction entry, read TallyPrime Voucher Types Explained.

STOCK OUTWARD

How to Record Batch-Wise Inventory During a Sales Transaction

When selling goods, select the appropriate batch so that the correct stock quantity is reduced.

Sales example: Batch PF101

ABC Traders has 100 units of Packaged Food Product in Batch PF101. A customer purchases 30 units from that batch.

Batch quantity calculation

PF101
Available quantity before sale100 units
Quantity sold30 units
Remaining quantity70 units

Steps to Record the Sale

  1. Open a sales voucher.
  2. Select the customer ledger.
  3. Select the stock item.
  4. Enter the quantity being sold.
  5. Select the appropriate batch in the batch allocation section.
  6. Verify the available quantity and applicable selling rate.
  7. Check the relevant tax details.
  8. Save the sales voucher.

After the transaction, Batch PF101 should show a balance of 70 units, assuming no other stock movements have occurred.

If the same product exists in multiple batches, select the correct batch according to your business's stock rotation policy and the product's expiry information.

EXPIRY MANAGEMENT

How to Track Expiry Dates in TallyPrime

Expiry tracking is important for businesses that sell goods with a limited shelf life. Examples include medicines, dairy products, packaged foods, cosmetics, and selected chemicals.

When expiry tracking is configured, batch details can help businesses identify products approaching their expiry dates.

Steps to Manage Expiry Information

  1. Enable the relevant batch and expiry-date features.
  2. Configure the applicable stock item.
  3. Enter batch details when recording stock receipts.
  4. Maintain the correct expiry information for each batch.
  5. Review available batch-wise inventory reports and expiry-related information supported by your TallyPrime version.
  6. Use the information to plan stock rotation, returns, or other appropriate actions.

Example of Expiry Monitoring

Product Batch Quantity Expiry Date
Product A A101 50 units 31 October 2026
Product A A102 100 units 31 December 2026
Product A A103 150 units 31 March 2027

In this example, Batch A101 expires first. The business can review its sales and stock rotation plans to reduce the risk of expired stock.

Best practice: Use a first-expiry, first-out (FEFO) approach when appropriate. FEFO means prioritising stock with the earliest expiry date, subject to product quality, customer requirements, and applicable regulations.

TallyPrime can help maintain batch information, but businesses should verify the reports and processes available in their version rather than assuming that every expiry alert or automatic FEFO function is enabled by default.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Batch-Wise Inventory Problems and Solutions

1. Batch Details Are Not Appearing

Possible reasons: Batch tracking is not enabled, the stock item is not configured for batch-wise details, or the current voucher workflow does not expose the required batch allocation.

Solution: Review the company inventory settings, stock item configuration, and voucher entry mode.

2. Expiry Date Is Not Displayed

Possible reasons: Expiry-date tracking is not enabled, the relevant stock item or batch configuration is incomplete, or the selected report does not display expiry information.

Solution: Verify the expiry-related settings and use a report or batch view that supports the information you need.

3. Incorrect Stock Quantity Appears

Possible reasons: A purchase was entered against the wrong batch, a sales voucher used an incorrect batch, a stock adjustment or return was recorded incorrectly, or opening stock was entered incorrectly.

Solution: Review the relevant vouchers, batch allocations, and stock reports. Correct discrepancies using the appropriate accounting and inventory procedures.

4. The Same Product Appears in Multiple Batches

This is expected when the product has been received in different batches.

Solution: Review the batch number, quantity, and expiry information before selecting a batch for a sale or stock adjustment.

5. Expired Stock Is Still Available

Batch tracking does not automatically mean that expired stock is blocked from sale in every configuration.

Solution: Review expiry information regularly and implement suitable procedures to prevent expired or unsuitable goods from being dispatched.

INVENTORY CONTROL

Best Practices for Batch-Wise Inventory Management

Follow these practices to maintain accurate inventory records:

Use consistent batch identification: Record the supplier's or manufacturer's batch number accurately.
Enter expiry information carefully: Verify dates when goods arrive.
Record purchases promptly: Update stock as soon as receipts are processed.
Select the correct batch during sales: Confirm quantity and expiry details before saving.
Review stock reports regularly: Identify discrepancies and slow-moving batches.
Use suitable stock rotation: Consider FEFO for products with expiry dates.
Maintain supporting documents: Keep purchase invoices, delivery documents, and related records.
Reconcile physical stock: Compare recorded quantities with actual inventory.
Review GST classifications: Maintain applicable tax details correctly for the stock item and transaction.
Train inventory staff: Ensure users understand batch allocation and stock adjustments.

These practices can help businesses improve stock accuracy, traceability, and inventory control.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is batch-wise inventory in TallyPrime?

Batch-wise inventory in TallyPrime is a method of tracking stock by individual batch or lot numbers. It helps businesses identify stock quantities associated with specific batches and maintain relevant batch information.

2. How do I enable batch-wise details in TallyPrime?

Open the company inventory configuration, enable the applicable batch-wise details feature, and save the settings. Configure the relevant stock items and verify that batch information can be entered in the appropriate vouchers.

3. Can TallyPrime track expiry dates?

TallyPrime supports expiry-date tracking for appropriately configured batch-wise inventory. The availability and presentation of expiry-related information depend on the version and configuration.

4. Can I maintain multiple batches for the same stock item?

Yes. A stock item can have multiple batches when batch-wise tracking is configured. Each batch can maintain its own quantity and relevant batch information.

5. How do I check batch-wise stock in TallyPrime?

Use the relevant stock summary, detailed stock report, or batch-related report available in your TallyPrime version. Review the product, batch, quantity, and other supported details.

6. Is batch-wise inventory useful for pharmaceutical businesses?

Yes. Batch tracking can help pharmaceutical businesses maintain batch identification, stock quantities, and expiry information. Businesses must also follow the applicable product-handling, storage, traceability, and regulatory requirements.

7. What is the difference between batch-wise inventory and regular inventory?

Regular inventory tracking focuses on overall stock quantities for a product. Batch-wise inventory separates stock into individual batches, making it easier to track batch-specific quantities and expiry information.

8. Can batch-wise inventory reduce stock wastage?

Batch tracking can help businesses identify stock approaching expiry and improve stock rotation. Actual wastage reduction depends on purchasing, sales, storage, and inventory management practices.
