How to Create Masters Quickly in TallyPrime

Learn how to create ledgers, stock items, groups, units, customers, and suppliers using clear, practical steps.

Practical Accounting GuideBeginner Friendly

01 · Getting started

Introduction

Creating masters is one of the first practical skills accounting students need to learn in TallyPrime. Before recording sales, purchases, payments, receipts, or inventory transactions, businesses need to configure the relevant ledgers, stock items, groups, units, and other master records.

A well-organised master structure helps users record transactions accurately and generate meaningful accounting reports.

TallyPrime allows users to create masters through dedicated creation screens. In supported fields, users can also create a master while entering a transaction, reducing the need to leave the current workflow.

This guide explains what masters are, the types of masters used in TallyPrime, how to create them, and how to maintain accurate master records.

02 · The basics

What Are Masters in TallyPrime?

Masters are records that define the accounts, inventory items, and other information used when recording business transactions.

For example, a business may create:

  • A customer ledger to record amounts receivable.
  • A supplier ledger to record amounts payable.
  • A sales ledger to record revenue.
  • A purchase ledger to record purchases.
  • A stock item to track inventory.
  • A unit to measure quantities.
  • A stock group to organise similar items.

Once these masters are configured correctly, users can select them while recording vouchers.

03 · Master categories

Types of Masters in TallyPrime

01

Accounting Ledgers

Ledgers record financial transactions and maintain account balances.

Examples

Cash, Bank, Sales, Purchases, Office Rent, Salary Expense, customer accounts, and supplier accounts.

Each ledger should be placed under the appropriate group.

02

Groups

Groups organise ledgers according to their accounting purpose.

Examples

Sundry Debtors, Sundry Creditors, Sales Accounts, Purchase Accounts, and relevant expense groups.

Correct grouping affects how balances appear in financial reports.

03

Stock Items

Stock items represent individual products that a business buys, sells, or holds in inventory.

Examples

Laptops, office chairs, stationery, and electronic components.

Items can include units of measurement and relevant inventory or GST information.

04

Stock Groups

Stock groups organise similar stock items.

Examples

Laptops, Desktops, and Accessories.

This helps users manage and analyse inventory more efficiently.

05

Units of Measurement

Units define how inventory quantities are measured.

Examples

Nos., Kg., Litre, Metre, and Box.

Choose a unit that suits the product and inventory process.

06

Customer and Supplier Masters

Customers and suppliers are generally maintained as ledger masters.

Keep contact, address, and registration information accurate to support receivables, payables, and GST records.

04 · Step-by-step

How to Create a Ledger Quickly in TallyPrime

Let us create a customer ledger as an example.

01

Open Ledger Creation

Open the relevant company and navigate to the ledger creation screen using the available menu or master-creation option. You can also use Alt + C from a supported field or selection while entering a voucher.

02

Enter the Ledger Name

Enter a clear name, such as ABC Traders. Use a consistent naming convention to avoid duplicate or confusing records.

03

Select the Appropriate Group

For a typical customer account, select Sundry Debtors. For a supplier account, select Sundry Creditors. Choose the group based on the actual purpose of the ledger.

04

Enter Relevant Details

Complete required information such as address, state, contact information, and GST registration details where applicable.

05

Save the Ledger

Accept or save the master using the available screen action. The ledger can now be selected in the relevant voucher, subject to its configuration.

How to Create a Ledger While Recording a Voucher

Creating a ledger during voucher entry can save time when a new customer or supplier needs to be added.

Example: A new customer during a sales entry

  1. Open the Sales voucher.
  2. Move to the customer ledger field.
  3. Use Alt + C if the current field supports master creation.
  4. Enter the new ledger name.
  5. Select the appropriate group.
  6. Complete the required details.
  7. Save the master and return to the voucher.
  8. Continue entering the sales transaction.

This workflow reduces the need to exit the voucher and navigate through the master menu separately.

Important: Alt + C is context-sensitive. If it does not open master creation in your current screen, use the available ledger-creation option.

05 · Inventory masters

How to Create a Stock Item in TallyPrime

For a business that sells or purchases goods, stock item masters help track inventory.

01

Open Stock Item Creation

Navigate to the inventory or stock item creation screen through the available menu.

02

Enter the Stock Item Name

For example, enter Wireless Keyboard.

03

Select a Stock Group

Choose an existing group, such as Computer Accessories, or create a suitable group if required.

04

Select the Unit of Measurement

Choose an appropriate unit, such as Nos., for products measured individually.

05

Configure Relevant Details

Enter applicable inventory, classification, and GST information. Verify the HSN code and applicable GST rate where relevant.

06

Save the Stock Item

Save the master and verify that it appears in the stock item list. You can then select it in supported inventory transactions.

How to Create a Stock Group

Stock groups help organise related inventory items. For example, a business selling computers may create this structure:

Computer Products
Laptops
Desktops
Computer Accessories
  • Keyboards
  • Mice
  • Cables

To create a stock group:

  1. Open the stock group creation screen.
  2. Enter the group name.
  3. Select the appropriate parent group, if applicable.
  4. Complete any required details.
  5. Save the group.

A clear group structure makes inventory reporting easier to understand.

How to Create a Unit of Measurement

Units are useful when the business tracks quantities. For example, a stationery supplier may measure paper by packets, while a hardware business may track certain materials by kilograms or metres.

To create a unit:

  1. Open the unit creation screen.
  2. Select the appropriate unit type.
  3. Enter the symbol, such as Nos. or Kg.
  4. Enter the formal unit name if required.
  5. Configure decimal places where relevant.
  6. Save the unit.
Tip: Choose units carefully because inconsistent units can affect inventory recording and quantity comparisons.

Tips for Creating Masters Quickly and Accurately

01

Plan Your Ledger Structure

Decide which accounting groups and ledgers the business needs before creating large numbers of records.

02

Use Clear Names

Avoid vague names that make it difficult to identify the correct customer, supplier, or stock item.

03

Check the Group Before Saving

Incorrect grouping can affect financial report presentation.

04

Avoid Duplicate Masters

Search the existing list before creating a new ledger or stock item.

05

Configure GST Carefully

Enter relevant GST and registration information accurately. Do not assume every ledger or stock item has the same tax treatment.

06

Use On-the-Fly Creation Where Supported

Alt + C can help create masters during voucher entry in supported fields.

07

Review Existing Masters Regularly

Correct duplicate names, outdated details, and incorrect classifications using the appropriate alteration workflow.

Common Master-Creation Errors and Solutions

1. Ledger Created Under the Wrong Group

Problem: The ledger balance appears in an unexpected section of the financial reports.

Solution: Review the ledger's group and correct it where appropriate.

2. Duplicate Customer or Supplier Ledgers

Problem: Similar names create confusion during voucher entry and reconciliation.

Solution: Check existing masters before creating a new one and maintain a consistent naming convention.

3. Missing GST Details

Problem: GST information may be incomplete or tax calculations may be incorrect.

Solution: Verify the relevant registration, classification, and ledger settings before recording transactions.

4. Incorrect Stock Unit

Problem: Inventory quantities are difficult to reconcile.

Solution: Select a unit that correctly represents the product and maintain consistency in transactions.

5. Incorrect Stock Group

Problem: Inventory reports become difficult to interpret.

Solution: Review the stock group hierarchy and place items under suitable categories.

07 · Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are masters in TallyPrime?

Masters are records such as ledgers, groups, stock items, stock groups, and units that define the accounts and inventory information used in transactions.

2. What is the shortcut key to create a ledger in TallyPrime?

Alt + C can open master creation from a supported field or selection. You can also create ledgers through the dedicated ledger-creation screen.

3. Can I create a ledger while entering a sales voucher?

Yes. Where the customer selection field supports master creation, you can use Alt + C to create the ledger and then continue with the voucher.

4. What is the difference between a ledger and a stock item?

A ledger records financial transactions and account balances. A stock item represents a product tracked in inventory, including relevant quantities and item details.

5. Why is ledger grouping important?

Ledger grouping determines how accounts are classified and presented in financial reports. Selecting the appropriate group helps maintain accurate accounting records.

6. Can I alter a master after creating it?

Yes. You can open the relevant master alteration screen and update supported details. Review the impact of changes before modifying masters used in existing transactions.

7. Should I create every master before entering transactions?

Creating frequently used masters in advance can make transaction entry more efficient. However, supported on-the-fly creation options can be useful when a new master is needed during voucher entry.

08 · Key takeaway

Conclusion

Creating masters correctly is an essential part of using TallyPrime effectively. Ledgers, groups, stock items, stock groups, and units provide the structure needed to record transactions and generate meaningful reports.

By planning your master structure, using clear names, avoiding duplicates, checking GST details, and using supported on-the-fly creation options, you can make accounting workflows more efficient.
