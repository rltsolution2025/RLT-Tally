TALLYPRIME BANKING Step-by-step learning guide

Introduction

Managing business banking and accounting separately can take time. Accountants often need to log in to bank portals, check balances, download bank statements, record transactions in accounting software, and reconcile bank entries with company records.

Connected Banking in TallyPrime helps simplify these activities by bringing supported banking services directly into the accounting software. Depending on the bank and available features, users can check bank balances, retrieve bank statements, reconcile transactions, and initiate online payments without switching between multiple applications for every task.

For business owners, accountants, and accounting students, understanding how Connected Banking works in TallyPrime is useful for learning modern accounting workflows.

In this guide, you will learn what Connected Banking is, how it works, how to connect a bank account, how to access bank balances and statements, and how it helps improve banking and accounting efficiency.

KEY IDEA

Connected Banking makes supported bank information and services available within TallyPrime. It helps with access and workflow, but accountants must still review entries and complete reconciliation.

What Is Connected Banking in TallyPrime?

Connected Banking is a TallyPrime feature that connects supported bank accounts to the accounting software through an authenticated banking connection.

Once the connection is established, users can access supported banking information from within TallyPrime instead of separately opening their bank's website for every task.

Depending on the bank and supported functionality, Connected Banking can help users:

  • Check current bank balances.
  • Retrieve bank statements for selected periods.
  • Compare bank information with accounting records.
  • Reconcile bank transactions.
  • Initiate online payments through supported banks and payment features.

The feature brings banking information closer to everyday accounting activities, making it easier to monitor cash positions and manage transactions.

For the basics of recording transactions before reconciliation, read How to Record Bank Transactions in TallyPrime.

How Does Connected Banking Work in TallyPrime?

Connected Banking works by establishing an authorised connection between TallyPrime and a supported bank. The process involves authentication, connecting the bank account, retrieving banking information, and using that information in accounting workflows.

01

Enable Connected Banking

The user signs in using Tally.NET credentials and completes the required verification. A valid mobile number linked to the Tally.NET ID is needed for OTP verification during setup.

02

Connect the Bank Account

The user selects a supported bank and follows the bank's authentication process. After connection, relevant bank accounts can be linked to matching bank ledgers.

03

Retrieve Banking Information

Once connected and authenticated, the user can request the available bank balance or a bank statement for a selected period.

04

Use the Information in Accounting

The accountant compares retrieved information with transactions recorded in TallyPrime, identifies differences, and proceeds with reconciliation.

In simple terms: Connected Banking links supported bank services with TallyPrime so banking information can be accessed and used alongside accounting records. Online payment options may also be available for eligible bank connections.

Key Features of Connected Banking in TallyPrime

1. Check Bank Balance

Connected Banking allows users to retrieve available bank balance information from within TallyPrime. This helps business owners and accountants understand their current cash position before planning supplier payments or other business expenses.

For example, an accountant can check the balances of connected bank accounts before deciding which account to use for an upcoming payment.

2. Get Bank Statements

Users can retrieve bank statements for a selected date range through a supported bank connection. This reduces the need to download statements manually from a bank portal before starting reconciliation.

The retrieved statement can then be used to compare bank transactions with the company's books.

3. Simplify Bank Reconciliation

Connected Banking makes bank statement information available in TallyPrime, where accountants can match entries, identify missing transactions, and investigate differences.

Retrieving a statement does not mean every transaction is automatically reconciled. Accountants still need to review unmatched or incorrect entries and complete the reconciliation process.

Read: How to Perform Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime →

4. Access Banking and Accounting in One Place

Instead of repeatedly switching between banking and accounting applications, users can access supported banking functions within TallyPrime. This can reduce repetitive work and make daily accounting tasks more convenient.

5. Make Online Payments Through Supported Banks

Connected Banking can support online payments through eligible bank connections. Availability depends on the bank, account type, configuration, and supported payment services. Online payments may require additional activation and bank authentication.

TallyPrime documentation describes online payment support for eligible Axis Bank and State Bank of India connections. Verify current availability for your own bank and account before using this feature.

How to Enable Connected Banking in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to enable the feature.

01

Open TallyPrime

Launch TallyPrime and open the company in which you want to manage banking transactions.

02

Open Connected Banking

Navigate to Alt+Z (Exchange) → All Banking Options → Login & Logout. The exact menu presentation may differ slightly between releases.

03

Enter Your Tally.NET Credentials

Enter the Tally.NET ID associated with your licence and provide the required password.

04

Link Your Mobile Number

Enter a valid mobile number to link it to your Tally.NET ID, if it has not already been linked.

05

Complete OTP Verification

Read and accept the applicable terms and privacy information, then enter the OTP received on your mobile number. After successful verification, you can proceed to connect bank accounts.

How to Connect Your Bank Account to TallyPrime

After enabling Connected Banking, follow these steps to establish a bank connection.

  1. Open Alt+Z (Exchange) → All Banking Options → Bank Connections.
  2. Log in to Connected Banking if required.
  3. Select F6 (New Connection).
  4. Select the supported bank.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to connect to the bank.
  6. Complete the bank's authentication and authorisation process.
  7. Return to TallyPrime and refresh the connection status.
  8. Verify that the connection is shown as connected.
  9. Check the linked bank accounts and match them with the appropriate bank ledgers.

The bank connection may require additional approval or authentication depending on the bank and user permissions.

Security reminder: Never share your banking password, OTP, or other authentication credentials with unauthorised people.

How to Check Your Bank Balance in TallyPrime

Once your bank account is connected, you can retrieve its balance directly from TallyPrime.

Step-by-step process

  1. Open TallyPrime and log in to Connected Banking.
  2. Press Alt+Z (Exchange) and select Get Balance.
  3. Select one or more connected bank accounts.
  4. Choose B (Get Balance).
  5. Review the retrieved bank balance information.

You can also configure the report to display the bank name and the ledger balance according to your books, making it easier to compare the two balances.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Compare the bank balance with the book balance

Particulars Amount
Bank balance according to the bank ₹1,20,000

Difference to investigate ₹5,000

The difference does not necessarily mean that an error has occurred. It could result from unrecorded bank charges, outstanding transactions, timing differences, or other unreconciled entries. Review the bank statement and accounting records to identify the reason.

How to Get a Bank Statement Using Connected Banking

Connected Banking also lets you retrieve bank statements for a selected period.

Step-by-step process

  1. Open Alt+Z (Exchange) → All Banking Options → Get Statement.
  2. Select the relevant bank ledger.
  3. Set the statement period by entering the start and end dates.
  4. Choose S (Get Statement).
  5. Wait for the statement request to complete.
  6. Select the statement that is ready to import and follow the on-screen instructions.

Once the statement is available in TallyPrime, you can use it to reconcile bank transactions with your accounting records.

Example: A business wants to reconcile bank transactions for October 2026. The accountant retrieves the statement for 1 October to 12 October 2026 and compares it with TallyPrime records.

The accountant can then identify:

  • Payments recorded in TallyPrime but not yet reflected in the bank statement.
  • Bank charges that have not been entered in the books.
  • Customer payments received directly into the bank.
  • Duplicate or incorrectly recorded transactions.

After investigating the differences, the accountant can record any missing transactions and complete the reconciliation.

Is Connected Banking in TallyPrime Secure?

Security is an important consideration when connecting business bank accounts to accounting software.

TallyPrime's Connected Banking uses authenticated access and secure communication mechanisms. Depending on the bank and the feature being used, additional verification and approval controls apply.

For supported online payments, OTP verification and configurable transaction limits provide additional safeguards. Online payments can also be enabled or disabled for a bank connection, subject to the supported workflow.

Security practices for businesses

  • Use authorised Tally.NET accounts.
  • Give employees only the banking access they require.
  • Keep passwords and OTPs confidential.
  • Verify payment details before authorising transactions.
  • Review bank connection permissions periodically.
  • Disconnect bank accounts when access is no longer required.
  • Keep TallyPrime and the relevant subscription up to date.
Remember: Connected Banking does not remove the need for internal controls. Businesses should continue to follow their normal payment approval and accounting procedures.

Common Connected Banking Problems and Solutions

1. Bank Connection Is Not Available

Possible reason: The bank, account type, or feature may not be supported.

Solution: Verify eligibility using the latest official TallyPrime Connected Banking documentation and confirm that the bank ledger is correctly configured.

2. Unable to Log In to Connected Banking

Possible reasons: Incorrect Tally.NET credentials, an unlinked mobile number, an incorrect OTP, or an expired session.

Solution: Verify your login details, check the mobile number linked to your Tally.NET ID, and complete the required authentication process again.

3. Bank Balance Is Not Updating

Possible reasons: Internet connectivity issues, an inactive session, or a problem with the bank connection.

Solution: Check your internet connection, log in again if necessary, verify the bank connection, and retry the balance request.

4. Unable to Retrieve a Bank Statement

Possible reasons: The account is not connected, the statement period is incorrect, or the bank service is temporarily unavailable.

Solution: Confirm that the account is connected, check the selected dates, and retry the request. If the problem continues, consult the bank or Tally support.

5. Bank Balance and Ledger Balance Do Not Match

Possible reasons: Missing transactions, bank charges, outstanding payments, or timing differences.

Solution: Compare the bank statement with the TallyPrime bank ledger, identify the difference, and record or reconcile the relevant transactions.

You can also review the Bank Book in TallyPrime to inspect recorded transactions and ledger balances.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Connected Banking in TallyPrime?

Connected Banking is a feature that allows users to access supported bank accounts and banking services from within TallyPrime. It can provide bank balances, statements, reconciliation support, and online payment functionality where available.

2. Which TallyPrime version supports Connected Banking?

Connected Banking was introduced in TallyPrime Release 6.0. Check the current official requirements for your particular bank and feature before enabling it.

3. Can I check my bank balance in TallyPrime without opening my bank website?

Yes. Once Connected Banking is enabled and your bank account is connected, you can retrieve the available bank balance through the Get Balance option, subject to the bank's supported services and a successful connection.

4. Can I download bank statements directly into TallyPrime?

You can request bank statements through Connected Banking for supported bank accounts and selected periods. The statement can then be used in the relevant accounting and reconciliation workflow.

5. Does Connected Banking automatically reconcile every bank transaction?

No. Connected Banking makes bank information available within TallyPrime and helps streamline reconciliation. Accountants must still review unmatched transactions, investigate differences, and ensure the books are accurate.

6. Can I make payments directly from TallyPrime?

Yes, for supported bank connections and payment features. Availability depends on the bank, account type, activation settings, and required authentication. Verify the current supported payment options before using the feature.

7. Is a Tally.NET ID required for Connected Banking?

Yes. A Tally.NET ID associated with the licence is required, along with the applicable login and mobile verification process.

8. Is Connected Banking useful for small businesses?

Yes. Small businesses can use it to check bank balances, access statements, and simplify the reconciliation process. The practical benefits depend on their bank's support and how frequently they process transactions.

9. What is the difference between Connected Banking and bank reconciliation?

Connected Banking provides access to supported banking information and services. Bank reconciliation is the process of comparing bank transactions with the entries recorded in the company's books. Connected Banking can make that process easier, but reconciliation still requires appropriate review.

Conclusion

Connected Banking in TallyPrime brings supported banking services closer to everyday accounting activities. It helps businesses retrieve bank balances and statements, streamline reconciliation, and access online payments where supported.

By learning how to enable Connected Banking, connect bank accounts, retrieve statements, and compare banking information with accounting records, users can develop a better understanding of modern banking and accounting workflows.

For accounting students and aspiring accountants, practising these features alongside ledger creation, payment vouchers, and bank reconciliation can strengthen practical TallyPrime skills.
