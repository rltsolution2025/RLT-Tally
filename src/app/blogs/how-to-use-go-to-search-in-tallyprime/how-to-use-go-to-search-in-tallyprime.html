Quick Answer

How do you use Go To in TallyPrime?

Open the relevant company in TallyPrime and press Alt + G to open Go To. Review the available destinations or enter a relevant search term, select the report or feature you need, and check the reporting period and filters before reviewing the result.

The available destinations and search behaviour depend on your TallyPrime release, company configuration and current context.
01 / GETTING STARTED

How to Use Go To and Search Features in TallyPrime

As businesses grow, their accounting data becomes more detailed. An accountant may need to find a sales register, open a ledger, review a balance sheet, locate a voucher, or access a specific accounting report.

Navigating through multiple menus for every task can slow down daily work. TallyPrime provides navigation features, including Go To, that help users access supported reports and functions more efficiently.

Understanding these features is especially useful for accounting students, billing executives, and professionals who work with multiple vouchers and reports. This guide explains how to use Go To, find reports and features, work with accounting information, and avoid common navigation mistakes.

Use a clear report or feature name, select the correct destination, and verify the period before relying on the result.
02 / UNDERSTAND THE FEATURE

What Is Go To in TallyPrime?

Go To is a navigation feature that helps users access supported reports, vouchers, and features from a central location. Instead of navigating through several menu levels, you can open Go To and search for or select the required destination.

Alt + G
Common Go To shortcut

Use this shortcut to open Go To and access supported destinations in TallyPrime.

The options available depend on your TallyPrime release, active company, and current context. A result may not appear in every screen or configuration.

05 / PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Practical Example: Finding the Trial Balance

Suppose an accountant wants to review the company's account balances at the end of a month.

WORKFLOW EXAMPLE 01

Open and review the Trial Balance

  1. 1
    Open the company

    Make sure the correct company is active.

  2. 2
    Press Alt + G

    Open the Go To navigation feature.

  3. 3
    Find Trial Balance

    Search for or select Trial Balance from the available destinations.

  4. 4
    Set the reporting period

    Choose the appropriate month-end date or period, if required.

  5. 5
    Review the balances

    Investigate discrepancies using the appropriate ledger details.

The Trial Balance helps review ledger balances and whether total debits equal total credits. A balanced Trial Balance does not guarantee every transaction has been recorded correctly.

How to Use Search Features in TallyPrime

TallyPrime's search and navigation options can help you find supported destinations more quickly. The precise search behaviour depends on where you are working and the version installed.

01
Step 01

Identify what you need

Decide whether you need a report, voucher, ledger, stock item, or another feature. A specific search term usually makes navigation easier.

02
Step 02

Open the relevant navigation or selection screen

Use Go To for supported destinations. If you are selecting a ledger, stock item or other master inside a voucher, use the relevant selection field and its available search options.

03
Step 03

Enter a relevant search term

Try the name of the report or feature you need.

Trial BalanceSales RegisterBalance SheetPurchase Register
04
Step 04

Select the correct result

Check the destination before opening it. Similar names or multiple reporting options may appear depending on the company and version.

05
Step 05

Apply filters and review the result

Confirm the reporting period, company and other relevant filters when a report opens. This is especially important when comparing sales, purchases, or GST records.

10 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Problems and Solutions

01

The required report does not appear

The report may have a different name, may not be available in the current context, or may depend on the software version or configuration.

Solution: Search with a relevant term, review the available destinations, or use the menu structure to locate the report.
02

The report shows unexpected figures

The wrong period, company, or filter may be selected.

Solution: Check the reporting period and relevant report options before analysing the figures.
03

A voucher is difficult to find

You may be looking in the wrong report or transaction period.

Solution: Start from the appropriate register, select the correct period, and use supported drill-down or search options.
04

Go To does not open the expected destination

The current screen, active field, or software version may affect how the shortcut works.

Solution: Return to an appropriate navigation screen and follow the shortcut hints displayed in TallyPrime.
