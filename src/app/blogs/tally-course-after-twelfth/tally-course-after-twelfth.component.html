Tally Course After 12th: Career Options

After completing Class 12, students often start looking for short-term professional courses that can help them develop practical skills.

Tally training is one option for students interested in accounting, office administration, billing and finance-related work.

Why Learn Tally After 12th?

A practical software skill can complement your formal education.

For example, a student may combine:

12th qualification + TallyPrime + Excel + accounting knowledge

This can create a stronger foundation for entry-level office and accounting positions.

Career Options After Tally

Accounts Assistant

Accounts assistants may support routine accounting and documentation activities.

Billing Executive

Billing roles can involve preparing invoices and maintaining transaction records.

Junior Accountant

A junior accountant may assist with bookkeeping, accounting entries and reports.

Accounts Executive

This role can involve broader accounting responsibilities depending on the company.

Payroll Assistant

Payroll-related positions involve employee salary and attendance information.

GST Assistant

Some businesses hire employees to support GST-related accounting and documentation activities.

05

Should You Do Tally Alongside College?

It can be useful.

For example:

B.Com + TallyPrime + Excel

This can provide both academic knowledge and practical software exposure.

The same principle applies to other degrees as well.

Tally After 12th Science or Arts
