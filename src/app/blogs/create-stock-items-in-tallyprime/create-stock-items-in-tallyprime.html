GETTING STARTED

Introduction

Managing inventory is an important part of running a trading, retail, or manufacturing business. Businesses need to maintain accurate details about the products they purchase, sell, store, and track. TallyPrime helps businesses manage this information by allowing users to create stock items and maintain inventory records.

If you are learning TallyPrime, one of the first inventory tasks you should understand is how to create a stock item. Once you create a stock item, you can use it in purchase and sales transactions. Learn more about recording these transactions in TallyPrime Voucher Types Explained, and track quantities and stock values through inventory reports.

In this guide, you will learn how to create stock items in TallyPrime, choose stock groups, assign units of measurement, enter opening stock details, and avoid common inventory setup mistakes.

Quick answer

Open your company, go to Create and select Stock Item, or use Alt+G → Create Master → Stock Item. Enter the item name, stock group, unit and applicable opening balance, then press Ctrl+A to save.

INVENTORY BASICS

What Is a Stock Item in TallyPrime?

A stock item is a product or material that a business purchases, sells, manufactures, or tracks as inventory.

For example, a stationery shop may maintain stock items such as notebooks, pens, pencils, and files. An electronics retailer may create stock items for laptops, keyboards, monitors, and printers.

In TallyPrime, stock items help businesses record inventory transactions and monitor the quantity and value of goods.

Examples of stock items

Laptop Keyboard Mouse Notebook Office Chair Printer Raw Materials Finished Goods

Each stock item can have details such as its name, stock group, unit of measurement, opening quantity, rate, and other inventory-related information.

PREPARATION

What Do You Need Before Creating a Stock Item?

Before creating a stock item, prepare the following details:

01

Stock item name

The name of the product or material.

02

Stock group

The category under which the item should be organised.

03

Unit of measurement

The unit used to count or measure the item, such as Nos, Kg, or Ltr.

04

Opening quantity

The quantity available at the beginning of the books, if applicable.

05

Opening rate and value

The relevant starting inventory valuation, if applicable.

06

Additional details

Taxability, batch details, expiry dates, or other information, depending on the business and its requirements.

You do not need to create every optional detail for every product. Configure only the fields relevant to your business.

STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE

How to Create Stock Items in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to create a new stock item.

01
STEP 1

Open Your Company in TallyPrime

Launch TallyPrime and select the company in which you want to create the stock item.

  • Open TallyPrime.
  • Select the required company.
  • Confirm that you are working in the correct company before creating inventory masters.
02
STEP 2

Open the Stock Item Creation Screen

There are two common ways to open the Stock Item Creation screen.

Method 1: Through the Create menu

  1. Open the company in TallyPrime.
  2. Go to Gateway of Tally → Create.
  3. Select Stock Item.
  4. Press Enter.

Method 2: Using Go To

  1. Press Alt+G.
  2. Select or search for Create Master.
  3. Select Stock Item.
  4. Press Enter.
Alt+GCreate MasterStock Item

The Stock Item Creation screen will open.

03
STEP 3

Enter the Stock Item Name

In the Name field, enter the name of the product you want to create.

Example stock item name

Wireless Mouse

Use a clear and consistent naming convention so employees can identify products easily when entering transactions. You may also enter an alias if your business uses an alternative name or product code.

04
STEP 4

Select the Stock Group

In the Under field, select the stock group to which the item belongs.

Example

Stock Item: Wireless Mouse
Stock Group: Computer Accessories

Stock groups help organise related products. If the required group does not exist, you can create it from the selection screen using Alt+C. You can also read how to create masters in TallyPrime for more information about accounting and inventory masters.

If you do not need separate groups for reporting, you can use the default Primary group. However, businesses with many products may benefit from creating suitable groups.

05
STEP 5

Select the Unit of Measurement

In the Units field, select the unit used to measure or count the product.

Product Unit of Measurement
Wireless Mouse Nos
Rice Kg
Cooking Oil Ltr
Fabric Mtr
Bottled Water Nos

If the required unit is unavailable, you can create it from the selection screen using Alt+C.

When creating a unit, enter the appropriate symbol and formal name. For applicable goods, select the relevant Unit Quantity Code (UQC) if required for statutory reporting.

Choose the unit carefully because it affects how quantities are entered and interpreted in inventory transactions.

06
STEP 6

Enter Opening Stock Details

If you already have inventory when starting to use TallyPrime, you may need to enter the opening stock details.

Depending on the configuration and stock item, the opening balance section can include:

  • Opening quantity
  • Opening rate
  • Opening value
Field Example
Opening Quantity 20 Nos
Opening Rate ₹500 per unit
Opening Value ₹10,000

Opening stock calculation

20 × ₹500 = ₹10,000

Enter the actual opening stock details based on your inventory records. If you are creating a new item with no existing stock, leave the opening balance at zero or blank as appropriate.

07
STEP 7

Configure Additional Details if Required

Depending on your business, you may need to configure additional stock item information.

Taxability or applicable statutory details Batch-wise tracking Manufacturing and expiry dates MRP details Additional product descriptions Inventory-related options

These settings depend on the type of business, the TallyPrime features enabled, and the applicable reporting requirements. For a related billing workflow, see the TallyPrime GST Invoice Format guide.

For example, a business selling perishable products may need batch and expiry tracking. A business selling ordinary office supplies may not need those features.

Do not enter tax rates or other statutory information without checking the correct classification and applicable requirements.

08
STEP 8

Save the Stock Item

After entering the required information, review the details.

Press Ctrl+A to accept and save the Stock Item master.

Ctrl+A Save Stock Item

Your stock item is now available for selection in relevant inventory transactions.

For the latest screen details, refer to the official TallyHelp guide to managing stock items in TallyPrime.

Assume ABC Computers sells computer accessories and wants to create a stock item for a wireless keyboard.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Creating Stock Items

1. Choosing the Wrong Stock Group

Assigning products to the wrong group can make inventory reports harder to analyse.

Solution: Create a consistent grouping structure before entering a large number of stock items.

2. Selecting the Wrong Unit of Measurement

Recording an item in kilograms when the business tracks it in numbers can cause confusion during transactions.

Solution: Choose the unit that matches the actual way the product is purchased, stored, and sold.

3. Entering Incorrect Opening Stock

Incorrect opening quantities or rates can affect inventory valuation and stock reports.

Solution: Verify opening quantities and values against physical stock records and previous accounting information.

4. Creating Duplicate Stock Items

Creating names such as Wireless Mouse, Wireless Mouse 1, and Mouse Wireless for the same product can split inventory information across multiple records.

Solution: Search existing stock items before creating a new one and follow a standard naming convention.

5. Ignoring Required Product Details

Some products may require batch tracking, expiry dates, MRP, or statutory information.

Solution: Identify the requirements of your business and configure the relevant fields before recording transactions.

6. Assuming Stock Is Updated Automatically After Master Creation

Creating a stock item only creates the product record. It does not mean stock has been purchased or received.

Solution: Record the relevant opening balance or inventory transaction to maintain accurate stock quantities and values.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Stock Items in TallyPrime

1. How do I create a stock item in TallyPrime?

Open TallyPrime, press Alt+G, select Create Master, choose Stock Item, enter the item name, stock group, unit, and other required details, then press Ctrl+A to save.

2. What is a stock item in TallyPrime?

A stock item is a product or material that a business purchases, sells, manufactures, or tracks as inventory. Examples include laptops, notebooks, furniture, and raw materials.

3. Is creating a stock group compulsory before creating a stock item?

No. You can assign a stock item to the default Primary group or create a suitable stock group when required.

4. What is the difference between a stock item and a stock group?

A stock item represents an individual product or material, while a stock group classifies similar items. For example, Wireless Mouse is a stock item, and Computer Accessories is a stock group.

5. How do I enter opening stock in TallyPrime?

When creating or altering the stock item, enter the opening quantity, rate, and value as applicable. The figures should match your actual inventory records at the beginning of the books.

6. Can I create a stock item while recording a purchase or sales voucher?

Yes. TallyPrime allows you to create a stock item during transaction entry when the required master is not already available. In a relevant selection field, use Alt+C where supported.

7. Can I create multiple stock items in TallyPrime?

Yes. TallyPrime provides multi-stock-item creation options in supported workflows. You can also use supported data-import methods for larger item lists.

8. How can I view the stock quantity after creating an item?

Use the Stock Summary or another relevant inventory report. The quantity shown depends on the opening stock and inventory transactions recorded for that item.

9. Can I change a stock item's details after creating it?

Yes. You can open the relevant Stock Item alteration screen and modify editable details. Take care when changing units or other fields after transactions have already been recorded, as the change may affect how existing records are interpreted.

10. Who should learn stock item creation in TallyPrime?

Commerce students, aspiring accountants, inventory executives, business owners, and people working in retail, trading, and manufacturing can benefit from learning how to create and manage stock items in TallyPrime.
