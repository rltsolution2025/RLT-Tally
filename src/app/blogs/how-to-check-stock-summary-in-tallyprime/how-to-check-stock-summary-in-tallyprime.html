01 · INTRODUCTION

How to Check Stock Summary in TallyPrime

Managing inventory is an important part of running a business. Companies need to know how much stock is available, which products are selling, and how much money is invested in inventory. Without accurate stock records, businesses may face stock shortages, excess inventory, and accounting discrepancies.

TallyPrime makes inventory management easier by providing a Stock Summary report that displays stock quantities and values based on the inventory transactions recorded in the company.

Whether you are an accounting student, business owner, inventory executive, or aspiring accountant, learning how to check Stock Summary in TallyPrime is a useful practical skill.

Quick answer

Open your company in TallyPrime, locate Stock Summary from the Gateway of Tally or the Go To search, and review the stock groups, item quantities and values for the selected period.

In this guide, you will learn how to open the Stock Summary report, understand its columns, view stock details, change the reporting period, troubleshoot common errors, and use inventory reports more effectively.

02 · REPORT BASICS

What Is Stock Summary in TallyPrime?

Stock Summary in TallyPrime is an inventory report that helps users understand the quantity and value of stock available in their business.

The report is calculated from the inventory records and transactions entered in TallyPrime, including purchases, sales, stock transfers, and other relevant inventory movements.

Depending on the company's configuration and the report view, the Stock Summary can display:

Opening stock quantity and value
Stock received during the period
Stock issued or sold
Closing stock quantity and value
Stock groups and individual items
Values based on the configured valuation method

The Stock Summary helps businesses monitor inventory levels and review the value of goods held in stock.

03 · BUSINESS BENEFITS

Why Is Stock Summary Important in TallyPrime?

Stock Summary is useful for businesses that maintain inventory, including retail stores, wholesalers, distributors, manufacturers, and trading companies.

Check available stock: Review the recorded quantity of goods without manually calculating every purchase and sale.
Monitor inventory value: Understand the recorded value based on the configured stock valuation method.
Identify low or excess stock: Find items that may need replenishment or are accumulating in excess.
Support purchase planning: Decide which products to reorder and which purchases can be postponed.
Improve accounting accuracy: Compare inventory reports with purchase, sales and physical stock records.
Support business decisions: Plan purchases, manage working capital and improve stock control.
04 · STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to Check Stock Summary in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to view the Stock Summary report in TallyPrime.

01
STEP 1

Open TallyPrime

  1. Launch TallyPrime on your computer.
  2. Select the company whose inventory information you want to review.
  3. Make sure the correct company is open before proceeding.
02
STEP 2

Open the Stock Summary Report

From the Gateway of Tally, navigate to Stock Summary.

Where to find the report

Gateway of Tally → Stock Summary

In many TallyPrime releases, Stock Summary is available as an option on the Gateway of Tally. You can also use the built-in search or Go To feature to locate it.

Select Stock Summary to open the inventory report.

Menu labels and navigation can vary by TallyPrime version and screen configuration. Use the built-in search if the report is not shown in the same place.

03
STEP 3

Review the Stock Summary

Once the report opens, review the available stock groups, stock items, quantities, and values.

Depending on your configuration and report view, you may see columns for opening balance, inward movement, outward movement, and closing balance.

Use the report to understand how much stock has been recorded and what its current value is.

04
STEP 4

View Individual Stock Items

If the report displays stock groups, select a group to drill down into its items.

For example, a business might maintain the following stock groups:

  • Electronics
  • Office Supplies
  • Accessories

Select the relevant group to view the stock items recorded under it. You can then select an individual item to access more detailed inventory information, where supported.

Learn more: how to create stock items in TallyPrime and organise stock groups and categories.

05
STEP 5

Change the Reporting Period

To review inventory movements for a particular period, change the report dates using the period settings available in TallyPrime.

1

Open period settings

Use the date or period option available on the Stock Summary screen.

2

Enter the required dates

For example, select 1 October 2026 to 14 October 2026.

3

Confirm the period

Review the report for the selected date range.

Important: The closing stock quantity is a balance as of the report's ending date, while inward and outward movements relate to the selected reporting period.

06
STEP 6

Review Stock Quantity and Value

Check the quantity and value columns to understand your inventory position. Pay attention to:

  • Items with unexpectedly low quantities.
  • Products showing negative stock.
  • Items with unusually high stock values.
  • Differences between expected and recorded inventory.
  • Items that may require physical stock verification.

Investigate discrepancies before making purchase or accounting decisions.

07
STEP 7

Export or Print the Report

If you need to share the information with your team, use the available export or printing options in TallyPrime.

Depending on your version and configuration, you may be able to export the report to formats such as Excel or PDF.

Before sharing the report, confirm that the company, reporting period, and inventory values are correct.

05 · REPORT INTERPRETATION

Understanding the Columns in Stock Summary

Understanding the report columns helps you interpret inventory information correctly.

Column Meaning
Opening Stock Quantity or value available at the beginning of the selected period.
Inward Stock received during the selected period.
Outward Stock issued, sold, or otherwise moved out during the selected period.
Closing Stock Quantity or value remaining at the end of the selected period.

The exact column labels and layout can vary depending on the report configuration and TallyPrime version.

06 · PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Practical Example: Checking Stock Summary in TallyPrime

Let us understand Stock Summary with a simple example. Suppose ABC Traders maintains a stock item called Office Chairs.

During the selected reporting period, the business records the following transactions:

Particulars Quantity
Opening stock 50
Purchased during the period 30
Sold during the period 20
Closing stock 60

Closing stock calculation

50 + 30 − 20 = 60 chairs.

When the purchase and sales transactions are correctly recorded in TallyPrime, the Stock Summary should reflect the corresponding stock movement.

How to Interpret the Result

The report indicates that 60 chairs remain in the recorded inventory at the end of the period. The business can use this information to:

  • Check whether additional stock is required.
  • Compare recorded stock with a physical stock count.
  • Review sales and purchase activity.
  • Estimate the value of remaining inventory.

This is a simplified example. Actual stock values depend on purchase costs, stock valuation settings, and the inventory transactions entered in TallyPrime.

07 · INVENTORY VALUATION

How to Check Stock Value in TallyPrime

Stock quantity tells you how many units are available, while stock value helps you understand the inventory's recorded monetary value.

To review stock value:

  1. Open the relevant company.
  2. Navigate to Stock Summary.
  3. Select the required reporting period.
  4. Review the available closing stock value columns.
  5. Drill down into the relevant stock group or item if more detail is needed.
  6. Verify the stock valuation method and underlying transactions if the value appears incorrect.

TallyPrime can calculate inventory values using supported valuation methods configured for the relevant stock items. For example, a business may use an applicable method such as FIFO or Average Cost, depending on its accounting and inventory requirements.

The value displayed can differ depending on the valuation method, purchase costs, and transaction history.

08 · ITEM-LEVEL DETAILS

How to Check Stock Item Details in TallyPrime

If you need information about a specific product, drill down from Stock Summary into the relevant stock group or stock item.

Depending on the available reports and configuration, you may be able to examine:

  • Stock quantities
  • Purchase and sales movements
  • Opening and closing balances
  • Inventory valuation details
  • Relevant transaction information

You can also use other inventory reports available in TallyPrime to investigate individual stock movements. This is useful when a product's closing quantity or value does not match your expectations.

09 · TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Problems When Checking Stock Summary in TallyPrime

Sometimes the Stock Summary report may display unexpected values or quantities. The following are common causes and troubleshooting steps.

1. Stock Summary Shows Zero Quantity

Possible reasons: No opening stock has been entered; purchase transactions have not been recorded correctly; sales or other inventory movements offset the available stock; or the wrong company or reporting period is selected.

Solution: Verify the stock item's opening balance, purchase vouchers, sales vouchers, and report dates.

2. Stock Quantity Is Incorrect

Possible reasons: Incorrect quantities were entered in vouchers; inventory movements are missing; stock transfers or adjustments were recorded incorrectly; or the wrong unit of measurement was selected.

Solution: Review the relevant stock item and inventory transactions. Compare the recorded quantity with supporting purchase, sales, and physical stock records.

3. Stock Value Is Different from Expectations

Possible reasons: The stock valuation method differs from your expectations; purchase costs or additional costs were entered incorrectly; inventory transactions are incomplete; or the reporting period is incorrect.

Solution: Review the item's valuation settings, purchase values, inventory transactions, and selected reporting period.

4. Negative Stock Appears in the Report

Negative stock may occur when recorded sales or stock issues exceed the available recorded quantity.

Solution: Check whether purchases were entered after sales, whether opening stock is missing, and whether inventory vouchers contain errors. Correct the underlying records rather than adjusting quantities without verification.

5. Stock Summary Does Not Match Physical Stock

Physical stock may differ from recorded stock because of unrecorded sales, damaged goods, missing purchase entries, stock transfers, or data-entry errors.

Solution: Conduct a physical stock count, identify the discrepancy, and record the appropriate adjustment using the correct inventory process.

10 · GOOD ACCOUNTING PRACTICE

Best Practices for Managing Stock in TallyPrime

Follow these practices to maintain accurate inventory records:

  • Enter purchase and sales transactions promptly.
  • Maintain correct stock item names, groups, and units of measurement.
  • Record opening stock accurately.
  • Review Stock Summary regularly.
  • Check negative stock and unusual inventory values.
  • Reconcile recorded inventory with physical stock.
  • Use a consistent and appropriate stock valuation method.
  • Maintain supporting documents for inventory adjustments.
  • Review inventory reports before making purchase decisions.

Regular stock monitoring helps businesses reduce errors, improve inventory control, and maintain reliable accounting records.

11 · FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I open Stock Summary in TallyPrime?

Open your company in TallyPrime and select Stock Summary from the Gateway of Tally. You can also use the Go To or search feature to locate the report, depending on your version.

2. What is the shortcut to view Stock Summary in TallyPrime?

The exact navigation depends on your TallyPrime release and screen configuration. You can use the built-in Go To or search feature to find Stock Summary instead of relying on a fixed shortcut.

3. Can I check the closing stock in TallyPrime?

Yes. Open Stock Summary and select the relevant reporting period. Review the closing balance columns to see the recorded stock quantity and value at the end of the period.

4. How do I check stock value in TallyPrime?

Open Stock Summary, review the closing stock value, and drill down into the relevant stock group or item if you need more detail. The displayed value depends on the stock valuation method and recorded transactions.

5. Why is Stock Summary important for accountants?

Stock Summary helps accountants review inventory quantities and values, verify stock movements, investigate discrepancies, and support accurate accounting records.

6. Can I export Stock Summary from TallyPrime?

TallyPrime provides report export and printing options. The available formats depend on the software version and configuration.

7. Does Stock Summary update automatically in TallyPrime?

Stock Summary reflects the inventory transactions recorded in the company. When relevant transactions are entered or corrected, the report generally reflects the updated inventory balances.

8. What is the difference between Stock Summary and Stock Item reports?

Stock Summary provides an overall view of inventory groups, quantities, and values. Stock item and related inventory reports help users examine individual products and their recorded movements in greater detail.
