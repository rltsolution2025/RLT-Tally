Quick Answer

Which shortcuts are useful for sales and purchase entries?

F8 is commonly used to select the Sales voucher and F9 to select the Purchase voucher. F2 changes the date in supported screens, Alt+C can create a master from supported fields, and Alt+G opens Go To for supported reports and features.

Shortcut availability depends on the active screen, configuration and TallyPrime release. Verify the shortcut hints shown in your installation.
01 / GETTING STARTED

TallyPrime Keyboard Shortcuts for Sales and Purchase Entries

Sales and purchase entries are among the most common activities performed by accountants and billing executives. Businesses use sales vouchers to record goods or services sold and purchase vouchers to record goods or services purchased.

Entering these transactions efficiently is important for maintaining accurate accounts, managing inventory and preparing GST records. TallyPrime provides keyboard shortcuts that help users access vouchers, change dates, create masters and complete billing tasks with less menu navigation.

This guide explains useful keyboard shortcuts for sales and purchase entries with practical examples for beginners and accounting professionals. Keep it handy as a quick reference while working in TallyPrime.

Shortcuts can reduce navigation time, but the customer or supplier, invoice details, ledger selection and tax information still need to be checked carefully.
02 / UNDERSTAND THE BASICS

What Are Sales and Purchase Entries in TallyPrime?

A sales entry records a transaction in which a business sells goods or services to a customer. A purchase entry records a transaction in which a business purchases goods or services from a supplier.

Sales entry

Records goods or services sold to a customer, with invoice and applicable tax details.

Purchase entry

Records goods or services purchased from a supplier and the supplier invoice details.
