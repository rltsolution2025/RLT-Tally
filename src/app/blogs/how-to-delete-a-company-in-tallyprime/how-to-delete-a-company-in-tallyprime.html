How to Delete a Company in TallyPrime

Learn how to back up company data, select the correct company, use Alt + D, and avoid mistakes when deleting company records.

Practical Accounting Guide Beginner Friendly Step-by-Step Tutorial

01 · Getting started

Introduction

TallyPrime allows businesses to create and manage company data for accounting, inventory, GST, and financial reporting. Sometimes, users create a company by mistake, set up a duplicate company, or no longer need a particular company's data.

In such situations, knowing how to delete a company in TallyPrime can help you manage your company list efficiently.

However, deleting a company is a permanent action. Before proceeding, back up the company data and confirm that you have selected the correct company.

This guide explains how to delete a company in TallyPrime, which shortcut key to use, how to back up data, and the difference between deleting and shutting a company.

QUICK ANSWER

What is the shortcut to delete a company in TallyPrime?

Back up the company data first. Then open Alt + K → Alter, select the company, open its Company Alteration screen, and press Alt + D. Confirm deletion only after verifying the company and backup.

Important: Deleting a company can permanently remove its data from the selected Tally data location. If you only need to close it temporarily, use Shut Company instead.

02 · Understand the action

What Does Deleting a Company in TallyPrime Mean?

Deleting a company in TallyPrime removes that company's data from the selected Tally data location. The company data may contain:

  • Ledgers and accounting groups
  • Sales and purchase vouchers
  • Payment and receipt entries
  • Inventory and stock records
  • GST-related accounting information
  • Financial reports and transaction history

Deleting a company should not be confused with shutting a company. Shutting a company closes it for the current session while retaining its data, so you can open it again later.

Before you continue: Take a backup before deleting a company. Do not proceed unless you are certain the company is no longer required or you have preserved the data you need.

03 · Step-by-step process

How to Delete a Company in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to delete a company using the standard TallyPrime interface.

01

Open TallyPrime

Launch TallyPrime on your computer. Check which company is currently open and identify the company you want to delete.

02

Back Up Your Company Data

Create a backup and verify that it is saved in a safe location. Depending on your TallyPrime release, backup options may be available through the Data menu, commonly accessed using Alt + Y.

Choose the relevant backup option, select the company and source/destination locations, then complete the backup. Keep the backup on a separate drive or in another secure location where possible.

03

Open the Company Menu

Press Alt + K to open the Company menu and select Alter. This opens the company selection or alteration workflow.

04

Select the Correct Company

Choose the company from the list and open its Company Alteration screen. Carefully verify the company name and relevant details, particularly if multiple companies have similar names.

05

Press Alt + D

On the Company Alteration screen, press Alt + D to select the delete action. TallyPrime will prompt you to confirm the deletion.

06

Confirm the Deletion

Review the confirmation prompt carefully. If you are certain that the selected company should be deleted and your backup is secure, confirm the action as prompted by TallyPrime.

Once deletion is completed, the company will be removed from the relevant company list.

Warning: Company deletion should be treated as permanent through the normal interface. Cancel the operation if you are uncertain whether the data is needed.

05 · Compare the options

Difference Between Delete Company and Shut Company in TallyPrime

Many beginners confuse deleting a company with shutting it. These options have different outcomes.

Feature Delete Company Shut Company
Purpose Removes company data from the selected data location Closes the company in the current session
Is the data retained? Not through the normal company list after deletion Yes
Can you open the company again? Not normally, unless you restore or recover data from a backup Yes
Recommended use Only when the company is no longer required When you have finished working and want to retain the data
Risk Permanent data loss Does not delete company data
Tip: If you only want to close a company and continue working with another one, use Shut Company instead of Delete Company.

06 · Safety checklist

What Should You Do Before Deleting a Company?

Use this checklist before confirming deletion.

  • Verify the company name.
  • Check whether accounting, inventory, or GST records are still needed.
  • Create a backup of the company data.
  • Confirm the backup is stored in a safe location.
  • Verify that the backup is accessible.
  • Obtain necessary authorisation if the company belongs to an organisation.
  • Confirm that you genuinely want to delete the company rather than shut it.

These precautions are especially important when the company contains historical transactions or statutory records.

07 · Troubleshooting

Common Problems When Deleting a Company in TallyPrime

1. The Delete Option Does Not Appear

The current screen may not be the Company Alteration screen, or the selected company may not be in the correct context.

Solution: Navigate to Alt + K → Alter, select the relevant company, and check the available options.

2. The Wrong Company Is Selected

Companies with similar names can easily be confused.

Solution: Verify the company name and relevant details before pressing Alt + D. Cancel the operation if you are uncertain.

3. You Deleted a Company by Mistake

Recovery depends on whether a valid backup or another recoverable copy exists.

Solution: Use the supported restore process to recover company data from the backup. Avoid creating conflicting replacement data before recovery.

4. You Want to Remove a Company Temporarily

You may only want to close a company while keeping its data.

Solution: Use Shut Company instead of Delete Company.

5. You Need to Remove a Group Company

A group company is different from an individual company. Deleting a group company removes the group structure; it does not ordinarily delete the individual member companies' data.

Solution: Verify the effect on the group structure and confirm that individual companies remain available as required before proceeding.

08 · Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the shortcut key to delete a company in TallyPrime?

The shortcut key is Alt + D. Open Alt + K → Alter, select the company, and press Alt + D on the Company Alteration screen.

2. Can I recover a deleted company in TallyPrime?

Recovery generally depends on whether a valid backup or another recoverable copy exists. Keep a backup before deletion because the normal delete action is permanent.

3. Does deleting a company remove all its accounting entries?

Deleting a company removes its associated company data from the selected data location, including transactions and records maintained within that company.

4. Can I delete the currently open company?

Use the standard company alteration workflow and follow the prompts available in your TallyPrime release. If the current company must first be shut, do so before selecting it for deletion.

5. What is the difference between shutting and deleting a company?

Shutting a company closes it for the current session but retains the data. Deleting a company removes its data from the selected data location and should be treated as permanent.

6. Should I take a backup before deleting a company?

Yes. A backup is strongly recommended because it provides a recovery option if you later need the company's accounting records.

7. Can I delete a company and create it again with the same name?

You may be able to create another company with the same name, depending on the existing data and configuration. Creating a new company does not restore the deleted company's transactions, ledgers, or historical records.

09 · Key takeaway

Conclusion

Deleting a company in TallyPrime is straightforward when you follow the correct steps: back up the data, open Alt + K → Alter, select the company, press Alt + D, and confirm the deletion only after checking the details.

Remember that deleting a company is different from shutting it. If you only want to stop working with a company temporarily, shut it instead of deleting it.
