INTRODUCTION

How to Track Stock Movement in TallyPrime

Tracking stock movement is an important part of inventory management for businesses that purchase, store, sell, or transfer goods. It helps businesses understand how products move from suppliers to warehouses and customers while maintaining accurate inventory records.

TallyPrime provides inventory management features that help businesses record stock receipts, sales, purchases, stock transfers, and adjustments. By using the appropriate inventory vouchers and stock reports, businesses can monitor stock availability, review item movements, and identify discrepancies.

Whether you are an accounting student, inventory executive, business owner, or aspiring accountant, learning how to track stock movement in TallyPrime can help you develop practical inventory management skills.

In this guide, you will learn how stock movement works, how to record inventory transactions, how to view stock movement reports, and how to avoid common inventory errors in TallyPrime.

What does stock movement tracking involve?

It involves recording goods received, goods issued, transfers between locations, and stock adjustments, then checking inventory reports to understand the resulting quantities.

BASICS

What Is Stock Movement?

Stock movement refers to the movement of goods into, out of, or between storage locations within a business. It helps businesses understand how inventory changes over a specific period.

Stock In

Goods received from suppliers or returned by customers.

Stock Out

Goods sold to customers, returned to suppliers, or issued for internal use.

Stock Transfer

Goods moved between warehouses, godowns, or storage locations.

Stock Adjustment

Corrections for shortages, excess stock, damage, or physical verification differences.

For example, a business purchases 100 units of a product, sells 40 units, and transfers 20 units to another warehouse. Tracking these movements helps the business understand how much stock remains at each location.

BUSINESS BENEFITS

Why Is Tracking Stock Movement Important?

Businesses need accurate inventory information to make better purchasing, sales, and operational decisions. The key benefits of tracking stock movement in TallyPrime include:

Monitor stock availability: Check available quantities and identify when products need replenishment.
Prevent stock shortages: Identify products selling quickly and plan additional purchases.
Reduce excess inventory: Identify slow-moving products and avoid unnecessary purchases.
Improve inventory accuracy: Record receipts, sales, transfers, and adjustments consistently.
Identify discrepancies: Compare physical and recorded stock to investigate differences.
Support better decisions: Use stock information to plan purchases, storage, and product demand.

STEP-BY-STEP TUTORIAL

Step-by-Step Guide to Track Stock Movement in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to record and monitor inventory movement.

01
CONFIGURATION

Step 1: Enable Inventory Features

First, make sure inventory management is configured for your company.

  1. Open TallyPrime.
  2. Select the required company.
  3. Open the company features or inventory configuration.
  4. Verify that the relevant inventory features are enabled.
  5. Enable godown management if you need to track stock across multiple locations.
  6. Save the configuration.

The available options and menu names may vary depending on your TallyPrime release and company setup.

02
INVENTORY MASTERS

Step 2: Create Stock Groups and Stock Items

Stock groups help organise products into categories, while stock items represent individual products.

Example stock groups

  • Mobile Phones
  • Laptops
  • Accessories

Example stock items

  • Samsung Galaxy Phone
  • HP Laptop
  • Wireless Keyboard

To create a stock item

  1. Open the inventory master creation screen.
  2. Select Stock Item.
  3. Enter the item name.
  4. Select the appropriate stock group.
  5. Choose the unit of measurement.
  6. Enter opening stock details if applicable.
  7. Save the item.

Use consistent item names and units to make inventory reports easier to understand.

When goods arrive from a supplier, record the purchase and inventory details using the appropriate voucher workflow.

In TallyPrime

06
INTERNAL MOVEMENT

Step 6: Record Stock Transfers Between Godowns

Businesses with multiple storage locations may need to transfer goods between warehouses. For example, a business transfers 20 units of Product A from its main warehouse to its Tambaram warehouse.

Location Before Transfer After
Main Warehouse 60 -20 40
Tambaram Warehouse 10 +20 30
Total stock 70 0 net change 70

Steps to record a transfer

  1. Open the appropriate stock journal or inventory transfer voucher.
  2. Select the stock item.
  3. Enter the quantity being transferred.
  4. Select the source godown.
  5. Select the destination godown.
  6. Verify the quantities and save the voucher.

The available workflow may differ depending on the inventory configuration. A correctly recorded internal transfer changes the quantity at each location but does not change the business's total quantity of that item.

Best practice: Record transfers promptly and retain supporting documents such as stock transfer notes or internal movement records when required by your business process.

07
STOCK CORRECTION

Step 7: Record Stock Adjustments

Physical stock verification may reveal differences between the actual quantity and the quantity recorded in TallyPrime.

Example: Physical stock shortage

Stock record Quantity
Recorded stock 50 units
Physical stock 48 units
Difference 2 units shortage

A stock adjustment may be required to account for the two-unit shortage after the reason is investigated.

Use the appropriate inventory voucher or stock reconciliation workflow to record the adjustment. Maintain supporting documentation for shortages, damage, or other differences.

Do not use stock adjustments as a substitute for recording ordinary purchases, sales, or transfers correctly.

REPORTING

How to View Stock Movement Reports in TallyPrime

After recording inventory transactions, review the relevant reports to understand how stock has changed over time. Depending on your TallyPrime version and configuration, the following reports may be useful.

1. Stock Summary

Review opening stock, inward and outward quantities, closing stock, and inventory values where applicable.

2. Stock Item Report

Review transactions and balances for an individual product to identify receipts, sales, transfers, or adjustments.

3. Godown Summary

When godown tracking is enabled, review stock balances by location.

4. Day Book

Review purchase vouchers, sales vouchers, stock journals, and other inventory transactions chronologically.

5. Movement Analysis

Where available, analyse inward and outward quantities over a selected period using report filters.

Tip: Report names, columns, and navigation can vary by release and configuration. If a specific report is unavailable, use the stock item, Stock Summary, godown, or voucher reports that provide the information you need.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Stock Movement Problems in TallyPrime and Their Solutions

1. Stock Balance Is Incorrect

Possible causes: Purchases or sales were not recorded, the wrong stock item was selected, opening stock was entered incorrectly, or an adjustment or transfer was recorded incorrectly.

Solution: Review the stock item report, relevant vouchers, opening balances, and inventory adjustments.

2. Stock Is Missing from a Godown Report

Possible causes: The wrong godown was selected, godown tracking was not configured correctly, or a transfer voucher was not recorded or was entered incorrectly.

Solution: Review the godown configuration and verify the source and destination locations in the relevant vouchers.

3. Stock Quantity Is Negative

Possible causes: Goods were sold before the corresponding receipt was recorded, a purchase or stock receipt was missed, or the transaction date or stock item is incorrect.

Solution: Review the transaction sequence and correct missing or inaccurate vouchers. Do not add stock through an unsupported adjustment simply to hide the discrepancy.

4. Stock Summary Does Not Match Physical Stock

Possible causes: Damage or loss was not recorded, physical stock counting was inaccurate, or transactions were duplicated or omitted.

Solution: Conduct a physical stock check, identify the reason for the difference, and record a supported adjustment with appropriate documentation.

5. Stock Transfer Does Not Update Location Balances Correctly

Possible causes: The wrong source or destination godown was selected, the transfer quantity is incorrect, or the voucher was not saved correctly.

Solution: Review the stock transfer voucher and check the inventory reports for both locations.

INVENTORY CONTROL

Best Practices for Tracking Stock Movement in TallyPrime

Follow these practices to improve inventory accuracy:

  • Record purchases and sales promptly.
  • Use consistent stock item names and units.
  • Maintain separate godowns when location-level tracking is required.
  • Record internal transfers using the appropriate inventory workflow.
  • Investigate physical stock differences before making adjustments.
  • Review Stock Summary and stock item reports regularly.
  • Verify transaction dates and quantities before saving vouchers.
  • Maintain supporting records for damaged, missing, or returned goods.
  • Reconcile recorded inventory with physical stock periodically.
  • Train staff to follow a consistent inventory recording process.

These practices help businesses maintain reliable inventory records and reduce avoidable stock discrepancies.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I track stock movement in TallyPrime?

Create stock items, configure inventory settings, record purchases and sales, enter stock transfers and adjustments, and review reports such as Stock Summary and stock item reports.

2. Which report is used to check stock movement in TallyPrime?

Stock Summary helps you review overall inventory balances. For detailed item-level movements, use the relevant stock item or inventory transaction reports available in your TallyPrime version.

3. How do I track stock in multiple godowns in TallyPrime?

Enable and configure godown management, create the required locations, and select the correct godown when recording stock receipts, issues, and transfers. Review the relevant godown reports to compare balances.

4. How is closing stock calculated?

Closing stock is generally calculated as opening stock plus stock received minus stock issued, adjusted for other relevant inventory movements.

5. How do I record stock transfers in TallyPrime?

Use the appropriate stock journal or inventory transfer workflow, select the stock item, enter the transfer quantity, and specify the source and destination godowns.

6. Can TallyPrime help identify stock discrepancies?

TallyPrime maintains recorded inventory transactions and balances. By comparing inventory reports with physical stock counts, businesses can investigate missing entries, incorrect quantities, and other discrepancies.

7. Is stock movement tracking useful for accounting students?

Yes. Learning stock movement helps accounting students understand inventory vouchers, purchases, sales, stock transfers, inventory valuation, and practical stock reconciliation.

8. What is the difference between stock movement and Stock Summary?

Stock movement refers to the changes in inventory over a period. Stock Summary provides an overview of stock balances and related quantities or values. Detailed movement analysis may require opening the relevant stock item or transaction reports.
