How to Export TallyPrime Reports to Excel and PDF

Learn how to export accounting reports, choose the right file format, verify exported data, and share financial information clearly.

Practical Accounting Guide Beginner Friendly Step-by-Step Tutorial

01 · Getting started

Introduction

Exporting reports from TallyPrime to Excel and PDF is an important skill for accountants, business owners, finance teams, and accounting students. Businesses regularly need to share financial statements, analyse accounting data, maintain records, and prepare reports for management or auditors.

TallyPrime provides options to export various accounting and business reports into different formats, including Excel-compatible spreadsheets and PDF documents. These options help users analyse data, share information, and maintain digital copies of important reports.

Whether you need to export a Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss Account, Trial Balance, Ledger Report, Stock Summary, or GST report, understanding the export process can make your accounting work more efficient.

In this guide, you will learn how to export TallyPrime reports to Excel and PDF, which reports can be exported, how to select the appropriate format, and how to troubleshoot common export problems.

02 · Understand the process

What Is Report Exporting in TallyPrime?

Report exporting in TallyPrime is the process of converting information displayed in a report into an external file format.

Instead of viewing accounting data only within TallyPrime, you can export it to a compatible format for further analysis, printing, sharing, or recordkeeping.

01

Management Reporting

Export a Balance Sheet to PDF for sharing with management or authorised stakeholders.

02

Financial Analysis

Export a Profit and Loss Account to Excel for calculations, comparisons, or summaries.

03

Transaction Review

Export a Ledger Report to Excel for transaction-level review and reconciliation.

04

Inventory Analysis

Export a Stock Summary in a supported format to review inventory quantities and values.

05

GST Review

Export a supported GST report for reconciliation and tax-related review.

Note: The available formats and export options depend on the report, TallyPrime release, and export configuration.

03 · Choose the right format

Why Export TallyPrime Reports to Excel and PDF?

Excel and PDF are both useful, but they serve different purposes.

XLS

Benefits of Exporting to Excel

Excel is useful when you need to work with accounting data after exporting it from TallyPrime.

  • Analyse data using formulas.
  • Sort and filter transactions.
  • Compare monthly or yearly performance.
  • Prepare management reports and charts.
  • Reconcile accounting records.

Example

Calculate the share of expenses spent on rent, salaries, electricity, and other categories.

PDF

Benefits of Exporting to PDF

PDF is generally suitable when you want to share a report in a consistent document format.

  • Share financial statements.
  • Maintain digital report copies.
  • Print reports for meetings.
  • Send reports to authorised stakeholders.
  • Preserve a fixed document layout.

Example

Share a readable Balance Sheet with management or keep a reference copy for recordkeeping.

Excel is generally more suitable for further calculations and analysis, while PDF is convenient for viewing, sharing, and printing.

05 · Excel workflow

How to Export TallyPrime Reports to Excel

Follow these steps to export an accounting report to an Excel-compatible format. The exact field names and file types can vary by TallyPrime release.

01

Open TallyPrime and Select the Company

Launch TallyPrime, select the company containing the required accounting data, and verify that you have opened the correct company before exporting.

02

Open the Required Report

Navigate to the report you want to export. For example, open the Balance Sheet from the available reports or statements menu. You can follow a similar workflow for the Profit and Loss Account, Trial Balance, Ledger Report, Stock Summary, and other supported reports.

03

Select the Reporting Period

Confirm the report's date range before exporting.

  • Monthly example: 1 October to 31 October 2026.
  • Quarterly example: 1 October to 31 December 2026.
  • Financial-year example: 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

These periods are illustrative. Choose the dates relevant to your actual accounting requirement.

04

Open the Export Option

With the required report displayed, open the Export option. Alt + E is commonly used to access the export workflow. Depending on your version and current screen, the option may also be available through a menu.

05

Select an Excel-Compatible Format

Locate the format or file type option and select the available Excel-compatible spreadsheet format. Choose the export details and destination settings shown on your screen. Do not assume every release provides identical file types or configuration fields.

06

Choose the Export Destination

Select the folder where the report should be saved. For example, create a folder named TallyPrime Reports and save the file as Balance_Sheet_October_2026. Use a clear naming convention so the report can be identified later.

07

Configure the Export Settings

Review the settings available for the selected report and format. Depending on the options provided, you may be able to configure the file destination, report structure, data presentation, or other report-specific settings.

Tip: Review the settings before exporting because the available options depend on the report and TallyPrime release.
08

Export and Verify the File

Confirm the export operation. Open the destination folder and check the exported file in Microsoft Excel or a compatible spreadsheet application. Verify headings, dates, amounts, and totals. If information is missing or formatting is unexpected, review the export configuration and repeat the process if needed.

06 · PDF workflow

How to Export TallyPrime Reports to PDF

PDF is useful when you need a report that can be shared or printed in a consistent layout.

01

Open the Required Report

Open TallyPrime, select the correct company, and navigate to the report you need, such as the Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss Account, Trial Balance, Ledger Report, Sales Register, or Stock Summary.

02

Select the Reporting Period

Choose the relevant date range or financial period. If you are sharing a monthly Profit and Loss Account, ensure that the report covers the intended month before exporting.

03

Open the Export Settings

With the report displayed, press Alt + E to access the export workflow, or use the export menu option available in your version.

04

Select PDF as the Export Format

Choose PDF from the available output formats. If PDF is not immediately visible, review the format options and settings displayed for that report.

05

Choose the File Name and Destination

Specify the file name and the folder in which the report should be saved. For example: Profit_and_Loss_October_2026.pdf. Use meaningful names that include the report and reporting period.

06

Review the PDF Settings

Check the available page layout and report presentation options. Where supported, review page orientation, paper size, report layout, page breaks, and print or export presentation settings. Not all options are available for every report or version.

07

Export the Report

Confirm the export operation and allow the file to be generated.

08

Open and Check the PDF

Open the exported PDF and confirm that the company name and reporting period are correct, all figures are visible, totals are readable, tables are not cut off, and multiple pages appear in the correct order. If the report is difficult to read, adjust the available layout settings and export it again.

09 · Troubleshooting

Common Problems When Exporting TallyPrime Reports

1. The Exported File Is Not Found

Possible reasons: The file was saved to a different folder, the destination path was entered incorrectly, or the export did not complete successfully.

Solution: Review the destination configured during export, search the selected folder, and repeat the export if necessary.

2. The Excel File Does Not Open Correctly

Possible reasons: The selected format is not supported by the spreadsheet application, the file was not generated completely, or the extension does not match the actual format.

Solution: Re-export using a supported Excel-compatible format and open the resulting file with a compatible application.

3. The Exported Report Has Missing Information

Possible reasons: The wrong report or date range was selected, the report configuration excludes details, or the export settings are unsuitable.

Solution: Check the original report in TallyPrime, confirm the reporting period, review the available export settings, and generate the file again.

4. The PDF Report Is Cut Off

Possible reasons: The report contains many columns, the page orientation or layout is unsuitable, or the selected page size does not accommodate the report.

Solution: Review available page layout settings and preview the report before exporting it again.

5. The Export Option Is Not Working

Possible reasons: The current screen does not support the expected action, a configuration or permission issue is preventing the operation, or the workflow differs in your installed release.

Solution: Confirm that the required report is open, check the export menu available on your screen, and verify applicable permissions and software configuration.

6. The Exported Numbers Do Not Match Expectations

Possible reasons: The wrong company was selected, the reporting period is incorrect, or the original report contains unexpected filters or settings.

Solution: Verify the source report in TallyPrime before assuming the exported file is incorrect. Confirm the company, period, report settings, and totals.

11 · Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I export TallyPrime reports to Excel?

Open the required report, select the relevant reporting period, access Export—commonly using Alt + E—and choose an available Excel-compatible format. Select the destination, complete the export, and verify the resulting file.

2. How do I export TallyPrime reports to PDF?

Open the report, choose the required period, access the export workflow, select PDF, specify the file name and destination, and confirm the export. Open the resulting PDF to verify the report layout and figures.

3. What is the shortcut key for exporting reports in TallyPrime?

Alt + E is commonly used to access the Export workflow in TallyPrime. Available options and screen behaviour may vary by version and context.

4. Can I export a Balance Sheet from TallyPrime?

Yes. You can generally export the Balance Sheet using the available export options. Select the correct reporting period and choose a supported format, such as an Excel-compatible spreadsheet or PDF.

5. Can I export the Profit and Loss Account to Excel?

Yes. Open the Profit and Loss Account, select the required period, and use the available export workflow to generate an Excel-compatible file.

6. Can I export TallyPrime reports to Google Sheets?

You can generally export a report in a spreadsheet-compatible format, then upload or import the file into Google Sheets if the format is supported. Review the imported data and formatting for accuracy.

7. Why should I export TallyPrime reports to Excel?

Excel is useful for filtering, sorting, calculating, comparing, and analysing accounting data. It can help accountants prepare management summaries and perform additional calculations.

8. Why should I export TallyPrime reports to PDF?

PDF is useful for sharing and printing reports in a consistent document layout. It is suitable for many management reviews, internal documentation needs, and authorised reporting workflows.

9. Can beginners learn report exporting in TallyPrime?

Yes. Beginners can practise exporting the Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss Account, Trial Balance, Ledger Report, and Stock Summary to understand practical accounting workflows.

10. Does exporting a TallyPrime report change the original accounting data?

Exporting a report normally creates an external copy of the displayed information. It does not itself alter the underlying accounting transactions in TallyPrime.

12 · Key takeaway

Conclusion

Exporting TallyPrime reports to Excel and PDF is a useful skill for accounting students, working accountants, and business owners.

Excel-compatible files help users analyse financial information, perform calculations, and prepare summaries. PDF files make it easier to share and print reports in a consistent layout.

By learning how to select the correct report, set the reporting period, choose the appropriate export format, and verify the generated file, you can make accounting work more efficient and organised.

Regular practice with the Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss Account, Trial Balance, Ledger Report, and other reports can help you build confidence in using TallyPrime for everyday accounting tasks.
