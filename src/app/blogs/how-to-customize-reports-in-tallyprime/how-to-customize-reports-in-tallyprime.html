How do you customize a report in TallyPrime?

Open the required report, select the reporting period, apply any available report filters or configuration options, drill down to verify details, and export the finished report in a supported format when needed.

Remember: Available filters and display options vary by report and TallyPrime version. Verify report totals against the underlying ledgers and vouchers.

01 · Getting started

Introduction

Reports play an important role in accounting because they help businesses understand their financial position, monitor transactions, and make informed decisions. However, the default reports may not always display the exact information a business owner or accountant needs.

Learning how to customize reports in TallyPrime helps users view relevant data, filter transactions by date, examine specific ledgers, adjust report details, and export information for further analysis.

TallyPrime provides several report configuration and navigation options that allow users to examine accounting information according to their requirements. Depending on the report and software version, users can change the reporting period, apply filters, alter the level of detail, and configure available display options.

In this guide, you will learn how to customize reports in TallyPrime step by step, explore common accounting reports, understand useful shortcuts, and troubleshoot common report-related problems.

02 · The basics

What Is Report Customization in TallyPrime?

Report customization in TallyPrime means adjusting how accounting and business information is displayed so that it is easier to understand and analyse.

Instead of reviewing every transaction, users can focus on specific dates, ledgers, vouchers, groups, or other available report criteria. For example, an accountant who wants to review monthly expenses can change the reporting period and examine the relevant expense ledgers.

01

Focus on a Period

Analyse transactions from a selected day, month, quarter, or financial period where supported.

02

Review Balances

Inspect account balances, outstanding receivables, and payables.

03

Control Detail

Display summary information or drill down to transaction-level details where available.

04

Share Information

Export reports for management reviews or additional analysis using supported formats.

Note: The exact customization options depend on the report, the user's permissions, and the TallyPrime version.

04 · Step-by-step tutorial

How to Customize Reports in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to explore the main report customization options. The specific menu labels and available settings can vary by report and software version.

01

Open the Required Report

Open TallyPrime and select the relevant company. Use report navigation or Go To search to find the report you need, such as Trial Balance, Profit and Loss Account, Balance Sheet, or Day Book. Open the report and review the information displayed.

Example: For monthly expenses, open the Profit and Loss Account and examine the expense groups or ledgers.

02

Change the Reporting Period

The reporting period determines which dates are covered by a report. Locate the period option, select the required start and end dates, confirm the selection, and review the updated report. Alt + F2 is commonly used to change the reporting period in supported reports.

Example

To analyse expenses for September 2026, set the reporting period to 1 September 2026 through 30 September 2026.

03

Use Available Filters

Depending on the report, available criteria may include ledger or account group, voucher type, reporting period, transaction details, or other report-specific conditions. Open the report's filtering or configuration options and select the relevant criteria.

Example: To examine payment transactions, use the relevant report and its available voucher-type or transaction-selection options.

Important: Not every report supports the same filters. Do not assume a filter available in one report will also appear in another.
04

Change the Level of Detail

Use summary information when you need an overview and drill-down options when you need to inspect individual transactions.

  1. Open the Trial Balance.
  2. Locate the ledger you want to investigate.
  3. Open the ledger balance or use the available drill-down action.
  4. Review the underlying transactions and open individual vouchers when needed.

This helps identify the transactions contributing to a balance without reviewing unrelated entries.

05

Configure Available Report Display Options

Report-specific configuration may allow you to display additional details, show or hide supported information, view ledger balances in different ways, change transaction detail, or display accounting information in another available format.

Open the report's on-screen configuration options and review the available settings before applying changes.

Tip: Start with one small adjustment, such as displaying additional transaction details, and confirm that the report answers your question.

06

Review Voucher Details

If a report shows an unexpected amount, inspect the transactions contributing to that balance. For example, if an expense ledger shows ₹25,000 for a month but you expected ₹20,000, open the relevant details and check whether the difference comes from an incorrect entry, an additional expense, or a transaction recorded in the wrong period.

Correct the underlying accounting entry when necessary rather than changing report settings to hide the discrepancy.

07

Export the Customized Report

After reviewing the report, use the available export option to generate a file in a supported format, such as Excel or PDF where available.

  1. Open the required report and select the reporting period.
  2. Apply the available configuration settings.
  3. Review the report totals and detail.
  4. Select Export and choose a supported format and destination.
  5. Open the exported file and verify the information before sharing.

The exported report may reflect the currently selected period and configuration, so verify these settings before distributing the file.

07 · Troubleshooting

Common Problems When Customizing Reports in TallyPrime

1. The Report Shows the Wrong Date Range

Possible cause: The report is using an unexpected reporting period.

Solution: Check the selected start and end dates and select the correct reporting period.

2. Transactions Are Missing from the Report

Possible causes: The transaction falls outside the selected period, was recorded in a different ledger, is excluded by a report filter, or was not saved as expected.

Solution: Verify the date range, ledger, voucher details, and report settings.

3. The Report Shows Too Much Information

Possible cause: The report displays more transaction-level detail than necessary.

Solution: Review available configuration options or return to a summary view where supported.

4. The Report Total Appears Incorrect

Possible causes: Incorrect accounting entries, transactions in the wrong period, incorrect ledger classification, or duplicate or missing vouchers.

Solution: Drill down into the relevant balances and verify the underlying transactions. Do not assume the problem is caused by report display settings.

5. Exported Information Looks Different

Possible causes: The export format handles columns or page layout differently, the report contains more detail than fits the layout, or export settings differ from expectations.

Solution: Review export options, choose an appropriate format, and inspect the exported file before distributing it.

09 · Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I customize reports in TallyPrime?

Open the required report, select the reporting period, use available filters and configuration options, and drill down into transaction details where needed. You can then export the report using a supported format.

2. Can I change the date range of a report in TallyPrime?

Yes. In supported reports, use the reporting-period option to select the required dates. Alt + F2 is commonly used to change the period.

3. Can I filter reports by ledger in TallyPrime?

The available ledger-selection and filtering options depend on the report. You can often open the relevant ledger report directly or drill down from a summary report to inspect transactions.

4. Can I export customized TallyPrime reports to Excel?

TallyPrime supports exporting reports in available formats, which may include Excel and PDF depending on the report and version. Verify the export options on your screen.

5. Which reports can I customize in TallyPrime?

Common reports include the Trial Balance, Profit and Loss Account, Balance Sheet, Day Book, Cash Book, Bank Book, and outstanding receivables and payables reports. Available customization options vary by report.

6. Does customizing a report change the original accounting entries?

Changing a report's period, filters, or display configuration does not normally change the underlying accounting entries. Editing or altering a voucher is a separate action and should be performed only when necessary.

7. Why is my TallyPrime report showing incorrect figures?

Incorrect figures may result from wrong accounting entries, missing transactions, incorrect ledger classification, or an unexpected reporting period. Review the report settings and drill down into underlying vouchers to identify the cause.

8. Can beginners learn report customization in TallyPrime?

Yes. Beginners can practise changing reporting periods, opening accounting reports, examining ledger balances, reviewing vouchers, and exporting reports using a properly configured practice company.

10 · Key takeaway

Conclusion

Learning how to customize reports in TallyPrime helps accountants, students, and business owners analyse financial information more effectively.

By changing reporting periods, using available filters, adjusting report details, examining underlying vouchers, and exporting relevant information, users can prepare reports that better support everyday accounting tasks and business decisions.

The most important skill is understanding what the report represents and how to verify its figures against the underlying accounting records.
