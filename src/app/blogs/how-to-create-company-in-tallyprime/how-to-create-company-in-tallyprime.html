How to Create a Company in TallyPrime

Follow this step-by-step guide to enter company details, choose the financial year, set the books beginning date, configure GST where applicable and prepare your company for accounting transactions.

9–11 min read Beginner guide 10 steps
Quick Answer

How do you create a company in TallyPrime?

Open TallyPrime, select Create Company, enter the business details, set the financial year and books beginning date, configure GST if applicable, review the settings and save the company. After creation, set up the required ledgers, groups, stock items and other accounting features before recording transactions.

Check dates and statutory details carefully before saving.
01 / GETTING STARTED

How to Create a Company in TallyPrime?

Company creation is one of the first practical tasks when starting with TallyPrime. A company is the accounting data environment where you maintain an organisation’s business and financial records.

In this guide, you will learn how to create a company in TallyPrime step by step, what information to keep ready, how to set the financial year and books beginning date, how to configure GST if it applies, and what to do after the company has been created.

Before you begin, keep your company name, business address, accounting start date and applicable GST details ready.

03 / UNDERSTAND THE BASICS

What Is a Company in TallyPrime?

A company in TallyPrime is the accounting data environment in which you maintain the financial and business records of an organisation. Each company has its own details, configuration and accounting data.

Ledgers & groups
Sales & purchases
Payments & receipts
Inventory
GST transactions
Financial reports
Banking information
Customers & suppliers

For example, if you maintain the accounts of ABC Traders, you can create an ABC Traders company in TallyPrime and record its transactions within that company.

How to Create a Company in TallyPrime

Follow these steps in order. The exact labels or screen arrangement may vary by TallyPrime version and configuration.

01
Step 01

Open TallyPrime

Start TallyPrime on your computer. If no company is open, look for the option to create or select a company. Choose Create Company. If another company is already open, access the company-related options and select the option to create a new company.

05
Step 05

Enter Contact Details

Enter the company’s current contact information. Depending on the fields available and the business requirements, this may include:

  • Phone number

06
Step 06

Set the Financial Year Beginning From

The Financial Year Beginning From date identifies the start of the company’s financial year. For businesses following the Indian financial year, it generally begins on 1 April.

Example financial year start1 April 2026

Choose the date that matches the company’s accounting period so that the financial records and reports align with the intended year.

07
Step 07

Set Books Beginning From

The Books Beginning From field specifies the date from which you want to start maintaining the books in TallyPrime. This date does not always have to match the financial year beginning date.

Financial year begins1 April 2026
Books begin1 June 2026

The example above shows different dates. Select the books beginning date according to when you intend to start maintaining the company’s records in TallyPrime.

Remember the difference

Financial Year Beginning From = the beginning of the financial year. Books Beginning From = the date from which you maintain the books in TallyPrime.

08
Step 08

Configure GST in TallyPrime

If the business is registered under GST, configure the settings according to its registration and applicable requirements. Depending on the business and configuration, you may need to enter:

  • GST registration status
  • Registration type
  • GSTIN/UIN
  • State
  • Applicable GST settings
Illustrative example
GST RegistrationRegular, if applicable
GSTINEnter the business GSTIN

Verify the GST details before saving. Incorrect details can affect tax calculations, transaction recording and GST-related reports.

09
Step 09

Configure Other Company Features

Enable features according to the business’s needs. You do not necessarily need to activate every feature during company creation.

  • Accounting
  • Inventory
  • GST
  • Payroll
  • Banking
  • Cost centres
  • Budgets
  • Security
  • Multiple currencies

A small service business may mainly require accounting and GST, while a trading or manufacturing business may also need inventory management.

10
Step 10

Review and Save the Company

Before accepting the configuration, review the information you entered:

  • Company name
  • Address and state
  • Contact details
  • Financial year
  • Books beginning date
  • GST details
  • Enabled features

When the information is correct, accept the configuration to create the company. You can then continue with the accounting setup.

07 / NEXT STEPS

What to Do After Creating a Company in TallyPrime?

Company creation is only the first step. Build the accounting structure before recording transactions.

1. Create Groups

Groups classify and organise ledgers. Examples include Sundry Debtors, Sundry Creditors, Bank Accounts, Cash-in-Hand, Sales Accounts, Purchase Accounts, Direct Expenses and Indirect Expenses.

2. Create Ledgers

Ledgers record specific accounts and transaction categories. Common examples include Cash, Bank, Sales, Purchase, Rent, Salary, Electricity, customer accounts and supplier accounts.

3. Create Stock Items

Product-based businesses may need Stock Groups, Stock Categories, Units of Measurement, Stock Items and Godowns to manage inventory.

4. Configure GST

If GST applies, check the GST-related ledgers, tax details and inventory configurations before recording sales and purchase transactions.

5. Start Recording Transactions

Begin practising voucher types such as Payment, Receipt, Contra, Sales, Purchase, Journal, Debit Note and Credit Note.

08 / IMPORTANT SETTINGS

Financial Year vs Books Beginning From in TallyPrime

These settings are related but describe two different dates.

Financial Year Beginning From

The date on which the company’s financial year begins.

Example1 April 2026

Books Beginning From

The date from which you want to maintain accounting books in TallyPrime.

Example1 July 2026

Example

If the financial year starts on 1 April 2026 but you intend to begin maintaining the books from 1 July 2026, the two settings may reflect those different dates. Choose them based on your business’s accounting requirements.

09 / COMPANY MANAGEMENT

Can You Create Multiple Companies in TallyPrime?

Yes. TallyPrime can be used to maintain data for multiple companies. For example, an accountant who manages more than one business may maintain separate company data for:

ABC Traders XYZ Enterprises Ravi Services

Each company can have its own accounting data and configuration. This can be useful for accountants, consultants and businesses managing multiple entities.

10 / COMPANY MANAGEMENT

Can You Alter Company Details After Creating a Company?

Yes. Company information and various configuration settings can be altered after the company has been created. Depending on what needs to be changed, you may update:

  • Address
  • Contact details
  • GST-related information
  • Company configuration
  • Security settings
  • Other company details

Make important changes carefully

Changes to accounting or statutory settings may affect transactions and reports. Review their consequences before altering important information.

12 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Creating a Company in TallyPrime

How do I create a company in TallyPrime?

Open TallyPrime, select Create Company, enter the business details, set the financial year and books beginning date, configure GST if applicable, review the information and save the company.

What is the first step in creating a company in TallyPrime?

Open TallyPrime and select Create Company. If another company is open, use the company-related options to start creating a new company.

Can I create a company without GST in TallyPrime?

Yes. GST configuration depends on the business registration status and applicable requirements. Configure GST when it applies to the business.

What date should I enter as the financial year in TallyPrime?

For businesses following the Indian financial year, the financial year generally begins on 1 April. Use the date that matches the company accounting period.

What is Books Beginning From in TallyPrime?

Books Beginning From is the date from which you want to start maintaining the company accounting books in TallyPrime.

Can the financial year and books beginning date be different?

Yes. They can differ depending on when the business wants to begin maintaining its books in TallyPrime and its accounting requirements.

Can I create multiple companies in TallyPrime?

Yes. TallyPrime can maintain data for multiple companies, with separate accounting data and configuration for each company.

Can I change company details after creating a company?

Various company details and settings can be altered after creation. Change important accounting and statutory settings carefully because they may affect transactions and reports.

What should I do after creating a company in TallyPrime?

Create the necessary groups, ledgers and stock items, configure GST if applicable, and then begin recording business transactions.

Is TallyPrime company creation difficult for beginners?

The basic process is straightforward. Understanding the purpose of each setting and practising with business examples helps beginners use TallyPrime more effectively.
