Tally Course: Fees, Duration, Syllabus & Career Opportunities

Explore Tally course fees, duration, syllabus, eligibility, practical training and career opportunities before choosing a Tally course.

August 22, 2026 7 min read Tally & Finance

A Tally course can be a practical option for students, job seekers and professionals who want to develop accounting software skills.

But before enrolling, learners usually have four questions:

What should you know before joining a Tally course?
  • How much does a Tally course cost?
  • How long does it take?
  • What is included in the syllabus?
  • What jobs can I get after completing it?

This guide explains the factors you should consider.

01

What Is a Tally Course?

A Tally course teaches learners how to use TallyPrime for accounting and business-related activities.

A comprehensive course may combine software training with accounting fundamentals, GST, inventory and practical business scenarios.

02

Tally Course Fees

There is no single standard Tally course fee across India.

Fees can vary depending on:

Course Duration

Therefore, don't select a course only because it is the cheapest. Compare what is actually included.

03

Tally Course Duration

Course duration can range from short-term introductory training to longer job-oriented programs.

Before enrolling, ask:

  • How many hours of training are included?
  • Is it live or recorded?
  • How many practical exercises are provided?
  • Are GST and inventory included?
  • Is payroll included?
  • Are projects included?

07

What Should You Look for in a Tally Institute?

Practical Training

Don't choose a course that focuses entirely on theory.

Updated Software Training

Check whether the institute trains students on the current version being used in their curriculum.

Realistic Business Examples

Students should practice transactions similar to those encountered in businesses.

Trainer Support

Being able to ask questions can significantly improve learning.

Career Guidance

Career guidance can help students understand realistic job opportunities and skill requirements.

Is Tally Certification Enough?

A certificate can demonstrate course completion, but employers may also assess practical skills.

Therefore, focus on what you can actually do.

Practical skills should be an important part of your Tally learning journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee for a Tally course?

Fees vary by institute, duration and syllabus. Always check the current fee directly with the training provider.

How long is a Tally course?

Duration varies from short-term introductory courses to comprehensive programs.

Is Tally useful after 12th?

Yes. It can be a useful practical skill for students interested in accounts and office-related careers.

Does Tally include GST?

Many comprehensive Tally courses include GST-related training, but students should verify the syllabus before enrolling.

Is Tally good for a career?

Tally can be useful as a professional skill, particularly when combined with accounting, Excel and practical experience.
