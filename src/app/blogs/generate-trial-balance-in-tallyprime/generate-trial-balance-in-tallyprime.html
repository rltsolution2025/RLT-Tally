How to Generate a Trial Balance in TallyPrime

Learn how to open the Trial Balance, change the reporting period, view ledger-wise details, display opening balances, and investigate debit-credit differences.

Practical Accounting Guide Beginner Friendly Step-by-Step Tutorial

01 · Getting started

Introduction

A Trial Balance is one of the most important accounting reports used to check the debit and credit balances of a business. It helps accountants review ledger balances, identify certain accounting discrepancies, and prepare financial statements.

With TallyPrime, you can generate a Trial Balance report in a few steps without manually preparing the entire report in a spreadsheet. Whether you are a commerce student, a beginner learning accounting, or a working accountant, understanding this report is an essential practical skill.

This guide explains how to view a Trial Balance in TallyPrime, change the reporting period, display ledger-wise details and opening balances, understand debit and credit totals, and troubleshoot common differences.

For more report-analysis skills, see our guides on customizing reports in TallyPrime and using filters in TallyPrime reports.

QUICK ANSWER

How do you generate a Trial Balance in TallyPrime?

Open the correct company, press Alt + G to open Go To, search for Trial Balance, and press Enter. Select the reporting period and use the available options to review group-wise or ledger-wise balances.

Important: Equal debit and credit totals confirm arithmetic agreement, but do not prove that every transaction is complete, correctly classified, or recorded in the correct ledger.

02 · Core accounting concept

What Is a Trial Balance?

A Trial Balance is an accounting report that lists ledger accounts and their balances under debit and credit columns for a specified date or period. It is used to check whether total debit balances equal total credit balances in the accounting records.

A business may have ledger accounts for cash, bank, capital, sales, purchases, rent, debtors, and creditors. The Trial Balance brings their relevant balances together in one report.

01

Debit-credit check

Review whether the debit and credit totals agree arithmetically.

02

Ledger review

Examine individual accounts and investigate balances that appear unexpected.

03

Final accounts

Support the preparation and review of financial statements.

04

Opening balances

Compare carried-forward balances with previous accounting records.

Keep in mind: Some errors—such as transactions posted to the wrong ledger or completely omitted transactions—may not cause the Trial Balance totals to differ.

04 · Step-by-step process

How to Generate a Trial Balance in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to open the report and use its main viewing and configuration options. Menu labels can vary slightly by TallyPrime version.

01

Open Your Company

Launch TallyPrime and select the company for which you want to generate the Trial Balance. Verify that the correct company is open and that the required accounting transactions have been entered.

02

Open the Trial Balance Report

Press Alt + G to open Go To, type Trial Balance, select it from the available options, and press Enter. Alternatively, use the relevant report menus, such as Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Trial Balance, where available.

03

Select the Reporting Period

By default, the report may display balances as of the date of the last voucher entry. Press F2 to select the required date or period where supported, enter the appropriate dates, and review the updated report. For example, to review a financial year, select 1 April 2025 through 31 March 2026 if that is the period you need.

04

Understand the Report Structure

The Trial Balance generally displays account groups and their balances under debit and credit columns. These can include Capital Account, Fixed Assets, Current Assets, Sundry Debtors, Sundry Creditors, Sales Accounts, Purchase Accounts, Direct Expenses, Indirect Expenses, Bank Accounts, and Cash-in-Hand.

05

View the Trial Balance Ledger-Wise

To examine individual ledgers rather than only group totals, press F5 to switch to Ledger-wise view where supported. Review each ledger and its debit or credit balance. Use the same option to return to group-wise view where available.

06

View the Detailed Trial Balance

Press Alt + F5 to access the detailed view where supported. If needed, open F12 (Configure) and enable Expand all levels in Detailed format. Review the expanded groups and the ledgers contributing to each balance.

07

Display Opening Balances

Press F12 to open Configure and set Show Opening Balance to Yes, where the option is available. Confirm the configuration and compare opening and closing balances. This is useful when checking carried-forward balances or migrating accounting data.

08

Review Debit and Credit Totals

Check the total debit and credit balances. Under a correctly balanced double-entry system, these totals should agree, subject to the report configuration and data being reviewed. If they do not, investigate the underlying accounting records rather than making an arbitrary adjustment.

Reference: For supported report options and configuration details, see the official TallyHelp guide to viewing the Trial Balance.

06 · Troubleshooting

How to Fix a Trial Balance Difference in TallyPrime

If the debit and credit totals do not match, investigate the underlying accounting records before making changes.

1. Check Opening Balances

Incorrect or missing opening balances may cause a difference. Enable Show Opening Balance through F12 (Configure), where available, and compare balances with the previous period's closing records.

2. Review Ledger Entries

Examine relevant ledgers and vouchers for incorrect amounts, missing postings, or transactions recorded in the wrong account. Use the available drill-down options to inspect underlying entries.

3. Check Voucher Entries

Review payment, receipt, sales, purchase, journal, and other relevant vouchers. Verify the amounts, accounts, and debit-credit treatment.

4. Verify Imported Accounting Data

If accounting information was migrated or imported, compare the transferred ledger balances with the source records and correct confirmed discrepancies.

5. Recheck the Trial Balance

After correcting an identified error, return to the report and review the totals again. Do not enter an arbitrary adjustment merely to make the totals match; find the cause and apply the appropriate correction.

07 · Keep records accurate

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Generating a Trial Balance

01

Selecting the Wrong Period

Confirm the reporting date or period so the report includes the transactions you intend to review.

02

Using the Wrong Company

Verify the selected company before generating or exporting the report.

03

Ignoring Opening-Balance Differences

Opening balances can affect carried-forward accounts. Compare them with previous records when appropriate.

04

Assuming Equal Totals Mean Every Entry Is Correct

A balanced Trial Balance can still contain incomplete, misclassified, or incorrectly posted transactions.

05

Ignoring Ledger Classifications

Incorrect ledger groups can affect how report balances are presented and interpreted.

06

Changing Balances Without Verification

Review the supporting vouchers and identify the problem before making accounting corrections.

07

Confusing Trial Balance with the Balance Sheet

These reports serve different purposes. Use the report that matches the question you are trying to answer.

08 · Report comparison

10 · Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Trial Balance in TallyPrime

1. How do I generate a Trial Balance in TallyPrime?

Open your company, press Alt + G, search for Trial Balance, and press Enter. Select the required reporting period and review the debit and credit balances.

2. Which shortcut key opens the Trial Balance in TallyPrime?

Alt + G opens the Go To feature. Search for Trial Balance and open the report from the available options.

3. Can I view the Trial Balance ledger-wise in TallyPrime?

Yes. Open the Trial Balance and press F5 to switch to the ledger-wise view, where supported by the current screen and version.

4. How do I display opening balances in the Trial Balance?

Open the Trial Balance, press F12 (Configure), and set Show Opening Balance to Yes, where the option is available.

5. Why are the debit and credit totals different in my Trial Balance?

Possible causes include incorrect opening balances, missing or incorrectly posted entries, incomplete accounting data, or other inconsistencies. Investigate the underlying records before correcting them.

6. Does the Trial Balance show closing stock in TallyPrime?

The standard Trial Balance does not ordinarily display closing stock as a separate balance. Closing stock is generally reflected through inventory valuation and the relevant financial-reporting process. Review the Stock Summary and financial reports for inventory information.

7. Can I generate a Trial Balance for a specific month?

Yes. Use the period-selection option in the report to select the dates you want to review.

8. Can I export the Trial Balance from TallyPrime?

TallyPrime provides report export options subject to the report and version. Open the Trial Balance and use the available export option to select a supported format, such as Excel or PDF where available.

9. Is a Trial Balance enough to confirm that all accounting entries are correct?

No. Equal debit and credit totals confirm arithmetic agreement, but do not prove that every transaction is complete, correctly classified, or recorded in the correct account.

10. Who should learn how to generate a Trial Balance?

Commerce students, aspiring accountants, business owners, accounts assistants, and professionals working with accounting software can all benefit from learning to generate and interpret a Trial Balance.

11 · Key takeaway

Conclusion

Learning how to generate a Trial Balance in TallyPrime helps accountants review ledger balances, investigate certain discrepancies, and support the preparation of financial statements.

The essential steps are to open the correct company, find the Trial Balance through Go To, select the right period, examine group-wise or ledger-wise information, review opening balances where required, and investigate any difference using the underlying vouchers.

Remember that equal debit and credit totals are an important check, but they do not guarantee that every accounting entry is correct.
