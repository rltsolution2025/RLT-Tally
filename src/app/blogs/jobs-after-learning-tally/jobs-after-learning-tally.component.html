Quick Answer

What jobs can you get after learning Tally?

Possible roles include Accounts Assistant, Junior Accountant, Billing Executive, Accounts Executive, Payroll Assistant, GST Executive or GST Assistant, Finance Assistant and Tally Operator.

01

What Jobs Can You Get After Learning Tally?

Learning TallyPrime can help you develop a practical accounting software skill, but many students want to know what happens after completing the course.

What jobs can you actually apply for?

The answer depends on your qualification, accounting knowledge, experience and additional skills.

02

Career Opportunities After Learning Tally

TallyPrime knowledge can be relevant to several accounting and finance-related entry-level roles.

01

Accounts Assistant

Supports daily accounting activities, documentation and transaction entry.

Recording transactions Maintaining records Supporting reconciliations Preparing documents Assisting senior staff
02

Junior Accountant

May assist with bookkeeping and accounting operations.

TallyPrime knowledge can be relevant when the employer uses the software.
03

Billing Executive

May prepare invoices and maintain billing-related records.

Communication and computer skills can also be important.
04

Accounts Executive

Accounts executive positions can involve broader responsibilities than entry-level roles.

Employers may expect stronger accounting knowledge and practical experience.
05

Payroll Assistant

May work with employee salary information, attendance records and payroll processing.

TallyPrime includes payroll-related functionality.
06

GST Executive / GST Assistant

May support GST-related accounting and documentation.

A Tally course covering GST can provide relevant practical exposure.
07

Finance Assistant

May support accounting and financial administration activities.

08

Tally Operator

Some organizations may hire employees specifically for accounting software data entry and transaction processing.

03

Skills Employers May Look For

Tally alone may not be enough. Consider developing a broader skill set.

Accounting

Understand the principles behind transactions.

Excel

Useful for spreadsheets, analysis and reporting.

GST

Understand the basics of GST and business transactions.

Communication

Accounts professionals communicate with management, customers, vendors and colleagues.

Accuracy

Financial data requires careful attention to detail.
