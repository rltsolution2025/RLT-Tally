TallyPrime Payroll: How to Manage Employee Salaries

Learn how to configure payroll, create employee records, set salary components, record attendance, calculate net salary, generate payslips, and review payroll reports in TallyPrime.

By RLT Edzaro•October 12, 2026•12–15 min read

Quick answer: How do you manage salaries in TallyPrime?

Enable payroll in company features, create employee groups and employee records, configure pay heads and salary structures, record attendance, process payroll, verify deductions, record salary payments, and generate payslips and payroll reports. Menu names and features can vary by TallyPrime version and setup.

Managing employee salaries is an important responsibility for every business. Companies need to calculate salaries accurately, track attendance, manage deductions, maintain payroll records, and prepare payslips every month. Performing these tasks manually can take time and increase the chances of calculation errors.

TallyPrime Payroll helps businesses organise employee salary details, record attendance, calculate earnings and deductions, and generate payroll reports from one system.

In this guide, you will learn how to manage employee salaries in TallyPrime, configure payroll settings, create employee records, calculate salaries, and generate payslips with practical examples.

What Is Payroll in TallyPrime?

Payroll is the process of calculating and managing employee compensation. It includes basic salary, allowances, overtime, incentives, deductions, and net salary payable.

TallyPrime provides payroll features that help businesses maintain employee information, configure salary structures, record attendance, process salary payments, and generate payroll-related reports.

Businesses can use payroll management to:

  • Maintain employee records and salary details.
  • Create salary structures for different employees.
  • Record attendance and employee leave.
  • Calculate earnings and deductions.
  • Process monthly salary payments.
  • Generate payslips and payroll reports.
  • Maintain payroll accounting records.

Example: If a company has 25 employees, the accounts team can use TallyPrime to organise their salary details and process payroll systematically instead of calculating every employee's salary separately.

Why Is Payroll Management Important?

An effective payroll process helps businesses pay employees accurately and maintain proper financial records.

1. Accurate salary calculations

Salary components can be configured in advance to reduce repetitive calculations and manual errors.

2. Better attendance management

Employee attendance and applicable leave or unpaid absence can be recorded to support salary calculations.

3. Organised salary records

Employee-wise payroll information makes it easier to review salary details and maintain accounting records.

4. Simplified payslip preparation

Payslips can be generated to show salary earnings, deductions, and net pay.

5. Improved payroll reporting

Payroll reports help businesses review employee costs and salary-related information.

6. Support for statutory payroll accounting

Where applicable, businesses can configure and account for statutory deductions such as provident fund (PF), employee state insurance (ESI), and professional tax according to the relevant rules and their payroll setup.

Note: Payroll features, statutory configurations, and report availability can vary depending on the TallyPrime version and configuration. Verify applicable statutory rates, thresholds, and filing requirements before processing payroll.

How to Enable Payroll in TallyPrime

Before managing employee salaries, you need to enable payroll in your company.

  1. Open TallyPrime and select the company for which you want to manage payroll.
  2. Open the company configuration or F11 (Features) screen.
  3. Locate the payroll-related feature.
  4. Enable Maintain Payroll, or the corresponding payroll option available in your version.
  5. Save the configuration.

Once payroll is enabled, you can configure employee information, salary components, attendance, and payroll transactions.

Tip: Menu names and configuration options may differ slightly between TallyPrime versions. Refer to the options displayed in your installed version.

Step-by-Step Guide to Managing Employee Salaries in TallyPrime

Step 1: Create Employee Groups

Employee groups help businesses organise employees by department, role, or other relevant categories. For example, a company may create Administration, Accounts, Sales, Human Resources, and Production groups.

  1. Open the employee group creation screen under the payroll-related masters.
  2. Enter the employee group name.
  3. Select the appropriate parent group, if required.
  4. Save the details.

Grouping employees makes it easier to organise employee records and review payroll information.

Step 2: Create Employee Records

After creating employee groups, add individual employee records. A typical employee record may include the employee name, employee ID or code, date of joining, department or employee group, designation, contact details, and applicable payroll and salary information.

  1. Open the employee creation screen under payroll masters.
  2. Enter the employee's name and other required details.
  3. Assign the employee to the appropriate group.
  4. Enter the relevant employment information.
  5. Save the employee record.

Practical example: An employee named Arun works in the Accounts department and receives a monthly salary. Creating an employee record allows the company to maintain Arun's details and associate the appropriate salary structure with him.

Step 3: Create Salary Components or Pay Heads

A salary structure consists of different earnings and deductions. In TallyPrime, these components can be configured using pay heads.

Common salary components in a payroll setup
Salary component Purpose
Basic Salary Main component of employee earnings
House Rent Allowance (HRA) Applicable housing allowance
Conveyance Allowance Applicable travel or conveyance allowance
Other Allowances Additional earnings defined by the employer
Overtime Eligible overtime earnings
Provident Fund (PF) Applicable retirement-benefit contribution
Employee State Insurance (ESI) Applicable employee insurance contribution
Professional Tax Applicable state-level tax deduction
Income Tax / TDS Applicable tax deduction, where relevant

To configure a pay head:

  1. Open the pay head creation screen.
  2. Enter the component name.
  3. Select the appropriate pay head type, such as earnings or deductions.
  4. Configure the calculation method and accounting allocation as applicable.
  5. Save the pay head.

The configuration should reflect the company's actual salary policy and applicable statutory requirements. Not every employee will have the same salary components, so configure each component according to the employee's salary structure and applicable rules.

Step 4: Define the Employee Salary Structure

Once employee records and pay heads are available, configure each employee's salary details. Depending on the configuration, these can include basic salary, allowances, other earnings, applicable deductions, salary calculation frequency, and the effective date of the salary structure.

Illustrative monthly salary structure
Component Amount
Basic Salary ₹15,000
HRA ₹5,000
Other Allowances ₹2,000
Gross Salary ₹22,000

The gross salary in this example is ₹22,000 per month, before deductions. Assign the relevant salary components and values through the salary details or salary structure configuration available in your version. If an employee receives a salary revision, update the applicable details with the correct effective date.

Step 5: Record Employee Attendance

Attendance can affect salary calculations, especially when employees receive payment based on attendance, payable days, or eligible overtime. For example, an employee may have 26 total working days, 24 days worked, and 2 days of unpaid leave.

  1. Configure the appropriate attendance or production-related types, if required.
  2. Open the relevant attendance voucher screen.
  3. Select the attendance date or period.
  4. Enter employee attendance details.
  5. Record the applicable attendance units, such as days or hours.
  6. Save the voucher.

The available attendance options and calculation methods depend on payroll configuration. Confirm how paid leave, unpaid leave, holidays, overtime, and partial attendance are treated under company policy.

Step 6: Process Monthly Payroll

After setting up employee details, salary components, and attendance, process payroll for the relevant period.

  1. Select the correct company and payroll period.
  2. Review employee salary structures.
  3. Check attendance and payable days.
  4. Verify eligible earnings and deductions.
  5. Open the payroll voucher or payroll processing screen.
  6. Select the applicable employees and salary components.
  7. Review the calculated amounts.
  8. Save the payroll transaction.

The exact steps depend on the version and payroll configuration. Before saving, verify the employee list, salary period, attendance details, and deductions.

Step 7: Calculate Net Salary

Net salary is the amount payable to an employee after deducting applicable amounts from gross salary.

Net Salary = Gross Earnings − Total Deductions

These figures are for learning purposes only. Actual PF, professional tax, and other deductions depend on the employee's circumstances, applicable law, and the company's payroll configuration. Review calculated salary and deductions before finalising payroll.

Step 8: Record Salary Payments

After calculating payroll, record payments made to employees. Salary payments may be made through bank transfer, cheque, or other permitted payment methods.

  1. Review the final payroll amount.
  2. Open the appropriate payment voucher.
  3. Select the relevant bank or cash ledger.
  4. Select the appropriate salary or payroll ledger, according to the accounting setup.
  5. Enter the payment amount and date.
  6. Provide the required reference or narration.
  7. Save the voucher.

The accounting entries depend on whether payroll has already been accrued or posted through payroll vouchers. For example, when salaries are first accrued, the entry may be:

Illustrative salary accrual entry

Salary Expense A/c Dr. ₹22,000

To Salary Payable A/c ₹22,000

When net salary of ₹20,000 is paid and ₹2,000 is withheld for applicable deductions, the payment and liability entries must reflect the actual accounting treatment. Record withheld amounts in the appropriate deduction or statutory liability accounts, not as additional salary paid to the employee.

Step 9: Generate Employee Payslips

A payslip provides an employee with a summary of salary earnings and deductions for a particular period. It typically includes employee name and identification details, salary period, basic salary, allowances, gross earnings, deductions, and net salary payable.

  1. Open the payroll reports or payslip section.
  2. Select the relevant employee and salary period.
  3. Review the displayed salary details.
  4. Choose the available printing or export option.
  5. Generate the payslip for distribution.

Report names and output options may differ by version and configuration. Check payslips for accuracy before sharing them with employees.

Step 10: Review Payroll Reports

Depending on the configuration, useful reports may include employee-wise salary details, payroll statements, attendance reports, pay head summaries, payroll register, payslips, and salary-related accounting reports.

Regularly reviewing reports can help identify missing attendance entries, incorrect salary components, unusual deductions, and discrepancies between payroll and payment records.

Common Payroll Mistakes to Avoid in TallyPrime

Incorrect configuration or incomplete data can affect salary calculations. Avoid these common mistakes:

  1. Incorrect employee details: Check employee names, joining dates, groups, and salary assignments.
  2. Incorrect pay head configuration: Verify earnings and deductions use appropriate types and calculation methods.
  3. Missing attendance entries: Ensure attendance and leave details are complete.
  4. Incorrect deduction settings: Check applicability and calculation of PF, ESI, professional tax, and other deductions.
  5. Duplicate salary payments: Review payment vouchers and bank records.
  6. Skipping payroll reports: Reconcile salary expenses, net payments, and outstanding deduction liabilities.
  7. Ignoring salary revisions: Reflect approved revisions from the correct effective date.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is payroll in TallyPrime?

Payroll in TallyPrime is a set of features used to maintain employee records, configure salary components, record attendance, process salaries, and generate payroll-related reports.

2. How do I enable payroll in TallyPrime?

Open the company configuration or F11 Features screen, locate the payroll option, and enable Maintain Payroll or the corresponding setting available in your version.

3. Can TallyPrime calculate employee salaries automatically?

TallyPrime can calculate salaries using configured salary structures, pay heads, attendance information, and applicable calculation methods. Accurate results depend on correct configuration and employee data.

4. Can I manage employee attendance in TallyPrime?

Yes. TallyPrime supports attendance-related workflows that can be used with payroll when the relevant features and attendance types are configured.

5. Can I generate payslips in TallyPrime?

Yes. Payroll reporting options can be used to generate employee payslips for a selected period, subject to the version and payroll configuration.
