Introduction

Tracking outstanding payments is an important part of accounting and financial management. Businesses need to know how much money customers still owe them and how much they need to pay suppliers. Without proper tracking, businesses may face delayed collections, cash flow problems, and difficulties managing daily expenses.

TallyPrime helps businesses monitor outstanding receivables and payables through its outstanding reports. These reports allow users to review pending invoices, check due dates, identify overdue balances, and understand the status of customer and supplier payments.

In this guide, you will learn how to check customer outstanding payments, view supplier dues, understand ageing reports, and use practical examples to manage pending payments in TallyPrime.

KEY CONCEPTS

What Are Outstanding Payments in TallyPrime?

Outstanding payments are amounts that have not yet been settled by the relevant party. In accounting, outstanding balances generally fall into two categories.

R

1. Outstanding Receivables

These are amounts that customers owe your business for goods or services supplied on credit.

Example ₹25,000 Customer invoice not yet paid
P

2. Outstanding Payables

These are amounts your business owes suppliers or vendors for goods or services purchased on credit.

Example ₹15,000 Supplier invoice not yet paid

TallyPrime helps users track both categories through its receivables and payables reports.

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

How to Find Outstanding Payments in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to check outstanding payments using TallyPrime. Menu names and navigation can vary slightly depending on your software release.

01

Open Your Company in TallyPrime

  1. Launch TallyPrime.
  2. Select the company whose outstanding payments you want to check.
  3. Verify that the correct company and financial period are selected.
Check before proceeding: Make sure the relevant sales, purchase, receipt, and payment transactions have been recorded accurately.
02

Open the Outstanding Reports

Navigate to the Outstandings reports in TallyPrime. Depending on your release, you can access the relevant report through the main reports or display menus. You can also use TallyPrime's search or Go To functionality to locate an outstanding report.

R
ReceivablesAmounts customers owe your business
P
PayablesAmounts your business owes suppliers
03

View Outstanding Receivables

Select the receivables report to view customer balances. The report can help you identify:

  • Customer names
  • Outstanding balances
  • Pending bill references
  • Due dates, where maintained
  • Amounts pending against individual invoices

For example, if a customer has three unpaid invoices, the report can help you review the pending balances and investigate which invoices remain unsettled.

Select a customer or report line to drill down into the underlying details, where supported.

04

View Outstanding Payables

Open the payables report to review supplier balances. This report helps you identify the amounts your business still owes vendors.

  • Review pending supplier invoices.
  • Check the balances due to individual suppliers.
  • Identify invoices approaching their due dates.
  • Plan upcoming payments.
  • Investigate differences between supplier statements and accounting records.

Regularly reviewing payables helps businesses avoid missed due dates and maintain better supplier relationships.

05

Check Individual Outstanding Bills

To understand exactly why a balance is pending, open the relevant customer or supplier details. Review the bill-wise information, including:

  • Original invoice amount
  • Amount received or paid
  • Remaining balance
  • Invoice reference
  • Due date, if configured
  • Relevant receipt or payment entries
PARTIAL PAYMENT EXAMPLE
Original invoice₹20,000
Amount received₹12,000
Remaining outstanding₹8,000

This example assumes the receipt has been correctly allocated and there are no other adjustments. A detailed review helps you identify whether an invoice is unpaid, partially settled, or incorrectly allocated.

AGEING ANALYSIS

How to Check Overdue Payments in TallyPrime

An outstanding invoice is not necessarily overdue. It becomes overdue when its due date has passed and the balance remains unpaid.

TallyPrime provides outstanding and ageing-report capabilities that can help businesses analyse pending amounts by their age or due status, depending on the configuration.

1Open the relevant receivables or payables report.
2Locate the available ageing or bill-wise analysis options.
3Set the required ageing basis or period, where available.
4Review the outstanding amounts against the relevant ageing categories.
5Identify invoices requiring collection or payment follow-up.

Example of an Outstanding Ageing Report

Suppose a business has the following pending customer invoices:

ACCURATE REPORTING

How to Record Bill-Wise Details for Accurate Outstanding Reports

To obtain useful outstanding reports, businesses should maintain accurate bill-wise references when recording transactions.

01

Enable the Relevant Bill-Wise Feature

Review the accounting features and ledger settings to confirm that bill-wise tracking is enabled where required. The exact configuration depends on your TallyPrime version and accounting setup.

02

Configure Customer and Supplier Ledgers

Open the relevant customer or supplier ledger and verify the applicable bill-wise tracking settings. Use the correct ledger group and maintain the necessary details for credit transactions.

03

Enter Bill References in Transactions

When recording credit sales or purchases, enter the appropriate bill reference or allocation details when prompted. Use a consistent reference system so that each invoice can be tracked accurately.

04

Allocate Receipts and Payments Correctly

When recording a customer receipt or supplier payment, allocate the amount against the appropriate outstanding bill. For example, allocate a ₹5,000 customer receipt to the correct invoice.

05

Verify the Outstanding Report

After saving the transactions, reopen the outstanding report and confirm that the balances reflect the recorded receipts, payments, and adjustments.

Remember: Correct bill references and allocations help make outstanding reports more reliable.

PRACTICAL ACCOUNTING

Practical Example: Finding Outstanding Customer Payments

Let us consider a simple business scenario. ABC Enterprises sells goods to a customer on credit.

Original sales invoice₹30,000
Payment received₹18,000
Remaining outstanding₹12,000
OUTSTANDING AMOUNT FORMULA Original invoice amount − Payments received

₹30,000 − ₹18,000 = ₹12,000

The accountant can open the receivables report in TallyPrime, locate the customer, and inspect the invoice-level details to verify the ₹12,000 balance.

If the report does not show the expected amount, the accountant should check whether the receipt was recorded correctly and allocated against the correct bill.

FOLLOW-UP PROCESS

How to Reduce Outstanding Payments Using TallyPrime

TallyPrime helps businesses identify pending amounts, but collecting payments also requires a consistent follow-up process.

01

Review Outstanding Reports Regularly

Check receivables and payables daily or weekly, depending on the volume of transactions.

02

Prioritise Overdue Invoices

Focus first on invoices that are significantly overdue or have high outstanding balances.

03

Send Payment Reminders

Use the customer contact details maintained by your business to follow up on pending invoices. Keep reminders professional and include the invoice reference and outstanding amount.

04

Reconcile Receipts and Payments

Confirm that all receipts and payments have been entered and allocated correctly.

05

Review Customer Credit Terms

Use outstanding information to assess whether customers are paying according to agreed credit terms.

06

Maintain Accurate Invoice Records

Correct invoice dates, references, and due-date information make outstanding reports easier to interpret.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Problems When Checking Outstanding Payments in TallyPrime

01 Outstanding Report Shows an Incorrect Balance

Possible reasons
  • A receipt or payment has not been recorded.
  • A receipt or payment has been allocated to the wrong bill.
  • A credit note, debit note, or other adjustment is missing.
  • Bill-wise tracking is not configured as expected.
Solution

Review the original voucher, bill allocation, and related adjustment entries.

02 Customer Invoice Is Not Appearing in the Outstanding Report

Possible reasons
  • The invoice was recorded under an incorrect ledger.
  • The invoice has been fully settled.
  • Bill references were not entered as expected.
  • The selected company, period, or report filters exclude the transaction.
Solution

Verify the sales voucher, customer ledger, bill-wise details, and report settings.

03 Due Dates Are Missing

Possible reasons
  • Payment terms were not configured.
  • The relevant due-date information was not maintained.
  • Bill-wise settings differ from the expected setup.
Solution

Review the ledger, voucher, and bill-wise configuration. Update the information according to the actual invoice and agreed payment terms.

04 Supplier Outstanding Does Not Match the Supplier Statement

Possible reasons
  • A supplier invoice or payment is missing.
  • A payment has been allocated to another bill.
  • A credit note or debit note has not been recorded.
  • The supplier statement covers a different period.
Solution

Compare invoice-level details, payment vouchers, adjustment entries, and the supplier statement.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I check outstanding payments in TallyPrime?

Open the relevant company, navigate to the Outstandings reports, and select Receivables or Payables. Review the customer or supplier balances and drill down into individual bills to investigate pending amounts.

2. What is the difference between outstanding receivables and payables?

Receivables are amounts customers owe your business. Payables are amounts your business owes suppliers or vendors.

3. Can I see customer-wise outstanding payments in TallyPrime?

Yes. The receivables report can help you review customer balances and, where configured, drill down into individual outstanding bills.

4. How can I check overdue bills in TallyPrime?

Use the relevant outstanding and ageing-report options to analyse pending bills. Ensure that bill-wise tracking and due-date details are maintained correctly.

5. Why is an invoice still showing as outstanding after receiving payment?

The receipt may not have been recorded, or it may not have been allocated to the correct bill. Review the receipt voucher and its bill-wise allocation.

6. Can TallyPrime show outstanding supplier payments?

Yes. The payables report helps businesses review supplier balances and pending bills so they can plan payments and monitor due dates.

7. Why is bill-wise tracking important in TallyPrime?

Bill-wise tracking connects outstanding balances to individual invoices. It helps accountants identify partial payments, pending invoices, and due dates more accurately.

8. Is learning outstanding reports important for accounting jobs?

Yes. Understanding receivables, payables, bill-wise allocations, and ageing reports is useful for accounting, accounts executive, billing, and finance support roles.
