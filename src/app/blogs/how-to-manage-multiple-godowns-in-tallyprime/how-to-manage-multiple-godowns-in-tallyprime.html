01 / INTRODUCTION

Introduction

Managing inventory across multiple locations can become challenging as a business grows. A company may store goods in a main warehouse, a branch office, a retail outlet, or a separate storage facility. Without proper inventory tracking, businesses may struggle to identify where stock is available, which location needs replenishment, and how much inventory is held at each warehouse.

TallyPrime helps businesses organise inventory by creating multiple godowns, recording stock movements, and reviewing stock balances for different storage locations.

Whether you are an accounting student, inventory executive, storekeeper, or business owner, learning how to manage multiple godowns in TallyPrime can help you understand practical inventory management.

In this guide, you will learn how to enable godown management, create multiple godowns, record stock transfers, check location-wise stock, and avoid common inventory errors.

What you will learn

Set up storage locations, allocate stock to the correct godown, record internal stock movement, and verify each location's inventory balance.

02 / THE BASICS

What Is a Godown in TallyPrime?

A godown in TallyPrime is a location used to track where inventory is stored. It may represent a warehouse, store, branch stockroom, distribution centre, or another physical storage location.

For example, a business may maintain the following godowns:

Main Warehouse Chennai Branch Tambaram Store Finished Goods Warehouse Raw Materials Store

By assigning stock to the appropriate godown, businesses can track inventory quantities at different locations instead of maintaining only one overall stock balance.

Simple example

If a company has 500 units of a product, with 300 units in its main warehouse and 200 units in its Chennai branch, godown management helps the company identify the stock available at each location.

05 / GODOWN MASTERS

How to Create Multiple Godowns in TallyPrime

Let us understand the process using a practical example. Suppose ABC Traders stores products in three locations: Main Warehouse, Chennai Branch, and Tambaram Store.

01
STEP ONE

Open Godown Creation

Navigate to the godown creation option in TallyPrime. In many TallyPrime configurations, godowns can be created through the inventory master creation menu. The precise menu path may vary by version.

02
STEP TWO

Create the Main Warehouse

Enter the godown name as Main Warehouse. Select the appropriate parent godown. For a standalone location, this is generally the primary godown, often named Primary. Save the godown master.

03
STEP THREE

Create the Chennai Branch

Create another godown named Chennai Branch. Select the appropriate parent godown and save the record.

04
STEP FOUR

Create the Tambaram Store

Repeat the process and create a godown named Tambaram Store. You should now have three locations available for inventory allocation.

05
STEP FIVE

Create a Hierarchical Godown Structure if Needed

If a business has multiple warehouses with smaller storage areas, it may use a parent-child structure.

Parent Godown Sub-Godown
Main Warehouse Raw Materials
Main Warehouse Finished Goods
Main Warehouse Packing Materials
Separate Location Chennai Branch
Separate Location Tambaram Store

This structure can help organise inventory when the business needs to track both the main storage location and its sub-locations. Use a hierarchy only when it matches the business's actual storage and reporting requirements.

06 / STOCK ALLOCATION

How to Allocate Stock to Different Godowns

After creating the godowns, the next step is to record inventory against the correct location. For this example, ABC Traders has 500 units of Product A.

Godown Product A Quantity
Main Warehouse 300 units
Chennai Branch 120 units
Tambaram Store 80 units
Total Stock 500 units

Steps to Allocate Stock

  1. Make sure Product A exists as a stock item.
  2. Open the appropriate inventory voucher.
  3. Select the stock item.
  4. Enter the relevant godown or location.
  5. Enter the quantity allocated to that godown.
  6. Repeat for other locations as required.
  7. Verify the total quantity and save the voucher.

Important: Creating a godown does not automatically allocate inventory to it. You must record the stock quantities and movements using the appropriate voucher workflow. The exact voucher and allocation process depends on whether you are recording opening stock, a purchase, a sales transaction, or a stock transfer.

For help setting up the product master first, see How to Create Stock Items in TallyPrime.

07 / STOCK MOVEMENT

How to Transfer Stock Between Godowns in TallyPrime

Stock transfers are common when one warehouse supplies goods to another branch or storage location. TallyPrime supports inventory transfer workflows that can be used to record such movements.

Practical example

ABC Traders transfers 50 units of Product A from its Main Warehouse to the Chennai Branch.

Stock Before the Transfer

Godown Stock Available
Main Warehouse 300 units
Chennai Branch 120 units
Tambaram Store 80 units
Total 500 units

Stock After Transferring 50 Units

Godown Stock Before Transfer Stock After
Main Warehouse 300 -50 250
Chennai Branch 120 +50 170
Tambaram Store 80 0 80
Total 500 0 500

Total stock remains unchanged

The transfer changes the stock location, not the overall quantity. The total remains 500 units.

Steps to Record a Stock Transfer

  1. Open the appropriate inventory voucher in TallyPrime.
  2. Select the supported stock transfer or stock journal workflow.
  3. Select Product A.
  4. Choose Main Warehouse as the source godown.
  5. Enter the quantity as 50 units.
  6. Select Chennai Branch as the destination godown.
  7. Enter the corresponding receipt quantity.
  8. Verify that the transfer quantities match.
  9. Save the voucher.

The available voucher options and fields depend on the TallyPrime configuration. Review the resulting godown balances to ensure the transfer was recorded correctly. Record transfers promptly and retain supporting documents such as stock transfer notes or internal movement records when required by your business process.

For more information about transaction entry, read TallyPrime Voucher Types Explained.

09 / OPENING INVENTORY

How to Manage Opening Stock in Multiple Godowns

When starting to use godown management, a business may need to record its existing inventory. Suppose the physical stock on the starting date is:

Product Main Warehouse Chennai Branch Total
Product A 200 100 300
Product B 150 50 200
Product C 80 20 100

Steps to Record Opening Stock

  1. Create the required godowns.
  2. Create or verify the stock item masters.
  3. Enter the opening quantities against the correct godowns using the supported opening-stock or inventory setup workflow.
  4. Enter the relevant valuation details where required.
  5. Compare the recorded balances with the physical stock count.
  6. Verify the resulting stock reports.

If the company already has inventory records, plan the migration carefully to avoid duplicating opening quantities or changing existing stock values unintentionally.

10 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Problems in Multiple Godown Management

1. Godown Name Does Not Appear

Possible reasons: The godown was not saved, the multiple-godown feature is not enabled, or the voucher or stock item configuration is incomplete.

Solution: Verify the company inventory settings and godown masters.

2. Stock Appears in the Wrong Godown

Possible reasons: The wrong location was selected in the voucher, the stock transfer was recorded incorrectly, or the inventory allocation was not checked before saving.

Solution: Review the relevant voucher and correct the allocation using the appropriate alteration or adjustment workflow.

3. Total Stock Does Not Match

Possible reasons: A stock movement is missing, a transfer quantity is incorrect, opening stock was entered more than once, or a purchase or sales voucher contains incorrect quantities.

Solution: Review the inventory vouchers, Stock Summary, and godown-wise reports. Compare the records with physical stock.

4. Stock Transfer Does Not Update Correctly

Possible reasons: The transfer was not saved, the source and destination godowns were selected incorrectly, or the voucher configuration is unsuitable for the transaction.

Solution: Verify the transfer voucher and confirm that the source quantity decreases while the destination quantity increases.

5. Too Many Godowns Make Reports Difficult to Manage

Possible reasons: Unnecessary duplicate godowns have been created, naming conventions are inconsistent, or the hierarchy does not match the business's actual storage structure.

Solution: Use consistent names and a clear hierarchy. Create separate godowns only when they serve a genuine tracking or reporting purpose.

11 / INVENTORY CONTROL

Best Practices for Managing Multiple Godowns

Follow these practices to improve inventory accuracy:

Use clear and consistent godown names.
Create a godown hierarchy that matches your storage structure.
Record every stock movement promptly.
Verify source and destination quantities during transfers.
Perform regular physical stock counts.
Review godown-wise stock reports periodically.
Restrict inventory alterations to authorised users where appropriate.
Maintain supporting records for stock movements.
Avoid creating duplicate or unnecessary godowns.
