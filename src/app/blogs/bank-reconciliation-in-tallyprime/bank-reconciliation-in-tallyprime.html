01 · INTRODUCTION

Introduction

Bank reconciliation is an important accounting activity that helps businesses verify whether the transactions recorded in their accounting books match the transactions shown in their bank statements. With TallyPrime, accountants can reconcile bank transactions manually or use statement-based reconciliation features to identify matching entries more efficiently.

If you are a beginner learning TallyPrime or an accountant managing daily business transactions, understanding bank reconciliation helps you maintain accurate financial records, identify missing entries, and investigate differences between book balances and bank balances.

In this guide, you will learn how to perform bank reconciliation in TallyPrime, understand the process with a practical example, identify common reconciliation errors, and review the report after completing the process.

What does bank reconciliation do?

It compares book transactions with bank statement transactions, helps identify matched and unmatched entries, and explains timing differences or missing records.

02 · BASICS

What Is Bank Reconciliation?

Bank reconciliation is the process of comparing the bank balance recorded in a company's books of accounts with the balance shown in its bank statement.

The balances may differ because certain transactions have not yet been recorded in the books, payments have not cleared the bank, bank charges have not been entered, or transactions have been recorded incorrectly.

Simple example

Your TallyPrime records may show that you issued a cheque to a supplier, but the cheque may not have been presented to the bank yet. The transaction exists in your books but may not appear in the bank statement for the same period.

Bank reconciliation helps identify and explain these differences.

03 · BENEFITS

Why Is Bank Reconciliation Important?

Regular bank reconciliation helps businesses maintain accurate accounting records and improve financial control.

Verify Bank Balances

Support Audits

Maintain clearer financial records for accountants, auditors, and business owners.

Improve Financial Control

04 · PREPARATION

What Do You Need Before Starting Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime?

Before you begin, keep the following information ready:

  • Your company data in TallyPrime.
  • The correct bank ledger in your company's accounts.
  • The bank statement for the period you want to reconcile.
  • Payment and receipt vouchers recorded in TallyPrime.
  • Bank transaction dates, amounts, and reference numbers, wherever available.

Version note: The exact steps and available options can vary by TallyPrime release. TallyPrime Release 6.0 and later support enhanced statement-based reconciliation, while manual reconciliation is also available.

If you need to review the underlying bank ledger entries first, see How to View Ledger Reports in TallyPrime.

05 · METHODS

How to Perform Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime

There are two common approaches to bank reconciliation:

Statement-Based Reconciliation

Import a supported bank statement and match transactions with entries recorded in TallyPrime.

Manual Reconciliation

Compare transactions directly with the bank statement and enter the relevant bank dates.

Choose the method that suits your TallyPrime version and accounting process.

06 · METHOD 1

Method 1: Bank Reconciliation Using a Bank Statement

TallyPrime Release 6.0 and later provides enhanced bank reconciliation features that can identify exact matches and potential matches between book transactions and imported bank transactions.

01

Open Your Company in TallyPrime

Start TallyPrime and select the company whose bank transactions you want to reconcile. Check that the correct company and financial period are selected.

02

Check Your Bank Ledger

Verify that the relevant bank ledger is available and represents the correct bank account. Examples include HDFC Bank Account, SBI Current Account, and ICICI Bank Account.

03

Import the Bank Statement

Obtain the statement for the required period. In supported versions, import it in a supported format such as Excel or CSV, verify the bank account and statement period, then complete the import.

04

Open Banking Activities

Press Alt+G, type or select Banking Activities, and open the Bank Reconciliation section.

05

Open the Bank Reconciliation Report

Open the Bank Reconciliation Summary and drill down into the relevant unreconciled transactions.

06

Reconcile Exact Matches

Check the transaction amount, date, reference number, and other available details. In the enhanced report, use F8 (Reconcile) where applicable.

07

Review Potential Matches

Verify entries with similar amounts but different dates or references. Check supporting documents before linking transactions.

08

Investigate Unmatched Transactions

Review entries available only in books or only in the bank. Identify pending payments, bank charges, interest credits, direct debits, or missing vouchers.

09

Verify the Reconciliation Summary

Return to the summary and review the expected bank balance, imported bank statement balance, and remaining difference. Investigate unexplained differences.

Transaction categories to review

Exact Matches Potential Matches Available Only in Books Available Only in Bank Partially Reconciled

Do not reconcile transactions merely because their amounts are similar. Confirm their dates, references, and supporting documents before matching them.

For detailed information about available reports and actions, refer to the official Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime – TallyHelp.

07 · METHOD 2

Method 2: How to Perform Manual Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime

Manual reconciliation is useful when you compare transactions directly with a bank statement instead of matching them through an imported statement. Navigation depends on your TallyPrime release and reconciliation configuration.

01
MANUAL PROCESS

Open the Bank Reconciliation Report

Open your company and navigate to the relevant bank ledger's reconciliation report. In supported versions, use Alt+G > Banking Activities. Choose the appropriate bank account and period.

02
COMPARE ENTRIES

Compare Transactions With the Bank Statement

Keep the bank statement open and compare each transaction with the corresponding entry in TallyPrime.

  • Transaction amount.
  • Payment or receipt details.
  • Cheque or instrument number, if applicable.
  • Transaction reference.
  • Date on which the transaction appears in the bank statement.
03
BANK DATE

Enter the Bank Date

For matching transactions, enter the relevant bank date in the reconciliation report where the manual option is available.

Example

A cheque may have been recorded in TallyPrime on 10 September but cleared by the bank on 13 September. The relevant bank date is 13 September.

04
UNRECONCILED ENTRIES

Review Unreconciled Entries

Identify entries without a matching transaction in the bank statement. Check whether the payment is pending, the statement period is incomplete, or the entry requires correction.

05
FINAL REVIEW

Check the Final Report

Review which transactions have been reconciled and which remain outstanding. Keep supporting bank statements and accounting records for future reference.

For more details about payment and receipt entries, read TallyPrime Voucher Types Explained.

08 · PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Practical Example of Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime

Let's understand the process using a simple example. Assume ABC Traders has the following transactions during September.

Transaction Amount
Opening bank balance ₹50,000
Customer payment received ₹20,000
Supplier payment recorded ₹10,000
Bank charges not yet recorded in TallyPrime ₹500

Book balance calculation

ABC TRADERS
Opening bank balance₹50,000
Add: Customer receipt₹20,000
Less: Supplier payment₹10,000
Book balance before bank charges₹60,000

The expected balance based on the company's existing book entries is:

₹50,000 + ₹20,000 − ₹10,000 = ₹60,000

The bank statement reflects bank charges of ₹500, so the statement balance, assuming no other transactions or timing differences, is ₹59,500.

How to Resolve the Difference

  1. Open the bank reconciliation report.
  2. Compare the customer receipt and supplier payment with the bank statement.
  3. Reconcile those transactions if their details match.
  4. Identify the ₹500 bank charge appearing only in the bank statement.
  5. Record the bank charge in TallyPrime using the appropriate voucher and expense ledger.
  6. Reconcile the bank charge with the corresponding bank transaction.
  7. Review the report again.
Accounting note: This example is illustrative. Actual balances depend on all transactions, outstanding payments, deposits in transit, and other adjustments for the selected period.

You can also review your Cash Flow report in TallyPrime to understand how banking transactions relate to business cash movements.

09 · TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Bank Reconciliation Problems in TallyPrime and Their Solutions

1. Bank Balance Does Not Match the Book Balance

Possible reasons: Unrecorded bank charges, missing interest credits, outstanding payments or deposits, or incorrect entries.

Solution: Compare the two records transaction by transaction and identify the reason for each difference before making corrections.

2. A Transaction Appears Only in the Bank Statement

Possible reasons: Bank charges, automatic payments, direct debits, interest credits, or a voucher not yet entered in TallyPrime.

Solution: Verify the transaction with the statement and supporting documents. Record a missing transaction using the appropriate voucher and then reconcile it.

3. A Transaction Appears Only in the Books

Possible reasons: Uncleared cheque, deposit not yet reflected, wrong statement period, or mismatched transaction details.

Solution: Check the transaction date, reference number, statement period, and payment status before deciding whether an adjustment is required.

4. TallyPrime Cannot Find a Matching Transaction

Possible reasons: Missing or incorrect instrument number, amount difference, date difference, or a transaction not recorded in the books.

Solution: Check the available details and use potential-match or find-match options where supported. Do not link transactions without sufficient evidence.

5. A Transaction Has Been Reconciled Incorrectly

Solution: Locate the transaction in the reconciliation report. Use available modify-match or unlink options, depending on the reconciliation method and TallyPrime version, and then reconcile the correct entries.

For additional troubleshooting guidance, refer to TallyHelp: Banking and Reconciliation FAQs.

If you need to review customer or supplier balances after reconciliation, see How to Track Receivables and Payables in TallyPrime.

10 · BEST PRACTICES

Best Practices for Bank Reconciliation

To maintain accurate banking records, follow these practices:

Reconcile regularly: Perform reconciliation monthly or more frequently when transaction volumes are high.
Use the correct statement period: Ensure the statement covers the same period you are reviewing.
Verify before matching: Check amounts, dates, and transaction references.
Record bank charges promptly: Enter verified bank fees, interest, and other missing transactions.
Investigate differences: Do not ignore unexplained discrepancies.
Maintain supporting documents: Keep statements, payment confirmations, and vouchers.
Review outstanding transactions: Follow up on old cheques, pending deposits, and unmatched entries.
Check reconciliation reports: Review reconciled and unreconciled entries before closing the period.
11 · FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions About Bank Reconciliation in TallyPrime

1. What is bank reconciliation in TallyPrime?

Bank reconciliation in TallyPrime is the process of comparing transactions recorded in the company's books with transactions shown in the bank statement to identify matches and investigate differences.

2. Can I perform bank reconciliation manually in TallyPrime?

Yes. TallyPrime supports manual reconciliation, where you compare entries with your bank statement and enter relevant bank dates using the available manual reconciliation options.

3. Can TallyPrime automatically reconcile bank transactions?

Supported TallyPrime versions provide statement-based reconciliation features that can identify exact matches and potential matches. Review the matches and confirm that the transactions correspond correctly.

4. Why is my bank balance different in TallyPrime?

The difference may be caused by unrecorded bank charges, interest, uncleared cheques, deposits in transit, missing transactions, incorrect entries, or differences in the reporting period.

5. What is the difference between bank reconciliation and a bank statement?

A bank statement is a record of transactions maintained by the bank. Bank reconciliation is the process of comparing that statement with the company's accounting records.

6. How often should bank reconciliation be performed?

Many businesses reconcile their bank accounts monthly. Businesses with high transaction volumes may reconcile weekly or daily to identify discrepancies sooner.

7. Do I need to import a bank statement to perform reconciliation?

Not always. You can use manual reconciliation where supported. Importing a bank statement enables statement-based matching features in compatible TallyPrime versions.

8. Can I reconcile multiple transactions against one bank transaction?

In supported statement-based workflows, TallyPrime can allow multiple book transactions to be matched against one bank transaction, or one book transaction against multiple bank transactions. Verify transaction details and amounts before reconciling.

9. Which TallyPrime version supports enhanced bank reconciliation?

The enhanced statement-based reconciliation workflow is available in TallyPrime Release 6.0 and later. Options may vary by release, bank support, and configuration. Refer to official TallyHelp documentation for version-specific instructions.
