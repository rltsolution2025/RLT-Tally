How to Use Filters in TallyPrime Reports: Step-by-Step Guide

Learn how to narrow down accounting reports, find relevant transactions, review specific ledgers, and improve reporting accuracy with practical TallyPrime workflows.

01 · Getting started

Introduction

Managing accounting data becomes easier when you can quickly find the information you need. Businesses using TallyPrime often handle hundreds or thousands of transactions, including sales, purchases, receipts, payments, expenses, and journal entries. Reviewing every transaction manually can take time and increase the chances of missing important details.

This is where filters in TallyPrime reports become useful.

Filters help users narrow down report information based on available criteria, such as dates, ledgers, voucher types, amounts, and other report-specific details. Instead of reviewing an entire report, you can focus on the transactions relevant to your accounting task.

In this guide, you will learn how to use filters in TallyPrime reports, explore practical examples, understand common filtering options, and discover how filtering can improve everyday accounting work.

02 · Understand the feature

What Are Filters in TallyPrime Reports?

Filters in TallyPrime reports are options that help users display a selected portion of accounting information based on specific conditions.

For example, a business may want to view only sales transactions recorded during a particular month. Instead of checking every entry, the user can open the relevant sales report, select the required period, and use the available filtering options to narrow down the information.

Depending on the report and TallyPrime version, filtering options may include:

  • Date or reporting period
  • Ledger or account name
  • Voucher type
  • Transaction amount
  • Particular transaction details
  • Other report-specific conditions

The available options differ between reports. Some reports provide built-in filters, while others offer period selection, search, configuration, or related drill-down features.

Remember: Search, filtering, report configuration, and drill-down are different features. Do not assume every TallyPrime report supports every type of filter.

04 · Filtering options

Types of Filtering Options in TallyPrime Reports

The filtering options available depend on the report you open. These are common ways to narrow down accounting information.

01

Date and Period Selection

Date selection allows you to view transactions for a specific day, month, quarter, or financial year.

Example

Review sales recorded between 1 October 2026 and 14 October 2026 by setting the report to the required dates.

This is useful for monthly accounting reviews, financial reporting, and transaction verification.

02

Ledger-Based Filtering

Ledger-based filtering helps you focus on transactions associated with a particular account, where the report supports this option.

Example

Open the relevant ledger report to review transactions associated with a specific customer, subject to the selected period and report configuration.

03

Voucher Type Selection

Voucher types classify accounting transactions, such as sales, purchases, receipts, payments, and journals.

Example

Use the appropriate purchase report or voucher-type selection, where supported, to review purchase entries without checking unrelated transactions.

04

Amount-Based Filtering

Some report views or analytical options allow transactions to be narrowed down based on values or amounts.

Example

To investigate transactions above ₹50,000, apply a supported amount filter. If unavailable, use an alternative report or export the data for further analysis.

05

Search and Text-Based Filtering

Search options can help locate a transaction using a ledger name, voucher number, or other visible information, depending on the screen.

Example

Use available search or voucher navigation options to locate a sales voucher with a particular reference number.

Important: Search, filtering, report configuration, and drill-down are different features. Available options vary by report and software version.

05 · Step-by-step tutorial

How to Use Filters in TallyPrime Reports

Follow these steps to narrow down report information. The exact controls depend on the report and TallyPrime version.

01

Open TallyPrime

Launch TallyPrime and select the company whose accounting records you want to review. Confirm that you have opened the correct company before analysing the report.

02

Open the Required Report

Navigate to the relevant report based on your task. Examples include:

  • Day Book
  • Sales Register and Purchase Register
  • Ledger Vouchers
  • Cash Book and Bank Book
  • Outstanding Receivables and Outstanding Payables
  • Trial Balance

The available reports depend on your company data and software configuration.

03

Select the Reporting Period

Choose the period you want to review. For example, to check transactions from 1 October 2026 to 14 October 2026, set the report to that date range.

In many TallyPrime report screens, Alt + F2 is used to change the reporting period. The exact behaviour may depend on the current screen and version.

After selecting the period, verify that the report displays the intended dates.

04

Identify the Available Filtering Options

Review the report screen and its available buttons, configuration options, and filtering controls. Depending on the report, you may be able to narrow information by ledger, voucher type, transaction details, or other conditions.

Use only the options supported by the report you are viewing.

05

Apply the Relevant Filter

Select the appropriate condition and enter the required value, where the report supports it.

  • Select a customer to review customer-related transactions.
  • Select the relevant voucher type to review a particular category of entry.
  • Use a supported search option to locate a voucher reference.

Confirm the selection and review the resulting report.

06

Review the Filtered Results

Check whether the displayed transactions match your requirements. Verify transaction dates, voucher references, ledger names, and amounts.

If the report does not show the expected information, check the reporting period and filter conditions.

07

Drill Down into a Transaction

If you need more information about a transaction, open the relevant entry using the available drill-down or voucher navigation options. For example, open a sales transaction to review its accounting details.

Filtering helps narrow the list; drilling down helps you inspect individual transactions.

08

Remove or Change the Filter

After completing your review, adjust the filter or return to the full report as needed. If you change the reporting period or filtering condition, review the results again to ensure the report reflects the intended selection.

06 · Practical use cases

Practical Examples of Using Filters in TallyPrime

These examples show how filtering can support everyday accounting tasks.

01
Sales report

Filter Sales Transactions for a Specific Month

Situation: A business wants to review sales transactions for October 2026.

  1. Open the relevant company.
  2. Navigate to the Sales Register.
  3. Set the reporting period to 1 October 2026 through 31 October 2026.
  4. Review the sales entries displayed.
  5. Open individual vouchers if additional details are required.
Result: The report focuses on the selected month instead of the entire accounting period.
02
Customer ledger

Review Transactions for a Specific Customer

Situation: An accountant wants to verify transactions recorded for ABC Traders.

  1. Open the relevant ledger report or another suitable report.
  2. Select the appropriate customer ledger.
  3. Set the reporting period.
  4. Review the displayed transactions.
  5. Compare entries with available invoices and receipts.
Result: The accountant can review transactions associated with ABC Traders more efficiently.
03
Purchase review

Review Purchase Transactions

Situation: A business wants to check its purchase entries for a selected period.

  1. Open the Purchase Register.
  2. Set the required reporting period.
  3. Review the purchase transactions.
  4. Open relevant vouchers to verify supplier details and amounts.
  5. Compare the records with supplier invoices.
Result: The business can focus on purchase accounting without reviewing unrelated sales transactions.
04
Voucher search

Find a Particular Voucher

Situation: A business needs to investigate a transaction associated with a particular voucher reference.

  1. Open a suitable report or voucher list.
  2. Use the available search or navigation options.
  3. Locate the relevant voucher.
  4. Open the voucher and verify its details.
Result: The transaction can be reviewed without manually browsing every entry.
05
Collections

Review Outstanding Receivables

Situation: A business wants to review unpaid customer balances.

  1. Open the Outstanding Receivables report.
  2. Select the appropriate reporting period or available display options.
  3. Review the outstanding balances.
  4. Open the relevant customer details.
  5. Investigate overdue amounts and compare them with payment records.
Result: The business can focus on collections and follow up on unpaid customer balances.
Important: A reporting-period selection does not necessarily mean every outstanding balance is limited to invoices raised during that period. Outstanding reports may include earlier balances, depending on report settings.

07 · Choose the right tool

Filters vs. Configuration vs. Drill-Down in TallyPrime

These features serve different purposes. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right method instead of looking for a filter that may not exist in a particular report.

Feature Purpose Example
Filter Narrows displayed information based on supported conditions. Display selected transactions.
Period selection Changes the reporting dates. View October transactions.
Configuration Changes how a report displays information. Adjust the columns or details shown.
Search Helps locate information matching a search term. Find a voucher reference.
Drill-down Opens more detailed information. Open a ledger or voucher.

08 · Troubleshooting

Common Problems When Using Filters in TallyPrime

1. The Expected Transactions Are Missing

Possible reason: The reporting period or filtering condition excludes the required entries.

Solution: Verify the selected dates, report, company, and filter conditions.

2. The Filter Option Is Not Available

Possible reason: The report does not support that particular filter, or the option is available through a different screen or configuration.

Solution: Check the report's available controls. Consider opening a more suitable report or using an available search or drill-down option.

3. The Report Displays Too Many Transactions

Possible reason: The reporting period is too broad, or the selected conditions are not specific enough.

Solution: Narrow the date range or use a supported ledger, voucher-type, or other relevant selection.

4. The Results Do Not Match the Expected Amount

Possible reason: The report includes transactions outside the intended scope, or the accounting entries need further investigation.

Solution: Verify the report period and criteria, then open the relevant vouchers to check the accounting details.

5. The Filtered Report Is Difficult to Analyse

Possible reason: The report displays more columns or details than needed.

Solution: Review available report configuration options and select a layout suitable for your task.

10 · Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I use filters in TallyPrime reports?

Open the relevant report, select the required reporting period, and use the filtering or selection options available on that screen. Review the results and open individual transactions when more detail is required.

2. Can I filter sales reports in TallyPrime by date?

Yes. You can generally change the reporting period in the Sales Register or another relevant report to view transactions for a selected date range.

3. Can I filter transactions by ledger in TallyPrime?

You can review transactions associated with a particular ledger through the appropriate ledger report. The exact filtering options depend on the report and TallyPrime version.

4. What is the shortcut to change the reporting period in TallyPrime?

Alt + F2 is commonly used to change the reporting period in TallyPrime reports. The available shortcut may depend on the current screen and software version.

5. Can I filter transactions by voucher type?

Some reports provide voucher-type selection or filtering options. You can also use a report dedicated to the transaction category you want to review, such as the Sales Register or Purchase Register.

6. Do all TallyPrime reports support filters?

No. The available filtering, search, configuration, and selection options vary by report. Some reports provide dedicated filtering controls, while others rely on period selection or drill-down navigation.

7. Can I use filters to find errors in accounting entries?

Filters can help narrow down transactions for investigation, especially when the report supports relevant criteria. However, filtering alone does not confirm that an entry is incorrect. Open the voucher and verify supporting documents before making adjustments.

8. Why are filters useful for accounting students?

Filters help accounting students practise report analysis, transaction verification, and reconciliation. These skills are useful when working with sales, purchases, ledgers, outstanding balances, and other accounting reports.
