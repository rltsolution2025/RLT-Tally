How to Create a Proforma Invoice in TallyPrime

Create a preliminary invoice using an optional Sales voucher, enter customer and item details, and print a clear document before finalising a sale.

Practical Accounting Guide Beginner Friendly Step-by-Step Tutorial

01 · Getting started

Introduction

A proforma invoice is a preliminary document that a business sends to a customer before finalising a sale. It provides an estimated breakdown of products or services, quantities, prices, taxes where applicable, delivery details, and payment terms.

Businesses use proforma invoices to communicate proposed transaction details, obtain customer approval, and confirm the terms of a potential sale before issuing the final sales invoice.

In TallyPrime, you can create a proforma invoice using an optional Sales voucher. This lets you print proposed transaction details without recording the proposed sale as a regular accounting transaction.

QUICK ANSWER

How do you create a proforma invoice in TallyPrime?

Open a Sales voucher, press Ctrl + L to mark it as Optional, enter the customer and proposed item or service details, save with Ctrl + A, then open the saved optional voucher and use Ctrl + P to preview and print it.

Remember: Verify that the voucher is marked Optional before saving. A regular Sales voucher may affect the books of accounts.

02 · Understand the document

What Is a Proforma Invoice in TallyPrime?

A proforma invoice communicates the expected value and terms of a proposed sale. It helps the customer review the transaction before proceeding with the purchase.

In TallyPrime, a proforma invoice is generated by entering the proposed transaction in a Sales voucher, marking the voucher as Optional, and printing it. An optional voucher does not affect the books of accounts like a regular recorded Sales voucher.

What details should a proforma invoice contain?

01

Seller and customer

Seller business name and contact details, customer name, billing address, and relevant contact information.

02

Products or services

Item or service description, quantities, rates, estimated value, and applicable taxes where relevant.

03

Document details

Document date, reference number, and any other information needed to identify the proposed transaction.

04

Commercial terms

Payment terms, expected delivery date, quotation validity, and special terms and conditions.

The information required may vary depending on the nature of the transaction and the business's requirements.

03 · Step-by-step tutorial

How to Create a Proforma Invoice in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to create a proforma invoice using an optional Sales voucher.

01

Open the Sales Voucher

Open TallyPrime and select the required company. Press Alt + G (Go To), choose Create Voucher, and press F8 (Sales). Alternatively, navigate through Gateway of Tally → Vouchers → F8 (Sales). You can now enter the details of the proposed sale.

02

Mark the Sales Voucher as Optional

In the Sales voucher screen, press Ctrl + L (Optional). Confirm that the voucher is marked Optional before entering and saving the proposed transaction.

Important: If the Optional option is unavailable, check whether optional vouchers are enabled in your TallyPrime configuration. A regular Sales voucher may record the transaction in your books.
03

Enter Customer Details

Select the customer's ledger in the Party A/c Name field. Enter or verify the customer or business name, billing address, contact information, and other buyer details required for the document. Create the ledger using the appropriate master-creation option if it does not exist.

04

Enter Product or Service Details

Add the products or services you intend to offer. For item-based transactions, enter the item name, quantity, rate, and any relevant details. For service-based transactions, use the appropriate accounting-ledger or service-entry mode supported by your configuration.

Field Example
Item name Office Chair
Quantity 10
Rate ₹2,000
Estimated total ₹20,000

In this example, the estimated value is 10 chairs multiplied by ₹2,000 per chair. Verify quantities, rates, descriptions, and any applicable tax treatment.

05

Enter Payment and Delivery Terms

Provide details that help the customer understand the proposal. Examples include 50% advance and the balance before delivery, delivery within seven working days after confirmation, quotation validity of 15 days, and any agreed special conditions. Use available fields or narration where appropriate.

06

Save the Optional Voucher

Review the customer, items, quantities, rates, and total. Add narration or other relevant information, then press Ctrl + A to save. Check that the voucher remains marked Optional. The proposed transaction is now recorded as an optional voucher rather than a regular sales transaction.

04 · Print and share

How to Print a Proforma Invoice in TallyPrime

After saving the optional Sales voucher, follow these steps to print the proforma invoice.

01

Open the Saved Optional Sales Voucher

Locate and open the saved voucher that contains the proposed transaction.

02

Open Printing Options

Press Ctrl + P (Current) to open the printing options.

03

Preview the Document

Use the preview option if available. Check the customer details, product descriptions, quantities, rates, totals, and payment or delivery terms.

04

Print or Share the Proforma Invoice

Select the print option to print the document. You may also use the export or sharing options available in your TallyPrime version to send the document electronically. Review the final output before sharing it.

Note: TallyPrime prints an optional Sales voucher as a Proforma Invoice. Always verify the document title and details in the final output.

06 · Know the difference

Difference Between a Proforma Invoice and a Sales Invoice

Although both documents may contain similar product and price details, they serve different purposes.

Feature Proforma Invoice Sales Invoice
Purpose Communicates proposed sale terms Records an actual sale
Typical timing Before the sale is finalised When the sale is recorded
Accounting treatment in TallyPrime Optional voucher does not affect books like a regular voucher Regular Sales voucher records the transaction
Customer use Reviews and approves proposed terms Supports the completed transaction and payment process
GST treatment Not a substitute for a required GST tax invoice Must meet applicable tax-invoice requirements when required
TallyPrime method Mark the Sales voucher as Optional Record a regular Sales voucher
Important: A proforma invoice should not be treated as a substitute for a GST tax invoice where a tax invoice is legally required. Follow the applicable invoicing and tax rules for the actual transaction.

07 · Avoid these errors

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Creating a Proforma Invoice

1. Forgetting to Mark the Voucher as Optional

If you save a regular Sales voucher instead, the transaction may affect your books of accounts. Solution: Verify the Optional status before saving.

2. Entering Incorrect Customer Details

An incorrect customer name, billing address, or buyer information can make the document confusing. Solution: Review the customer details before printing.

3. Using Incorrect Rates or Quantities

Inaccurate figures can lead to disputes or revisions. Solution: Verify item calculations and the estimated total value.

4. Treating the Proforma Invoice as a Final Tax Invoice

A proforma invoice communicates proposed terms. Solution: Complete the actual transaction and issue the appropriate invoice when required.

5. Not Reviewing the Printed Document

Important information may be missing or difficult to read. Solution: Preview the output and check the title, business details, item descriptions, totals, and terms before sharing it.

08 · Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I create a proforma invoice in TallyPrime?

Open a Sales voucher using Alt + G → Create Voucher → F8 (Sales). Press Ctrl + L to mark it as Optional, enter the proposed transaction details, save using Ctrl + A, and print using Ctrl + P (Current).

2. Which shortcut keys are used to create a proforma invoice?

F8 opens the Sales voucher, Ctrl + L marks it Optional, Ctrl + A saves the voucher, and Ctrl + P opens the print options for the current voucher.

3. Does a proforma invoice affect the books of accounts?

An optional Sales voucher does not affect the books of accounts like a regular Sales voucher. Make sure the Optional status is selected correctly before saving.

4. Can I print a proforma invoice directly from TallyPrime?

Yes. Open the saved optional Sales voucher, press Ctrl + P (Current), preview the document if needed, and print it.

5. Can I edit a proforma invoice after saving it?

You can generally reopen the saved optional voucher and make changes, subject to your access rights and configuration. Review the updated document before sharing it again.

6. Is a proforma invoice the same as a quotation?

No. They are related but not identical in every business workflow. In TallyPrime, an optional Sales voucher can be printed as a proforma invoice, while an optional Sales Order can be printed as a quotation. Choose the document that fits the transaction.

7. Can a proforma invoice be used as a GST invoice?

A proforma invoice is not a substitute for a GST tax invoice where one is legally required. Follow the applicable invoicing and tax requirements when the actual transaction takes place.

8. Can I create a proforma invoice for services?

You can use an optional Sales voucher to prepare a preliminary document for a proposed service transaction. Enter the relevant service details and estimated amount using the appropriate voucher mode and configuration.

09 · Key takeaway

Conclusion

Creating a proforma invoice in TallyPrime is straightforward once you understand how optional Sales vouchers work. Open the Sales voucher, mark it as Optional, enter the customer and transaction details, save the voucher, and print the document.

This workflow helps businesses share proposed prices and payment terms with customers without recording the proposed sale as a regular accounting transaction.
