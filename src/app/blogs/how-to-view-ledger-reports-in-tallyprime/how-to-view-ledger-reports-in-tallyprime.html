01 / INTRODUCTION

Introduction

Ledger reports are an essential part of accounting because they help businesses track financial transactions recorded under individual accounts. Whether you want to review customer payments, supplier balances, sales, purchases, expenses, or bank transactions, ledger reports provide a clear view of account activity.

TallyPrime allows users to view ledger reports, examine debit and credit entries, check opening and closing balances, and review transactions for a selected period.

Learning how to view ledger reports in TallyPrime is useful for accounting students, business owners, accountants, and anyone preparing for an accounting job. In this guide, you will learn how to open a ledger report, select a specific ledger, change the reporting period, understand debit and credit entries, and troubleshoot common ledger report issues.

02 / BASICS

What Is a Ledger Report in TallyPrime?

A ledger report is a summary of transactions recorded under a particular ledger account during a specified period.

In accounting, each ledger records transactions related to an account, such as Cash, Bank, Sales, Purchases, Rent Expenses, Sundry Debtors, or Sundry Creditors.

For example, a customer ledger can show invoices raised, payments received, credit notes, and the outstanding balance. A bank ledger can show deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and other recorded bank transactions. TallyPrime helps users review these transactions through its ledger reporting features.

04 / REPORT TYPES

Types of Ledger Reports in TallyPrime

TallyPrime provides different reports for reviewing ledger transactions and account balances.

Ledger Vouchers Report

Displays transactions recorded under a selected ledger, such as rent payments or customer invoices.

Cash Ledger Report

Displays recorded cash receipts and payments and helps review cash movements.

Bank Ledger Report

Shows deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and other transactions in a bank account.

Customer Ledger Report

Records sales invoices, receipts, and applicable adjustments for a customer.

Supplier Ledger Report

Records purchase transactions, payments, and adjustments for a supplier.

Expense Ledger Report

Records expenses such as rent, salaries, electricity, and office expenses.

Reviewing these reports helps users understand account activity, verify balances, and investigate transactions in more detail.

05 / STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to View Ledger Reports in TallyPrime

Follow these steps to open and review a ledger report in TallyPrime.

01

Open TallyPrime

Launch TallyPrime, select the company whose accounting records you want to review, and confirm that the correct company is active. If multiple companies are available, verify the company name before proceeding.

02

Open the Ledger Report

A common navigation method is Gateway of Tally or the relevant navigation screen, then Display More Reports (if available), Account Books, and Ledger or Ledger Vouchers. Select the ledger you want to review.

03

Select the Required Ledger

Choose the account for which you want to view transactions. Examples include Cash, Bank Account, Sales Account, Purchase Account, Rent Expense, Customer Account, or Supplier Account.

04

Set the Reporting Period

Choose the month, quarter, financial year, or custom date range required for your review. Confirm the starting and ending dates before analysing the report.

05

Review Debit and Credit Transactions

Examine the date, particulars, voucher type, voucher number, debit, credit, and balance details shown in the report.

06

Open an Individual Transaction

Select a transaction to review the voucher date, accounts affected, amount, narration, and other available details. Verify supporting documents before making corrections.

07

Check the Closing Balance

Review the balance at the end of the selected period. Consider the ledger's nature and accounting classification when interpreting whether the balance represents an amount receivable, payable, or another account balance.

The menu layout can vary by TallyPrime release. If you do not see the same navigation options, use the report search or Go To feature to locate the Ledger report.

01

Change the Date Range

09 / OUTPUT OPTIONS

How to Export or Print Ledger Reports in TallyPrime

Businesses sometimes need ledger reports for audits, management reviews, or sharing with an accountant. Depending on your TallyPrime version and report screen, you can use the available export and printing options.

EXPORT REPORT

To Export a Ledger Report

  1. Open the required ledger report.
  2. Set the correct reporting period.
  3. Review the report for accuracy.
  4. Select the available export option.
  5. Choose a supported format, such as Excel or PDF, where available.
  6. Confirm the destination and export settings.
  7. Open the exported file and verify the data.
PRINT REPORT

To Print a Ledger Report

  1. Open the required ledger report.
  2. Select the relevant date range.
  3. Review the transactions and balances.
  4. Open the print option.
  5. Check the print preview and available settings.
  6. Print the report or save it to an available supported format.

Menu names and supported formats can vary by release and configuration.

10 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Ledger Report Problems in TallyPrime and Their Solutions

1. Ledger Report Is Showing No Transactions

Possible reasons: The wrong company is selected, the reporting period excludes the transaction dates, the ledger has no recorded transactions, or voucher entries were not saved.

Solution: Verify the active company, date range, ledger name, and relevant vouchers.

2. Closing Balance Is Incorrect

Possible reasons: The opening balance is incorrect, a transaction was entered into the wrong ledger, a receipt or payment was recorded incorrectly, or a voucher is missing or duplicated.

Solution: Review the opening balance, transaction details, voucher allocations, and relevant account entries.

3. A Transaction Is Missing from the Ledger

Possible reasons: The transaction was posted to another ledger, the voucher was not saved, the reporting period excludes it, or the selected company is incorrect.

Solution: Check the original voucher and confirm which ledger was affected.

4. Debit and Credit Amounts Are Confusing

Possible reasons: The user is unfamiliar with the account type, the report uses a different column arrangement, or the entry affects multiple ledgers.

Solution: Open the voucher and examine the complete accounting entry. Debit and credit treatment depends on the account type and nature of the transaction.

5. Ledger Report Does Not Match the Financial Statements

Possible reasons: Transactions were posted to incorrect ledgers, opening balances are inaccurate, entries are missing or duplicated, or different reporting periods were selected.

Solution: Confirm the reporting period and review the affected ledgers and vouchers. Use the appropriate financial reports to investigate the difference.

11 / BEST PRACTICES

Tips for Using Ledger Reports Effectively

Follow these practices to improve the accuracy of your accounting work:

  • Check the reporting period before reviewing transactions.
  • Confirm the correct company and ledger.
  • Review large or unusual transactions carefully.
  • Verify opening and closing balances.
  • Check customer and supplier balances regularly.
  • Use ledger reports during bank reconciliation.
  • Review vouchers before changing recorded entries.
  • Export reports only after confirming the selected period and data.
  • Keep supporting invoices, receipts, and payment documents organised.

For accounting professionals, these habits help make ledger verification faster and more reliable. You can also learn how to generate a Trial Balance in TallyPrime to review account balances together.

12 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I view a ledger report in TallyPrime?

Open the relevant company, navigate to the Ledger or Ledger Vouchers report, select the required ledger, and set the reporting period. You can then review the transactions and balances.

2. What is the shortcut key to change the date range in TallyPrime?

Alt+F2 is commonly used to change the reporting period in TallyPrime. The shortcut may depend on the current screen and release.

3. Can I view a ledger report for a specific month?

Yes. Use the reporting period option to select the starting and ending dates for the required month.

4. How can I check a customer outstanding balance in TallyPrime?

Open the customer's ledger report, select the relevant period, and review invoices, receipts, and other adjustments. You can also use the appropriate outstanding reports to examine unpaid invoices.

5. How do I view a bank ledger in TallyPrime?

Open the Ledger report, select the relevant bank account, and set the reporting period. Review deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and other recorded transactions.

6. Can I export ledger reports from TallyPrime to Excel?

TallyPrime provides report export options, with the available formats depending on the report and software version. Open the ledger report and review the available export formats.

7. What is the difference between a ledger and a ledger report?

A ledger is an account used to record transactions relating to a particular category, person, or account. A ledger report displays the recorded transactions and balances for that account.

8. Why is my ledger closing balance showing incorrectly?

An incorrect closing balance may result from an inaccurate opening balance, missing transactions, incorrect ledger allocations, or duplicate entries. Review the ledger transactions and supporting vouchers to identify the cause.

9. Is learning ledger reports important for accounting jobs?

Yes. Ledger reports are used in everyday accounting tasks such as checking transactions, verifying balances, tracking receivables and payables, and supporting reconciliation. Practising these reports in TallyPrime helps learners develop practical accounting skills.

13 / CONCLUSION

Conclusion

Knowing how to view ledger reports in TallyPrime is an important skill for anyone learning accounting or working with financial records.

By selecting the correct ledger, setting the reporting period, reviewing debit and credit transactions, opening individual vouchers, and verifying closing balances, you can understand account activity and identify discrepancies more effectively.

Regular practice with cash, bank, customer, supplier, sales, and expense ledgers will help you become more confident in TallyPrime and everyday accounting tasks.
