How to Make a Credit Note in TallyPrime

Learn how to enable the Credit Note voucher, record sales returns and adjustments, verify GST details, and print or export a credit note.

Practical Accounting Guide GST-Aware Workflow Step-by-Step Tutorial

01 · Getting started

Introduction

A credit note is an important accounting document used when a business needs to reduce the amount previously charged to a customer. Common reasons include sales returns, damaged goods, incorrect billing, price reductions, and eligible post-sale discounts.

Learning how to make a credit note in TallyPrime is useful for accounting students, billing executives, GST practitioners, and business owners. TallyPrime allows users to record credit notes, adjust customer balances, account for sales returns, and maintain relevant GST records.

This guide explains how to enable the Credit Note voucher, record a credit note with a practical example, verify the accounting effect, and review the related reports.

QUICK ANSWER

How do you make a credit note in TallyPrime?

Open Vouchers, press F10: Other Vouchers, choose Credit Note, enter the customer and adjustment details, verify the original invoice and GST information where applicable, then save the voucher.

Important: A commercial credit adjustment does not automatically qualify for a reduction in output GST. Verify the applicable GST rules and transaction conditions before making a tax adjustment.

02 · Understand the document

What Is a Credit Note in TallyPrime?

A credit note records a credit adjustment in favour of a customer or another relevant party. For example, a business sells goods worth ₹10,000 before GST and later receives eligible goods worth ₹2,000 back because they are damaged. The seller may issue a credit note for the returned value and applicable tax adjustment.

In TallyPrime, the Credit Note voucher helps record this adjustment in the accounting books. The exact accounting and tax treatment depends on the reason for the credit note, the original transaction, the voucher configuration, and applicable rules.

01

Sales Returns

Record eligible goods returned by a customer and adjust the original sale as appropriate.

02

Damaged or Defective Goods

Record a qualifying credit adjustment when goods are returned or a price correction is agreed.

03

Price Reductions

Document an eligible reduction in the original selling price after the transaction.

04

Billing Corrections

Address an excess amount charged or another qualifying adjustment with supporting records.

GST reminder: A commercial credit note and a GST credit note are not automatically the same for tax purposes. Whether output GST can be reduced depends on the applicable GST provisions and conditions.

03 · When to use it

When Should You Create a Credit Note?

A credit note may be appropriate when a qualifying adjustment reduces the amount charged to a customer. Common situations include:

  • Goods returned by a customer.
  • Damaged or defective products.
  • A reduction in the original selling price.
  • Eligible discounts granted after a sale.
  • An excess amount charged on an invoice.
  • Other qualifying accounting adjustments.

Keep the supporting invoice, return details, approval, and reason for the adjustment available. Do not issue a credit note solely to force a balance to match; first identify and document the actual reason for the adjustment.

04 · Voucher setup

How to Enable the Credit Note Feature in TallyPrime

Before entering a credit note, check whether the voucher is available in your company.

01

Open the Company

Launch TallyPrime and select the company in which you want to record the credit note.

02

Open Vouchers

Navigate to the voucher-entry screen from the available menu or Go To navigation.

03

Open Other Vouchers

Press F10 for Other Vouchers, where available in your screen and version.

04

Select Credit Note

Choose Credit Note. If TallyPrime prompts you to activate the voucher, confirm activation.

05

Check Voucher Settings if Needed

If the option remains unavailable, review the accounting features or voucher-type settings for your configuration.

Version note: Menu labels and available controls may vary across TallyPrime releases. Follow the option names and shortcut hints displayed in your installation.

05 · Main workflow

How to Make a Credit Note in TallyPrime: Step-by-Step Process

Consider this example: ABC Traders sold goods worth ₹10,000 before GST to XYZ Enterprises. The customer subsequently returns goods worth ₹2,000 before GST. The example assumes an intra-state taxable sale at an illustrative GST rate of 18%, split equally between CGST and SGST.

01

Open the Credit Note Voucher

Use Alt + G → Create Voucher → F10: Other Vouchers → Credit Note, where available. Alternatively, navigate through the relevant Vouchers menu.

02

Select the Appropriate Voucher Mode

For a stock return, use the appropriate item-based invoice mode. For a price adjustment or service-related adjustment, an accounting-based entry may be appropriate. Use Ctrl + H (Change Mode) if required and supported by your version.

03

Enter the Credit Note Date

Enter the date on which the credit note is issued or recorded, as appropriate. Keep it consistent with supporting documents and applicable accounting and GST requirements.

04

Select the Customer Ledger

In the Party A/c Name field, select the customer from the original sale. For this example, select XYZ Enterprises. Verify the customer and GST details before continuing.

05

Enter the Original Invoice Details

Where required by the voucher, enter the original invoice number and date, customer particulars, and other required information. Linking the adjustment to the source invoice supports audit trail and reconciliation.

06

Enter the Returned Goods or Adjustment Amount

For an item-based credit note, select the relevant stock item and enter the returned quantity and rate. In this example, the taxable value of the returned goods is ₹2,000 before GST.

07

Verify the GST Calculation

Check the classification, rate, tax components, and total against the actual transaction. The illustrative calculation is shown in the next section; do not assume the same rate applies to every supply.

08

Enter the Reason and GST Details

Where requested, select or enter the reason for the credit note, such as Sales Return, along with the correct GST classification and other transaction details.

09

Save the Credit Note

Review the customer, credit note number and date, original invoice information, quantities or adjustment amount, GST, total value, and reason. Ctrl + A is commonly used to accept and save screens in TallyPrime.

10

Print or Export the Credit Note

Reopen the recorded voucher and use the available print, export, or sharing controls if you need to provide a copy to the customer. Review the final output before sending it.

11

Verify Accounting and GST Reports

Review the credit note register, customer ledger, sales records, and relevant GST reports. Confirm that the adjustment is reflected correctly and has not been recorded twice.

06 · Worked calculation

Practical GST Calculation for a Credit Note

For the illustrative intra-state transaction, assume the returned goods have a taxable value of ₹2,000 and a GST rate of 18%, split equally between CGST and SGST.

Particulars Calculation Amount
Value of returned goods Taxable value ₹2,000
CGST 9% of ₹2,000 ₹180
SGST 9% of ₹2,000 ₹180
Total credit note value ₹2,000 + ₹180 + ₹180 ₹2,360
Original sale comparison

The original sale was ₹10,000 plus illustrative GST of ₹1,800, giving a total of ₹11,800. If the full ₹2,360 adjustment is valid, the remaining invoice value would be ₹9,440.

Important: These figures are illustrative only and assume that the GST adjustment is permitted. Verify the applicable rate, tax treatment, and eligibility to reduce output tax under current GST rules.

09 · Avoid errors

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Creating Credit Notes

1. Selecting the Wrong Customer

Verify the customer ledger before saving. Selecting the wrong party can create reconciliation problems.

2. Entering Incorrect Original Invoice Details

Where invoice particulars are required, check that the original invoice number and date match the source document.

3. Applying the Wrong GST Rate

Use the correct GST classification and rate for the original supply and the adjustment, and review the tax result before saving.

4. Recording the Same Adjustment Twice

Avoid creating both a manual journal entry and a credit note voucher for the same adjustment unless a separate entry is genuinely required.

5. Ignoring the Reason for the Credit Note

Keep supporting information about whether the adjustment is for returned goods, a price reduction, or another reason.

6. Assuming Every Credit Note Reduces GST Liability

A commercial credit adjustment does not automatically qualify to reduce output GST. Verify the applicable legal requirements before making a tax adjustment.

12 · Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I make a credit note in TallyPrime?

Open Vouchers, select F10: Other Vouchers, choose Credit Note, enter the customer and transaction details, provide relevant original invoice information where required, verify the adjustment and GST details, and save the voucher.

2. What is the shortcut key for a credit note in TallyPrime?

In commonly used voucher-entry workflows, Alt + F6 opens the Credit Note voucher. You can also access it through F10: Other Vouchers or Go To, depending on your version and screen.

3. Why is the Credit Note option not visible in TallyPrime?

The voucher may not yet be activated. Open Vouchers, press F10: Other Vouchers, and select Credit Note. If prompted, activate it. If it remains unavailable, review voucher-type settings.

4. Can I create a GST credit note for a sales return?

Yes, TallyPrime supports credit note entries for sales returns. Enter the relevant item, quantity, original invoice details, and GST information, then verify the tax treatment before saving.

5. Can I issue a credit note without returning goods?

A credit note may be used for qualifying adjustments other than physical returns, such as eligible price reductions or discounts. Accounting and GST implications depend on the reason and applicable rules.

6. How do I check credit notes in TallyPrime?

Review the relevant voucher register, customer ledger, and GST reports. Select the appropriate reporting period and confirm that the transaction is classified correctly.

7. Does a credit note reduce the customer's outstanding balance?

A credit note generally reduces the amount owed by the customer when it relates to a qualifying sales adjustment. The exact effect depends on the transaction and how it is recorded and allocated.

8. Is a credit note mandatory for every sales return?

The documentation required depends on the transaction, the applicable accounting process, and GST requirements. Follow the invoicing and documentation rules relevant to the particular return or adjustment.

13 · Key takeaway

Conclusion

Learning how to make a credit note in TallyPrime helps accounting students and business professionals record sales returns, price adjustments, and other qualifying credit transactions accurately.

The key steps are to activate the Credit Note voucher if necessary, select the appropriate voucher mode, enter customer and original invoice details, record the adjustment, verify GST, save the voucher, and review relevant reports.

Practise the workflow in a test company and verify GST treatment against the applicable requirements before using a credit note for a live transaction.
