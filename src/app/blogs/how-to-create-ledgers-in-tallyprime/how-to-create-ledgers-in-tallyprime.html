How to Create Ledgers in TallyPrime With Real Examples

Learn ledger creation step by step with practical examples for customers, suppliers, sales, purchases, expenses, bank accounts, capital and GST. Understand ledger groups and choose the right account for everyday business transactions.

Quick Answer

How do you create a ledger in TallyPrime?

Open the relevant company in TallyPrime, press Alt+G, search for Create Master, and select Ledger. Enter a meaningful ledger name, select the correct group, fill in any required details, review the configuration and press Ctrl+A to save in the standard creation screen.

Choose the ledger group based on the account's actual accounting purpose.
01 / GETTING STARTED

How to Create Ledgers in TallyPrime

Creating ledgers is one of the most important steps when learning accounting in TallyPrime. Before recording sales, purchases, payments, receipts or expenses, you need to know which ledger accounts are required and how to create them correctly.

A business may need separate ledgers for customers, suppliers, sales, purchases, rent, salaries, bank accounts and capital. Each ledger helps record transactions under the appropriate account and makes it easier to review financial reports.

This guide is designed for beginners, accounting students, business owners and aspiring accountants. It explains the ledger creation process, ledger groups, common mistakes and ten practical examples.

Before creating a ledger, decide what the account represents and which group correctly describes its purpose.
02 / UNDERSTAND THE BASICS

What Is a Ledger in TallyPrime?

A ledger in TallyPrime is an account used to record and track financial transactions related to a particular item, person, income, expense, asset or liability.

Cash and bank

Track cash balances, deposits, receipts and payments.

Customers and suppliers

Monitor receivables, payables and party balances.

Income and expenses

Classify sales, purchases, rent, salary and other costs.

Assets and liabilities

Record capital, loans, fixed assets and other balances.

When you record a transaction using these ledgers, TallyPrime updates the relevant accounting records and helps generate reports such as the Trial Balance, Profit and Loss Account and Balance Sheet.

Simple example

If your business pays ₹10,000 as office rent, the transaction generally uses the Office Rent ledger and the relevant Cash or Bank ledger.

03 / LEDGER GROUPS

What Is a Ledger Group in TallyPrime?

A ledger group is a category used to classify ledger accounts according to their accounting purpose. TallyPrime provides predefined groups such as Sundry Debtors, Sundry Creditors, Sales Accounts, Purchase Accounts, Direct Expenses, Indirect Expenses, Capital Account and Bank Accounts.

Selecting the correct group helps TallyPrime classify transactions properly in accounting reports.

Ledger Ledger Group Purpose
Office Rent Indirect Expenses Records office rent costs.
ABC Traders Sundry Debtors Tracks a customer balance when appropriate.
XYZ Suppliers Sundry Creditors Tracks amounts payable to a supplier.
Sales Account Sales Accounts Classifies sales transactions.

Detailed Ledger Creation Steps

01

Choose the appropriate group based on the nature of the account and your business's accounting requirements.

Ledger Name Common Under Group

05
Step 05

Enter the Required Details

Depending on the ledger group and transaction type, TallyPrime may ask for additional information.

  • Mailing name and address
  • Contact information
  • GST registration details
  • Opening balance
  • Credit period or bill-wise details

Not every ledger requires all these details. Enter only the information relevant to that account.

Your ledger is ready to use in the relevant accounting vouchers.

09 / AVOID COMMON ERRORS

Common Ledger Creation Mistakes to Avoid

1. Selecting the wrong group

For example, creating Office Rent under Sales Accounts instead of Indirect Expenses can lead to incorrect account classification.

2. Creating duplicate ledgers

Check whether a customer, supplier or bank ledger already exists before creating another one.

3. Entering incorrect opening balances

Use verified accounting records. Incorrect balances can affect the Trial Balance and other reports.

4. Ignoring GST configuration

For GST-related ledgers and parties, review the applicable tax settings and registration details.

5. Using unclear ledger names

Names such as Expense 1 or Customer 2 can be confusing. Use meaningful account names.

6. Forgetting bill-wise tracking

Businesses that need invoice-level receivable and payable tracking should configure the relevant options appropriately.

11 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Ledger Creation in TallyPrime

1. How do I create a ledger in TallyPrime?

Open TallyPrime, select the company, press Alt+G, search for Create Master and select Ledger. Enter the ledger name, choose the appropriate group, fill in required details and press Ctrl+A to save in the standard creation screen.

2. What is the shortcut key to create a ledger in TallyPrime?

You can use Alt+G to access Create Master and then select Ledger. While entering a voucher, Alt+C can also create a new master in supported contexts.

3. What is the difference between a ledger and a group in TallyPrime?

A ledger is an individual account used to record transactions, while a group classifies that ledger according to its accounting purpose. For example, Office Rent is a ledger and Indirect Expenses is its group.

4. Under which group should I create a customer ledger?

A customer ledger is generally created under Sundry Debtors when the business sells goods or services to that customer on credit.

5. Under which group should I create a supplier ledger?

A supplier ledger is generally created under Sundry Creditors when the business purchases goods or services from the supplier on credit.

6. Under which group should I create a salary ledger?

A Salary Expense ledger is generally created under Indirect Expenses for routine employee salary costs, subject to the business's accounting structure.

7. Can I create multiple ledgers in TallyPrime?

Yes. TallyPrime supports creating ledgers individually and through multi-ledger creation options where available. This is useful when setting up several customer, supplier, expense or other accounts.

8. Do I need to create a separate ledger for every customer?

Separate customer ledgers are generally useful for tracking individual balances, credit sales, receipts and outstanding invoices. The exact structure depends on the business's accounting requirements.

9. Can I change a ledger group after creating a ledger?

A ledger's group can generally be altered. Check the accounting implications before changing classifications, especially for ledgers with existing transactions.

10. Is ledger creation important for learning TallyPrime?

Yes. Ledger creation is a fundamental skill because vouchers, accounting reports and transaction tracking depend on correctly configured accounts.
