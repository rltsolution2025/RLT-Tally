How Long Does It Take to Learn TallyPrime?

Find out how long it takes to learn TallyPrime, from basic accounting and voucher entry to GST, inventory, payroll and practical accounting skills.

Quick Answer

How long does it take to learn TallyPrime?

There is no fixed number of days because learning speed depends on your accounting knowledge, previous software experience, practice schedule and course depth.

01

How Long Does It Take to Learn TallyPrime?

If you are planning to learn TallyPrime, one of your first questions may be:

“How many days will it take to learn Tally?”

There is no fixed number of days because learning speed depends on your current accounting knowledge, previous software experience, practice schedule and course depth.

A person learning basic voucher entry has a very different goal from someone preparing for an accounts executive role.

Can You Learn Basic Tally in 15 Days?

Some learners can understand basic concepts within a short period, especially if they already know accounting.

Basic Learning

A short introduction may teach you how to create a company and enter transactions.

However, learning the basics is different from becoming job-ready.

A job-oriented program should give you enough practice to handle realistic business scenarios.

04

Can You Learn Tally in One Month?

For many beginners, one month of consistent training can provide a useful foundation.

But the result depends heavily on the syllabus and practice.

Consistent Training

A learner studying regularly can gain more exposure than someone attending only occasionally.

Course Structure

A structured syllabus can provide a planned learning sequence instead of random lessons.

For example, a learner studying two hours daily will have significantly more exposure than someone attending a single weekly session.

05

How Many Hours Should You Practice?

There is no mandatory number of practice hours.

However, regular practice is more important than studying for a long time once and then stopping.

Learning Tally vs Becoming an Accountant

This distinction is important.

Learning software does not automatically make someone a qualified accountant.

Professional accounting roles may require

Educational qualifications
Accounting knowledge
Software skills
Taxation knowledge
Practical experience
Communication skills

Therefore, think of TallyPrime as one important professional skill rather than the entire accounting profession.

Final Thoughts

The time required to learn TallyPrime depends on your target.

If your goal is basic software familiarity, you may need a relatively short learning period.

If your goal is to become comfortable with accounting, GST, inventory, payroll and practical business transactions, you should plan for a more comprehensive learning journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I learn Tally in 15 days?

You may learn basic concepts in that time, but practical proficiency requires continued practice.

A month can provide a foundation, depending on your learning schedule and course structure.

How long does advanced Tally training take?

Advanced training generally requires more time because it covers areas such as GST, inventory, payroll and practical reporting.

Is Tally enough to get a job?

Tally can be a useful skill, but employers may also look for accounting knowledge, education, communication and practical experience.
