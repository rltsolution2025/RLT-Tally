INTRODUCTION

How to Record Stock Transfers Between Godowns in TallyPrime

Businesses that maintain inventory in multiple warehouses, storage locations, or branches need an accurate way to track stock movement. When goods are moved from one godown to another, the business must update its inventory records to reflect the new location without incorrectly recording the movement as a sale or purchase.

TallyPrime helps businesses manage inventory across multiple godowns by recording stock transfers and tracking stock quantities at different locations.

Learning how to record stock transfers between godowns in TallyPrime is useful for accounting students, inventory executives, warehouse staff, and aspiring accountants who want to develop practical inventory management skills.

In this guide, you will learn how to enable multiple godowns, create godown records, record stock transfers using a Stock Journal voucher, and verify the movement through inventory reports.

What does a stock transfer do?

A normal internal transfer moves inventory from one godown to another. It changes the stock available at each location while keeping the company's total quantity unchanged.

INVENTORY BASICS

What Is a Godown in TallyPrime?

A godown is a location where a business stores its goods or inventory. It can represent a warehouse, storage room, distribution centre, or another inventory location.

For example, a business may maintain the following godowns:

Main Warehouse Chennai Warehouse Tambaram Storage Unit Retail Store Finished Goods Warehouse

TallyPrime allows businesses to maintain stock records for different godowns so they can track where inventory is stored and how much stock is available at each location.

UNDERSTANDING THE PROCESS

What Is a Stock Transfer Between Godowns?

A stock transfer between godowns is the movement of inventory from one storage location to another within a business.

For example, ABC Traders has 100 units of a product in its Main Warehouse. The business transfers 20 units to its Chennai Warehouse.

Particulars Main Warehouse Chennai Warehouse
Stock before transfer 100 units 0 units
Quantity transferred -20 units +20 units
Stock after transfer 80 units 20 units

The total inventory remains 100 units. Only the location of the stock changes.

Important: An internal stock transfer between godowns within the same company generally does not represent a sale or purchase. Transfers involving different GST registrations, separate legal entities, or other special circumstances may have additional accounting and GST implications.

STEP 1

Enable Multiple Godowns in TallyPrime

To transfer stock between different godowns, first enable the multiple-godown inventory feature.

01
COMPANY CONFIGURATION

Turn on multiple-godown management

  1. Open TallyPrime.
  2. Select the relevant company.
  3. Open Company Features or the inventory configuration options.
  4. Locate the inventory settings.
  5. Enable the option for maintaining multiple godowns, if it is not already enabled.
  6. Save the configuration.

Note: The exact menu labels may vary by TallyPrime release. Once this feature is enabled, you can create multiple storage locations and assign stock to them.

STEP 2

Create Godowns in TallyPrime

Create the source and destination godowns before recording the transfer.

For this example, we will create two godowns:

Main Warehouse

Source storage location

Chennai Warehouse

Destination storage location

  1. Open the inventory master creation screen.
  2. Select Godown or the corresponding location master option.
  3. Enter the name of the first godown: Main Warehouse.
  4. Select the appropriate parent godown, if required.
  5. Save the godown.
  6. Repeat the process to create Chennai Warehouse.

If you already maintain a godown hierarchy, select the appropriate parent location.

You can create godowns based on your business's actual inventory structure. For more details, see How to Manage Multiple Godowns in TallyPrime.

STEP 3

Create Stock Items

If the product you want to transfer has not been created, add it as a stock item.

Example Stock Item

Office Chair Unit: Nos Opening Quantity: 100
  1. Open the inventory master creation screen.
  2. Select Stock Item.
  3. Enter the stock item name.
  4. Select the appropriate stock group.
  5. Configure the unit of measurement.
  6. Enter the relevant inventory details.
  7. Save the stock item.

If opening stock is already recorded, verify that the quantity is assigned to the correct godown. For accurate stock tracking, the same stock item should be used at both the source and destination locations.

STEP 4

Open the Stock Journal Voucher

TallyPrime commonly uses a Stock Journal voucher to record internal stock movements between godowns.

04
VOUCHER ENTRY

Open Stock Journal

  1. Open the relevant company.
  2. Go to Vouchers.
  3. Select the Stock Journal voucher type.
  4. Choose the appropriate stock movement or godown transfer entry mode, depending on your TallyPrime version.

Stock Journal is also used for certain other inventory adjustments and movements, so ensure that you are entering the transfer correctly.

To understand the different voucher types, read TallyPrime Voucher Types Explained.

STEP 5

Enter the Source and Destination Godowns

Suppose ABC Traders wants to transfer 20 Office Chairs from Main Warehouse to Chennai Warehouse.

Field Details
Stock Item Office Chair
Source Godown Main Warehouse
Destination Godown Chennai Warehouse
Quantity 20 Nos

In the Stock Journal voucher, enter the item and quantity being transferred out of the source location and the same item and quantity being received at the destination location.

Illustrative Stock Movement

20 NOS
Source: Main Warehouse-20 Nos
Destination: Chennai Warehouse+20 Nos
Net change in total stock0 Nos

Check that both sides use the correct stock item and unit of measurement. The precise field arrangement depends on the voucher mode and configuration in your TallyPrime release.

STEP 6

Save the Stock Transfer Voucher

Before saving the voucher, verify the following:

Correct stock item is selected.
Source godown is correct.
Destination godown is correct.
Transfer quantity is accurate.
Source godown has sufficient available stock.
Transfer-out and transfer-in quantities match.

Save the voucher once all details have been verified. The stock movement should now be reflected in the inventory records for the relevant godowns.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Recording Stock Transfers

1. Forgetting to Enable Multiple Godowns

If the multiple-godown feature is disabled, the required location fields may not be available. Solution: Review the inventory configuration before creating godowns or entering transfers.

2. Selecting the Wrong Godown

Selecting an incorrect source or destination can make stock appear in the wrong location. Solution: Verify the godown names before saving each voucher.

3. Entering Different Quantities on Each Side

The quantity transferred out should match the quantity received for a straightforward internal transfer without losses or separate adjustments. Solution: Confirm that the transfer-out and transfer-in quantities match.

4. Transferring More Stock Than Is Available

An incorrect source quantity may create stock discrepancies or negative stock, depending on the settings and transaction sequence. Solution: Check the available stock at the source godown before recording the transfer.

5. Creating Duplicate Stock Items

Creating separate stock items for the same product at different godowns can make inventory tracking unnecessarily complicated. Solution: Use the appropriate common stock item across godowns unless the products genuinely require separate item masters.

6. Forgetting to Verify Inventory Reports

A saved voucher should still be checked to ensure that the inventory records reflect the intended movement. Solution: Review the Stock Summary and relevant godown-wise details after saving the voucher.

INVENTORY CONTROL

Best Practices for Managing Godowns in TallyPrime

Follow these practices to maintain accurate inventory records:

  • Use clear and consistent godown names.
  • Maintain an organised hierarchy for warehouses and storage locations.
  • Record transfers promptly.
  • Check source stock before transferring inventory.
  • Use accurate units of measurement.
  • Verify quantities before saving Stock Journal vouchers.
  • Review godown-wise stock reports regularly.
  • Reconcile physical stock with system records.
  • Maintain supporting documents for significant stock movements.
  • Apply the appropriate GST and accounting treatment when transfers involve separate registrations or legal entities.

These practices can help businesses improve inventory accuracy and reduce stock discrepancies.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I transfer stock from one godown to another in TallyPrime?

Enable multiple godowns, create the source and destination godowns, and open a Stock Journal voucher. Enter the stock item, transfer-out quantity, source godown, transfer-in quantity, and destination godown. Save the voucher and verify the inventory reports.

2. Which voucher is used for stock transfer in TallyPrime?

A Stock Journal voucher is commonly used to record stock transfers between godowns within the same company.

3. Can I transfer multiple stock items in one voucher?

Yes, a Stock Journal can support multiple stock item movements, depending on the voucher configuration. Enter and verify the source and destination quantities for each item.

4. Does stock transfer between godowns affect total stock?

A normal internal stock transfer changes the stock quantity at individual locations but does not change the company's total inventory quantity.

5. Does stock transfer between godowns affect profit and loss?

A normal internal transfer at the same inventory valuation does not, by itself, represent sales revenue or a purchase expense. Separate costs, losses, valuation changes, or other adjustments may have accounting implications.

6. How can I check godown-wise stock in TallyPrime?

Review the Stock Summary and the relevant godown-wise inventory views or drill-down reports available in your TallyPrime version.

7. Can I transfer stock between different branches using a Stock Journal?

It depends on how the branches are structured. Locations within the same company and GST registration may be handled differently from branches maintained as separate GST registrations or legal entities. Review the applicable accounting and GST requirements before recording the transaction.

8. What should I do if stock is not appearing in the destination godown?

Open the Stock Journal voucher and check the destination godown, stock item, quantity, and voucher status. Then review the relevant inventory report filters and confirm that the voucher was saved correctly.

9. Is stock transfer the same as a sales entry?

No. A normal internal stock transfer records inventory movement between locations. A sales entry records a sale to a customer. Transfers involving separate registrations or entities may require a different transaction and tax treatment.
